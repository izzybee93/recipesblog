# Creamy Pasta Consolidation Implementation Plan

> **For Claude:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Replace the separate creamy broccoli and creamy courgette pasta recipes with one flexible “Creamy pasta” recipe and one consistently named image set.

**Architecture:** Retain and rewrite the courgette recipe as `creamy-pasta.mdx`, delete the broccoli recipe, and treat the courgette image as the canonical creamy-pasta image. Regenerate derived blur data, update enhancement progress, and rely on the repository’s existing commit hook to synchronize production images, blob mappings, and the backup image repository.

**Tech Stack:** MDX with YAML frontmatter, JSON, Node.js image scripts, Next.js 15, Git hooks

### Task 1: Consolidate the recipe content

**Files:**
- Create: `content/recipes/creamy-pasta.mdx`
- Delete: `content/recipes/creamy-courgette-pasta.mdx`
- Delete: `content/recipes/creamy-broccoli-pasta.mdx`

**Step 1: Rename the retained recipe file**

Rename `content/recipes/creamy-courgette-pasta.mdx` to `content/recipes/creamy-pasta.mdx` without changing unrelated recipe files.

**Step 2: Rewrite the retained recipe**

Use this frontmatter structure and content:

```yaml
---
title: Creamy pasta
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.279Z'
categories:
  - mains
  - pasta
featured_image: /images/recipes/creamy-pasta.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 180g dry pasta
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - Ground black pepper, to taste
  - '## Any veggie:'
  - '2 courgettes, chopped'
  - '½ broccoli, chopped and roasted'
  - '## Any additional veggie:'
  - 150g peas, cooked
  - 60g spinach
  - '## Sauce'
  - Creamy pasta sauce (see recipe)
directions:
  - Cook the pasta, reserving some of the pasta water.
  - In a large pan, heat the oil on a medium heat. Add the raw veggie and cook for 5-10 minutes, until softened and lightly browned.
  - Make the creamy pasta sauce. Add to the pan and gently heat through. Stir in a few tbsp of pasta water, adding more as needed.
  - Add the pasta, any roasted veggie and additional veggie. Stir until combined and heated through. Serve topped with ground black pepper.
---
```

**Step 3: Delete the superseded broccoli recipe**

Delete `content/recipes/creamy-broccoli-pasta.mdx`.

**Step 4: Validate the MDX frontmatter**

Run: `npm run build`

Expected: the recipe frontmatter parses and the build completes successfully.
