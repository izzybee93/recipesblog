---
title: "Mashed chickpea and avocado on toast"
date: "2026-09-23"
categories:
  - breakfast
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/mashed-chickpea-and-avocado-on-toast.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - ½ medium avocado
  - '80g chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 2 tsp lemon juice
  - 1 tsp chimichurri herb mix
  - 'Pinch sea salt, to taste'
  - '2 medium tomatoes, sliced'
  - '2 slices sourdough, toasted'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - 'In a medium bowl, add the chickpeas, avocado, lemon juice, herbs and salt. Mash together until a chunky spread forms.'
  - 'Serve on the toasted sourdough, topped with tomato slices and ground black pepper.'
---
