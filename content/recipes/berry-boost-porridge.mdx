---
title: "Berry boost porridge"
date: "2026-09-19"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/berry-boost-porridge.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - 50g oats
  - 250ml plant milk or water
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water'
  - '50g blackcurrants, redcurrants, blueberries or raspberries'
  - '50g strawberries, sliced'
  - ½ tsp cinnamon
  - 1 tbsp hemp seeds
  - 1 tbsp almond butter
directions:
  - 'In a small pan, add the oats, chia seeds, plant milk and mixed berries. Cook for a few minutes over medium heat, stirring frequently. Stir in the cinnamon.'
  - 'Serve topped with strawberry slices, hemp seeds and a drizzle of almond butter.'
