date: "2026-10-06"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/orange-miso-and-ponzu-chickpea-salad-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '⅔ chickpeas with orange, miso and ponzu sauce (see recipe)'
  - '80g rocket, lettuce or mixed salad leaves'
  - '¼ cucumber, sliced'
  - '1 avocado, chopped'
  - '1 carrot, grated'
  - 'Small handful fresh coriander, chopped'
  - Juice of ½ lime
  - 'Pinch sea salt, to taste'
  - '2 pitta breads, warmed (optional)'
directions:
  - Make the chickpeas with orange, miso and ponzu sauce.
  - In a medium bowl, add the leaves, cucumber, carrot, coriander, lime juice and salt. Toss to combine. Add the avocado and gently mix.
  - Divide the salad between serving bowls. Top with the chickpeas and serve with the pitta bread.
