title: "Coffee icing"
date: "2026-10-01"
categories:
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/coffee-icing.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1 cake"
ingredients:
  - '200g icing sugar, sieved, sieved'
  - '2 tsp instant coffee, dissolved in 1 tbsp cold water'
  - 2 tsp plant milk
directions:
  - 'In a medium bowl, mix the icing sugar and the coffee. Add the plant milk, 1 tsp at a time, and mix until smooth, glossy and spreadable.'
