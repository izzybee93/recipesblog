title: Chocolate frosting
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.276Z'
categories:
  - treats
featured_image: /images/recipes/chocolate-frosting.jpeg
servings: serves 1 cake or 12 muffins
ingredients:
  - 80g plant butter
  - 120g icing sugar, sieved, sieved
  - 25g cocoa powder
  - Pinch sea salt
  - ½ tsp vanilla extract
  - 1–2 tbsp soya milk
directions:
  - >-
    In a medium bowl, beat the plant butter until creamy. Add the icing sugar,
    cocoa powder, salt and vanilla and beat until light and fluffy. Add 1–2 tbsp
    of soya milk as needed and beat until smooth and fluffy.
