---
title: "Butternut squash and hummus salad bowl"
date: "2026-09-09"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/butternut-squash-and-hummus-salad-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '½ medium butternut squash, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - '2 medium beetroot, cooked and chopped'
  - '¼ cucumber, chopped'
  - 4 tbsp chickpea almond hummus (see recipe)
  - '## Dressing'
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 'Small handful fresh parsley, chopped'
  - 'Pinch sea salt, to taste'
directions:
  - 'Preheat the oven to 180°C and prepare a large baking tray. Add the butternut squash, brush with the olive oil and smoked paprika, and roast for 20–25 minutes until tender and slightly browned.'
  - Add the chickpeas for the final 10 minutes of cooking and toss together.
  - Whisk together the dressing ingredients in a small jug.
  - 'To assemble the bowls, add the butternut squash, beetroot, cucumber and chickpeas. Pour over the dressing and top with spoonfuls of hummus.'
