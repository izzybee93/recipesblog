---
title: Creamy harissa and tomato butter beans
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.281Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/creamy-harissa-and-tomato-butter-beans.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 120g silken tofu cream (see recipe)
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '½ onion, finely chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - 1 tsp harissa paste
  - ½ tsp smoked paprika
  - '6 sun-dried tomatoes, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp tomato paste
  - ¾ tsp dried oregano
  - Pinch sea salt, to taste
  - 300ml vegetable stock
  - '1 400g can butter beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 80g spinach
  - 1½ tbsp nutritional yeast
  - 'Small handful basil leaves, chopped'
  - 2 tbsp lemon juice
directions:
  - Make the silken tofu cream and set aside.
  - >-
    In a large pan, heat the olive oil over a medium heat. Add the onion and
    cook for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Stir in the 
    harissa paste and paprika and cook for another 3 minutes.
  - >-
    Stir in the sun-dried tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano and salt.
  - >-
    Add the butter beans and vegetable stock. Bring to a simmer and cook for 5 minutes, 
    stirring occassionally. 
  - Stir in the nutritional yeast and spinach until wilted.
  - Reduce to a medium-low heat and stir in the tofu cream. Gently simmer until thickened slightly.
  - 'Remove from the heat and stir in the lemon juice and basil. Serve with extra basil to garnish.'
