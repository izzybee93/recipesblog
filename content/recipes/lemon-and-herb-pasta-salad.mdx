---
title: "Lemon and herb pasta salad"
date: "2026-01-17"
categories: ["mains", "pasta", "salad"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/lemon-and-herb-pasta-salad.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g pasta
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 200g cherry tomatoes, chopped
  - Small handful fresh basil, chopped
  - Lemon and herb dressing (see recipe)
  - '## Any plant protein:'
  - '200g extra firm tofu, chopped'
  - '150g tempeh, chopped'
  - 50g soya chunks
  - '## Any 1-2 veggies:'
  - ½ red pepper, finely chopped
  - ¼ cucumber, chopped
  - 10 olives, halved
  - 1 small avocado, chopped
  - 80g sweetcorn
  - 40g rocket
directions:
  - Cook the pasta.
  - Make the lemon and herb dressing.
  - Heat the olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Cook the tofu or tempeh for 5 minutes on each side until browned. Reduce to low heat, add 2 tbsp lemon and herb dressing and cook for 2 more minutes.
  - Alternatively, In a small bowl, add the soya chunks and enough boiling water to just cover them. Leave to sit for 5-10 minutes. Drain off any excess liquid.'
  - Combine the pasta, tomatoes, protein, veggies, basil and remaining dressing in a bowl and gently mix.
