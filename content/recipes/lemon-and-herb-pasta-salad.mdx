---
title: "Lemon and herb pasta salad"
date: "2026-01-17"
categories: ["mains", "pasta", "salad"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/lemon-and-herb-pasta-salad.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g pasta
  - 200g extra firm tofu, sliced
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 200g cherry tomatoes, chopped
  - 1 small avocado, chopped
  - 2 tbsp basil leaves, chopped
  - Lemon and herb dressing (see recipe)
directions:
  - Cook the pasta.
  - Make the lemon and herb dressing.
  - Heat the olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Cook the tofu for 5 minutes on each side until browned. Reduce to low heat, add 2 tbsp lemon and herb dressing and cook for 2 more minutes.
  - Combine the pasta, tomatoes, tofu, basil and remaining dressing in a bowl and mix well. Add the avocado and gently mix.
