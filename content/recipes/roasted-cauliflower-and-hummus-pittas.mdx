servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - '½ cauliflower, chopped into florets'
  - 120g chickpeas
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - ½ tsp turmeric
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ¼ tsp chilli powder
  - ¼ tsp ground coriander
  - Pinch sea salt, to taste
  - 2 wholemeal pittas, warmed
  - 50g rocket
  - 1 tbsp pomegranate seeds
  - '## Any hummus:'
  - 4 tbsp lemon and coriander hummus (see recipe)
  - 4 tbsp chickpea almond hummus (see recipe)
  - 4 tbsp roasted red pepper hummus (see recipe)
  - 4 tbsp hummus (see recipe)
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 200°C. Place the cauliflower on a lined baking tray and drizzle with olive oil.
    Add the chickpeas and spices, and mix until coated. Roast for 25 minutes, until starting to brown.
  - >-
    Spread the hummus in each pitta. Fill with rocket, cauliflower and chickpeas, and top with pomegranate seeds.
