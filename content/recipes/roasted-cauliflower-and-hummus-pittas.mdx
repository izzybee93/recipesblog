title: Roasted cauliflower and hummus pittas

categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/roasted-cauliflower-and-hummus-pittas.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - '½ cauliflower, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - ½ tsp turmeric
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ¼ tsp chilli powder
  - Pinch sea salt
  - 2 wholemeal pittas, warmed
  - 4 tbsp hummus (see recipe)
  - 50g rocket
  - 2 tsp pomegranate seeds (optional)
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 190°C. Place the cauliflower on a lined baking tray.
    Drizzle with the olive oil and spices, and toss well until evenly coated.
    Roast for 20 minutes.
  - >-
    Spread 1 tbsp of hummus in each pitta. Add the rocket then cauliflower. Top with pomegranate seeds and serve.
