---
title: "Oatmeal raisin cookies"
date: "2025-08-16T15:27:07.294Z"
categories:
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/oatmeal-raisin-cookies.jpeg"
servings: "makes 18"
ingredients:
  - 100g raisins
  - 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  - 3 tbsp water
  - 110g plant butter
  - 100g soft brown sugar
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - 140g plain flour
  - ¾ tsp cinnamon
  - ½ tsp baking powder
  - ¼ tsp sea salt
  - 140g rolled oats
directions:
  - 'In a small bowl, add the raisins, then cover with boiling water and set aside.'
  - 'Whisk together the flaxseed and water in a small bowl and set aside for 15 minutes to thicken.'
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C and prepare 2 large baking trays.
  - 'In a large bowl, cream the butter until light and fluffy. Add the sugar and mix again. Add the flax egg and vanilla extract and mix well.'
  - 'In a medium bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, cinnamon and salt. Add the dry ingredients to the wet and mix until a smooth dough forms.'
  - 'Drain the raisins, then add to the dough. Add the oats and mix well.'
  - 'Scoop out 1 tbsp of dough at a time, roll into a ball and place each ball on the tray, 3cm apart. Flatten each cookie slightly. Bake for 13-15 minutes, or until golden brown. Transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.'
