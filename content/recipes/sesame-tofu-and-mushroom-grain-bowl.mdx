---
title: "Sesame tofu and mushroom grain bowl"
date: "2026-04-14"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/sesame-tofu-and-mushroom-grain-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g quinoa or rice
  - '200g extra firm tofu, cubed'
  - 1 tbsp sesame oil
  - '200g mushrooms, chopped'
  - '150g edamame, cooked'
  - '¼ cucumber, chopped'
  - '## Any 1–2 veggies:'
  - 80g sugar snap peas
  - ½ red pepper, finely sliced
  - 6 tbsp pickled red cabbage (see recipe)
  - 4 tbsp pickled radishes (see recipe)
  - ½ avocado, sliced
  - '## Dressing'
  - Sesame dressing (see recipe)
  - 2 tsp sesame seeds
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the quinoa or rice.
  - 'In a large pan, heat the sesame oil over medium heat. Add the tofu and mushrooms and cook for 10–15 minutes, turning to brown each side.'
  - 'Make the sesame dressing. Stir in 2 tsp sesame seeds. Add half of the dressing to the pan and cook for 5 more minutes.'
  - Cut the cucumber into ribbons using a vegetable peeler, then place in a bowl. Pour the remaining dressing over the cucumber and toss to combine.
  - 'Assemble the bowls with the quinoa or rice, then top with tofu and veggies. Serve with a drizzle of remaining dressing and a sprinkle of sesame seeds.'
