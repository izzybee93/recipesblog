---
title: "Chocolate fudge frosting"
date: "2026-05-05"
categories:
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/chocolate-fudge-frosting.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1 cake"
ingredients:
  - '4 Medjool dates, chopped'
  - 60ml soya milk
  - 65g dark chocolate
  - 1½ tbsp almond butter
  - 1 tsp cocoa powder
  - ½ tsp vanilla extract
  - Pinch sea salt
directions:
  - In a small pan, add the dates, soya milk, chocolate and almond butter. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring frequently, until the chocolate has melted and the mixture is shiny.
  - Remove from the heat, cover and set aside for 2 minutes to soften the dates.
  - Transfer to a food processor and add the cocoa powder, vanilla extract and salt. Process for a few minutes, scraping down the sides as needed, until the mixture is shiny and smooth. Add more soya milk if needed, 1 tsp at a time, until smooth and spreadable.
  - Transfer to the fridge for 5–10 minutes to thicken slightly.
