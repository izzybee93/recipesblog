title: Mashed avocado on toast

categories:

featured_image: /images/recipes/mashed-avocado-on-toast.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 1 large ripe avocado
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - Pinch sea salt, to taste
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '4 slices sourdough, toasted'
directions:
  - >-
    In a medium bowl, mash the avocado, lemon juice, salt and ground
    black pepper until smooth.
  - Serve on the toasted sourdough and top with extra black pepper.
