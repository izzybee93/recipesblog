---
title: "Burrito bowl"
date: "2025-08-27"
categories: ["mains"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/burrito-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 80g rice
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - ½ onion, chopped
  - 1 garlic clove, minced
  - 1 400g can kidney beans, black beans or pinto beans, drained and rinsed
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - ¼ tsp chilli powder
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ¼ tsp ground coriander
  - ¼ tsp sea salt, to taste
  - 100ml water
  - Juice of ½ lime
  - Small handful coriander, chopped
  - 2 tbsp fresh tomato salsa (see recipe)
  - '## Any 3 veggies:'
  - 1 pepper, chopped
  - 200g mushrooms, sliced
  - 80g sweetcorn
  - ½ avocado, sliced
  - Small handful lettuce, chopped
directions:
  - Cook the rice.
  - In a small pan, heat ½ tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Add the peppers, mushrooms or sweetcorn and cook for 8-10 minutes.
  - In a large pan, heat ½ tbsp oil. Add the onion and cook for a 5 minutes until softened. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
  - Add the beans and cook for 2 minutes, stirring occasionally. Stir in the spices and salt. Add the water, adding more as needed. Cook on medium-low heat for 5 minutes, until heated through. Remove from the heat and stir in the lime juice and fresh coriander.
  - Assemble the bowls with the rice, topped with the beans, veggies and fresh tomato salsa.
---
