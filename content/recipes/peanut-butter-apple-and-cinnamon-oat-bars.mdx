---
title: "Peanut butter, apple and cinnamon oat bars"
date: "2026-09-04"
categories:
  - breakfast
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/peanut-butter-apple-and-cinnamon-oat-bars.jpeg"
servings: "makes 16"
ingredients:
  - 180g oats
  - 100g peanut butter
  - 50g maple syrup
  - 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  - 3 tbsp water
  - 15-25ml plant milk
  - '100g apple, finely chopped'
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - 1 tsp cinnamon
  - ¼ tsp sea salt
  - 30g pumpkin seeds
directions:
  - 'In a small bowl, add the ground flaxseed and water and set aside for 10-15 minutes to thicken.'
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Line a 20cm square baking tin with baking paper.
  - 'In a small pan, add the peanut butter and maple syrup and heat over a low heat until smooth. Remove from the heat, then stir in the flaxseed mixture, vanilla extract and 15ml plant milk.'
  - 'In a large bowl, combine the oats, cinnamon, salt, apple and pumpkin seeds. Add the peanut butter mixture and mix well until everything is evenly coated. Add a little more plant milk as needed until the mixture is sticky and holds together when pressed.'
  - Transfer the mixture to the prepared tin and press down very firmly with the back of a spoon or the base of a glass.
  - 'Bake for 20-25 minutes, until lightly golden around the edges. Leave to cool completely before slicing into bars.'
