---
title: "Peanut butter, apple and cinnamon oat bars"
date: "2026-09-04"
categories:
  - breakfast
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/peanut-butter-apple-and-cinnamon-oat-bars.jpeg"
servings: "makes 16"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  - 3 tbsp water
  - 180g oats
  - 1 tsp cinnamon
  - ¼ tsp sea salt
  - 30g pumpkin seeds
  - 100g peanut butter
  - 45g maple syrup
  - '100g apple, finely chopped'
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - 1-2 tbsp plant milk
directions:
  - 'In a small bowl, add the ground flaxseed and water and set aside for 10 minutes.'
  - Preheat the oven to 170°C. Prepare a 20cm square baking dish.
  - 'In a large bowl, combine the oats, cinnamon, salt, apple and pumpkin seeds.'
  - 'In a small pan, add the peanut butter and maple syrup and heat over a low heat until smooth. Remove from the heat, then stir in the flaxseed mixture, vanilla extract and 1 tbsp milk.'
  - 'Add the peanut butter mixture to the dry ingredients and mix well until evenly coated. Add another tbsp milk as needed until the mixture holds together when pressed.'
  - 'Transfer to the dish and press down firmly with the back of a spoon. Bake for 25-30 minutes, until golden. Leave to cool completely before slicing into bars.'
