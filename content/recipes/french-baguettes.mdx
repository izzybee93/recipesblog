---
title: Baguette tradition
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.284Z'
categories:
  - bread
featured_image: /images/recipes/french-baguettes.jpeg
servings: makes 4
ingredients:
  - 750g strong white bread flour
  - 540-600g water
  - 15g salt
  - 7g active dry yeast
directions:
  - >-
    Mix: In a large bowl, add the flour, salt and yeast and mix. Add the water
    and mix well for a few minutes; the dough will be quite liquid. Cover with a
    damp tea towel and leave at room temperature for 20 minutes.
  - >-
    Perform a fold: lift and fold the dough on itself, trying to trap air
    inside. Rotate the bowl 90 degrees, then lift, fold and rotate 3 more times.
    Cover and leave for 20 minutes. Repeat this process 2 more times to make a
    total of 1 hour. Let the dough rest for 2 hours (or place in the fridge
    overnight).
  - >-
    Shape: Prepare a large baking tray. Gently divide
    the dough into 4 pieces and shape each into a ball without releasing too much air.
    Leave the dough to rest for 10 minutes (or 40 minutes if refrigerated
    overnight). Slightly flatten the dough and fold in half. Fold it over again
    and gently shape it into a baguette, keeping the bubbles intact. Place on the
    tray, cover and leave to rise for 30 minutes. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
  - >-
    Slash the top of the baguettes with a sharp knife to control where the
    bread will open. Pour a glass of water into a tray at the base of the oven,
    then bake for about 30 minutes.
