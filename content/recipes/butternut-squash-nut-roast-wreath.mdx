---
title: Butternut squash nut roast wreath
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.266Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/butternut-squash-nut-roast-wreath.jpeg
servings: serves 10
ingredients:
  - '2 onions, roughly chopped'
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - '4 cloves garlic, minced'
  - '1 large butternut squash, cubed'
  - '100g mushrooms, finely chopped'
  - ¼ tsp sea salt
  - '300g hazelnuts, toasted'
  - '4 tbsp pine nuts, toasted'
  - '4 tbsp sunflower seeds or pumpkin seeds, toasted'
  - 50g spinach
  - '180g chestnuts, chopped'
  - 2 tbsp soy sauce
  - 4 tbsp nutritional yeast
  - 1 tbsp miso paste
  - 1 tbsp whole grain mustard
  - 1 tsp sea salt
  - Zest of 1 lemon
  - 1 tbsp maple syrup
  - '2 tbsp fresh thyme, chopped'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Grease a bundt tin.
  - >-
    Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, until softened.
    Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  - >-
    Add the butternut squash, mushrooms, and salt and cook for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. 
  - Add the toasted nuts and seeds to a food processor and pulse a few times, until roughly chopped.  
  - >-
    Add the spinach to the pan and stir until wilted. Remove from the heat and stir in the nuts, seeds and 
    all the remaining ingredients.
  - >-
    Transfer to the bundt tin and press down firmly. Bake for 40
    minutes. Cover with foil for the final 10 minutes of baking if browning too quickly.
  - Leave to cool for 10 minutes then carefully turn out onto a plate.
