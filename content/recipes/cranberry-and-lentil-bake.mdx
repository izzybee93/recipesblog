---
title: Cranberry and lentil bake
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.279Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/cranberry-and-lentil-bake.jpeg
servings: makes 4
ingredients:
  - 100g dried or fresh cranberries
  - 4 tbsp caster sugar (if using fresh cranberries)
  - 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  - '2 400g cans cooked lentils, drained and rinsed'
  - 4 tsp olive oil
  - '2 onions, finely chopped'
  - '2 garlic cloves, minced'
  - 2 tbsp chopped sage
  - 1 tbsp chopped thyme
  - ½ tbsp chopped rosemary
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ½ tsp allspice
  - 1 tbsp tomato paste
  - 4 tsp soy sauce
  - 2 tsp miso paste
  - 1 tbsp cornflour
directions:
  - >-
    Heat oven to 180°C. Line the base of 4 small ovenproof ramekins with a
    circle of baking parchment and oil the sides.
  - >-
    Put the cranberries in a small pan with the apple cider vinegar (and sugar if using fresh
    cranberries). Cook over a medium heat for 5-10 minutes, stirring frequently. Pour into the 
    base of the ramekins, even out with a spoon and set aside. 
  - >-
    In a medium bowl, add the lentils and roughly mash with a fork, leaving some whole.
  - >-
    Heat the oil in a small pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook 5 minutes,
    until softened. Stir in the garlic, herbs and spices, and cook
    for 2 minutes. Remove from the heat and add the lentils, tomato paste, soy
    sauce, miso paste and cornflour. Mix well, then spoon into the ramekins,
    pressing down gently with the back of a spoon. If making a head of time, seal and
    chill for up to two days, or freeze for up to two months. Defrost in the fridge before cooking.
  - >-
    Put the ramekin on a baking tray and bake for 25-30 minutes. Leave to cool
    for 1 minute, then turn out onto a plate.
