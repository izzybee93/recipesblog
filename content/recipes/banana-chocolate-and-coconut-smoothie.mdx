---
title: "Banana, chocolate and coconut smoothie"
date: "2026-08-27"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/banana-chocolate-and-coconut-smoothie.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 banana
  - 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  - 2 tbsp shredded coconut
  - '2 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 2 drops vanilla extract
  - Pinch sea salt
  - 4 tbsp oats
  - 200ml plant milk
  - 1 tsp cacao nibs
directions:
  - Place all ingredients except ½ tbsp coconut and the cacao nibs in a blender and blend until smooth. Top with a sprinkle of coconut and cacao nibs.
