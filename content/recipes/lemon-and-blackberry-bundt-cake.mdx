title: Lemon and blackberry bundt cake
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.290Z'
categories:
  - cakes
  - treats
featured_image: /images/recipes/lemon-and-blackberry-bundt-cake.jpeg
servings: serves 12
ingredients:
  - 340g self-raising flour
  - ¾ tsp bicarbonate of soda
  - ½ tsp sea salt
  - 150g caster sugar
  - 110ml vegetable oil
  - 200ml soya milk
  - Juice of 1 lemon
  - 'Zest of 2 lemons, finely grated'
  - 170g blackberries
  - ½ lemon icing (see recipe)
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Grease a bundt tin. Set aside 40g blackberries for the topping.
  - 'In a large bowl, combine the flour, sugar, bicarbonate of soda and salt.'
  - >-
    Combine the oil, milk, lemon juice and lemon zest and mix well. Pour the wet
    mixture into the dry mixture and gently fold together, then fold in the
    blackberries.
  - >-
    Pour the batter into the tin. Bake for 50–55 minutes, until a skewer
    inserted in the centre comes out clean. Leave to rest for 10 minutes before
    turning out to cool completely.
  - >-
    Make the lemon icing. Spoon on top of the cake, allowing the icing to
    trickle down the sides. Place the remaining blackberries on top. Leave to set before slicing.
