---
title: "Banana, berry and chocolate smoothie bowl"
date: "2026-10-02"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/banana-berry-and-chocolate-smoothie-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - '1 frozen banana, chopped'
  - 1 tsp cocoa powder
  - ¼ tsp cinnamon
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - '1 tbsp almond butter or hazelnut butter'
  - 2 tbsp oats
  - 50ml plant milk
  - '25g strawberries, sliced'
  - 25g raspberries
  - 1 tbsp granola
  - 1 tsp cacao nibs
  - '1 tbsp hazelnuts, chopped'
directions:
  - Blend the banana, nut butter, chia seeds, oats, cocoa powder, cinnamon and plant milk until smooth. Transfer to a bowl and top with strawberries, raspberries, granola, cacao nibs and hazelnuts.
---
