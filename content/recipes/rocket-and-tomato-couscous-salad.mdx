featured_image: "/images/recipes/rocket-and-tomato-couscous-salad.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g giant couscous
  - '1 courgette, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 60g rocket
  - '2 large tomatoes, chopped'
  - '10 olives, chopped'
  - 'Small handful fresh basil or parsley, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a baking tray. Add the courgette and brush with the olive oil. Add the chickpeas to the tray and roast for 15-20 minutes.
  - Rinse and cook the couscous.
  - 'In a large bowl, add the rocket, tomatoes, olives, herbs, couscous, salt, lemon juice and extra virgin olive oil and gently mix. Add the roasted courgette and chickpeas and mix again.'
