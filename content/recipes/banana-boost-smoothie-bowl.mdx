title: Banana boost smoothie bowl

categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: /images/recipes/banana-boost-smoothie-bowl.jpeg
servings: serves 1
ingredients:
  - '1 banana, sliced'
  - '1 kiwi, sliced'
  - 15g spinach
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water'
  - ½ tbsp almond butter
  - 2 tbsp oats
  - 50ml plant milk
  - 1 tbsp granola
  - 1 tbsp hemp seeds
  - 1 tbsp pomegranate seeds
directions:
  - Set aside a few slices of banana and kiwi for the topping.
  - >-
    Blend the remaining banana, kiwi, spinach, chia seeds, almond butter, oats and plant milk
    until smooth. Transfer to a bowl and top with the remaining banana, kiwi, granola, hemp 
    seeds and pomegranate seeds.
