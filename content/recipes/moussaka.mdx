---
title: Moussaka
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.293Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/moussaka.jpeg
servings: serves 4
ingredients:
  - '2 large aubergines, cubed'
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 onion, finely chopped'
  - '2 garlic cloves, minced'
  - ¼ tsp chilli powder
  - ½ tsp cinnamon
  - ½ tsp allspice
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ½ tsp dried oregano (optional)
  - ¼ tsp sea salt, to taste
  - 1 tbsp tomato paste
  - 1 400g can chopped tomatoes
  - 250ml water
  - '1½ 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - Small handful mint leaves, chopped
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 200°C. Coat the aubergine with 1 tbsp olive oil and roast for 20 minutes, until lightly browned.
  - >-
    In a large pan, heat 1 tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion
    and cook for 5 minutes, until softened. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. 
    Stir in the spices, oregano and tomato paste, and cook for 2 minutes, until fragrant. Add the tomatoes and cook for 5 more minutes.
  - >-
    Add the aubergine, chickpeas and water. Bring to a simmer for 15-20 minutes, adding more water as needed.
  - >- 
    Stir in the mint leaves and season to taste. Serve with pitta bread, couscous or salad.
---
