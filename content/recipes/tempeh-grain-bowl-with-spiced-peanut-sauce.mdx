---
title: "Tempeh grain bowl with spiced peanut sauce"
date: "2026-06-02"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/tempeh-grain-bowl-with-spiced-peanut-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g rice or quinoa
  - '150g tempeh, sliced'
  - '¼ cucumber, sliced'
  - '## Any 2-3 veggies:'
  - '1 avocado, sliced'
  - 1 pepper, chopped and roasted
  - ¼ broccoli, chopped and roasted
  - 1 small aubergine, chopped and roasted
  - 100g mushroom, chopped and cooked
  - 6 tbsp pickled red cabbage (see recipe)
  - 4 tbsp pickled radishes (see recipe)
  - '## Sauce'
  - Spiced peanut sauce (see recipe)
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the quinoa or rice.
  - Heat a pan over medium heat. Add the tempeh and cook for 5-10 minutes, turning to brown each side.
  - Make the spiced peanut sauce. Add 4 tbsp sauce to the tempeh and cook for 5 more minutes.
  - 'Assemble the bowls with the rice or quinoa, then top with tempeh, cucumber and veggies. Drizzle over the remaining sauce.'
