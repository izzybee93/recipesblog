---
title: "Chickpeas with orange, miso and ponzu sauce"
date: "2026-10-06"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/chickpeas-with-orange-miso-and-ponzu-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 3"
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 'Small handful fresh coriander, chopped'
  - '## Sauce'
  - '1 orange, peeled and chopped'
  - 2 tsp miso paste
  - 1 tbsp ponzu
  - 1 tsp lime juice
  - ¼ tsp lime zest
  - '1 tsp ginger, grated'
  - 1 tsp sesame oil
  - Pinch chilli flakes
  - 1–2 tbsp water
directions:
  - Add all the sauce ingredients to a blender and blend until smooth. Add water, 1 tbsp at a time, as needed until the sauce reaches the desired consistency.
  - In a medium bowl, add the chickpeas and pour over the sauce. Mix well, then stir in the coriander.
  - Serve with a refreshing salad, grain bowl or wrap. Optionally, serve with orange, miso and ponzu chickpea salad bowl (see recipe).
