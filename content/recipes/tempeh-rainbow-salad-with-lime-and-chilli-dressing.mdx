---
title: "Tempeh rainbow salad with lime and chilli dressing"
date: "2026-09-30"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/tempeh-rainbow-salad-with-lime-and-chilli-dressing.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '1 sweet potato, chopped'
  - '½ aubergine, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '200g tempeh, chopped'
  - 80g rocket
  - '½ Romano pepper, thinly sliced'
  - '¼ cucumber, chopped'
  - 'Handful fresh coriander, chopped'
  - Lime and chilli dressing (see recipe)
  - '2 tbsp peanuts or cashews, roughly chopped and toasted'
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a large baking tray and add the aubergine and sweet potato. Coat with the olive oil and roast for 20-25 minutes, until softened and lightly browned.
  - Heat a large pan over medium heat. Add the tempeh and cook for 8-10 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden and lightly crisp.
  - Make the lime and chilli dressing.
  - In a large bowl, add the rocket, pepper, cucumber, coriander and roasted vegetables. Pour over the dressing and gently toss to combine.
  - Serve topped with the tempeh and toasted peanuts or cashews.
