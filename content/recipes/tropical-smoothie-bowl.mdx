---
title: "Tropical smoothie bowl"
date: "2026-08-07"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/tropical-smoothie-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - 80g frozen mango
  - 2 passion fruit
  - 1 tbsp almond butter
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 2 tbsp oats
  - 50ml plant milk
  - '1 kiwi, sliced'
  - 1 tbsp granola
directions:
  - 'Blend the mango, passion fruit, almond butter, chia seeds, oats and plant milk until smooth. Transfer to a bowl and top with kiwi and granola.'
