categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: /images/recipes/bean-salad.jpeg
servings: serves 2-3
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can mixed beans, drained and rinsed'
  - '⅓ cucumber, chopped'
  - '200g cherry tomatoes, chopped'
  - 120g sweetcorn
  - '60g roasted artichoke, chopped'
  - 12 olives
  - Small handful basil leaves, chopped
  - Balsamic dressing (see recipe)
directions:
  - Add all the vegetables to a large bowl and gently toss together.
  - Make the balsamic dressing.
  - Pour the dressing over the salad and toss until coated.
