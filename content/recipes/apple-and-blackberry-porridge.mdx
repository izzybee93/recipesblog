---
title: "Apple and blackberry porridge"
date: "2026-09-10"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/apple-and-blackberry-porridge.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - 50g oats
  - 250ml plant milk or water
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 1 small apple
  - 50g blackberries
  - ½ tsp cinnamon
  - 1 tbsp almond butter (optional)
  - 1 tsp pumpkin seeds
directions:
  - Halve the apple. Cut one half into thin slices and set aside. Chop or grate the remaining half.
  - 'Add the oats, milk and chia seeds to the pan and cook for a few minutes on a medium heat, stirring frequently. Add the apple and blackberries, stirring frequently until the juices start to release. Stir in the cinnamon.'
  - 'Serve topped with apple slices, extra cinnamon, pumpkin seeds and a drizzle of almond butter.'
---
