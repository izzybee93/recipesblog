---
title: "Apple and blackberry porridge"
date: "2026-09-10"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/apple-and-blackberry-porridge.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - 50g oats
  - 250ml plant milk or water
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 1 small apple
  - 50g blackberries
  - ½ tsp cinnamon
  - 1 tsp pumpkin seeds
  - 1 tbsp almond butter (optional)
directions:
  - Halve the apple. Cut one half into thin slices and set aside. Chop or grate the remaining half. Set aside a few blackberries for topping.
  - 'Add the oats, milk and chia seeds to a pan and cook for a few minutes over medium heat, stirring frequently. Add the chopped or grated apple and blackberries, then continue cooking and stirring until the juices start to release. Stir in the cinnamon.'
  - 'Serve topped with the apple slices, reserved blackberries, extra cinnamon, pumpkin seeds and a drizzle of almond butter.'
---
