---
title: "Green apple and cinnamon smoothie"
date: "2026-09-10"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/green-apple-and-cinnamon-smoothie.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '2 small apples, chopped'
  - ½ frozen banana
  - 50g spinach
  - '2 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 1 tbsp almond butter or peanut butter (optional)
  - 4 tbsp oats
  - 1 tsp cinnamon
  - 200ml plant milk
directions:
  - Place all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
