---
title: Chickpea and edamame rice bowl
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.271Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/chickpea-and-edamame-rice-bowl.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 100g rice
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '½ onion, finely chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '½ tbsp fresh ginger, minced'
  - 1 tsp turmeric
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ¼ tsp sea salt, to taste
  - '120g chickpeas'
  - 120g frozen edamame
  - 1 tbsp tahini
  - 100ml soya milk
  - 2-4 tbsp water
  - 100g spinach
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - 'Small handful fresh coriander, chopped'
  - '## Any 1-2 veggies:'
  - 50g baby corn, chopped
  - 50g sugar snap peas
  - 50g broccolini, chopped
directions:
  - Cook the rice.
  - >-
    In a large pan, heat the olive oil. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes
    until softened. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 1 minute.
    Add the turmeric, cumin and salt and cook for another minute.
  - Add the veggies and cook for 2-5 minutes.
  - >-
    Stir in the chickpeas, edamame, tahini and soya milk. Simmer for 5 minutes, 
    stirring frequently. Add a few tbsp water as needed. Stir in the spinach until wilted. 
  - >-
    Remove from the heat and stir in the lemon juice and coriander. Serve with the rice.
