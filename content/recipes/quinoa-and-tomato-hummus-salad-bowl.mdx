title: "Quinoa and tomato hummus salad bowl"
date: "2026-09-09"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/quinoa-and-tomato-hummus-salad-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 60g quinoa
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - '150g cherry tomatoes, chopped'
  - 120g sweetcorn
  - '60g artichoke hearts, chopped'
  - 'Small handful fresh basil, chopped'
  - '## Any hummus:'
  - 4 tbsp sun-dried tomato hummus (see recipe)
  - 4 tbsp red pepper hummus (see recipe)
  - '## Dressing'
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 'Pinch sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - Cook the quinoa.
  - Heat a pan over medium heat. Add the chickpeas and cook for a few minutes. Add the smoked paprika and toss until coated. Cook for 10 minutes until slightly crispy.
  - Whisk together the dressing ingredients in a small jug.
  - 'In a large bowl, add the quinoa, tomatoes, sweetcorn, artichoke, chickpeas and basil. Pour over the dressing and mix well. Serve topped with spoonfuls of hummus.'
