---
title: "Teriyaki tempeh grain bowl"
date: "2026-01-26"
categories: ["mains"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/teriyaki-tempeh-grain-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g tempeh, sliced
  - 100g quinoa or rice
  - 120g edamame, cooked
  - ¼ cucumber, chopped
  - 4-6 tbsp teriyaki sauce
  - 2 tsp sesame seeds
  - '## Marinade'
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 tbsp rice vinegar or lime juice
  - 1 tsp sesame oil
  - '## Any 2-3 veggies:'
  - '1 avocado, sliced'
  - 2 small beetroot, cooked and chopped
  - 1 small aubergine, chopped and roasted
  - 1 pepper, chopped and roasted
  - 6 tbsp pickled red cabbage (see recipe)
  - 4 tbsp pickled radishes (see recipe)
directions:
  - Whisk the marinade ingredients together in a small bowl. Place the tempeh in a shallow dish and pour over the marinade. Set aside for 10 minutes.
  - Rinse and cook the quinoa or rice.
  - Heat a pan over medium heat. Add the tempeh and cook for 5-10 minutes, turning to brown each side.
  - Assemble the bowl with the quinoa or rice, then top with tempeh, edamame, cucumber and veggies. Serve with a drizzle of teriyaki sauce and a sprinkle of sesame seeds.
