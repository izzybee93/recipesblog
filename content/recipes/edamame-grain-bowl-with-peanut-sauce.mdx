title: "Edamame grain bowl with peanut sauce"
date: "2026-07-21"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/edamame-grain-bowl-with-peanut-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g rice or quinoa
  - '150g edamame, cooked'
  - '½ cucumber, diced'
  - '½ red pepper, finely sliced'
  - 6 tbsp pickled red cabbage (see recipe)
  - Peanut sauce (see recipe)
  - 2 tsp sesame seeds
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the rice or quinoa.
  - Make the peanut sauce.
  - Assemble the bowls with rice or quinoa and top with the edamame, cucumber, pepper and red cabbage. Drizzle over the peanut sauce and top with sesame seeds.
