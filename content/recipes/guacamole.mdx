---
title: "Guacamole"
date: "2026-09-17"
categories:
  - snacks
featured_image: "/images/recipes/guacamole.jpeg"
servings: "serves 6"
ingredients:
  - 2 ripe avocados
  - Juice of 1 lime
  - Zest of ½ lime (optional)
  - ¼ tsp cumin (optional)
  - '½ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - 'Small handful coriander, finely chopped'
directions:
  - Mash the avocados in a bowl until creamy.
  - 'Mix in the lime juice, lime zest, cumin, salt, ground black pepper and coriander.'
  - 'Serve immediately, or transfer to a container, smooth the top and gently cover with 2cm water to create an airtight seal. Store in the fridge for up to 2 days. Pour the water off and stir before serving.'
---
