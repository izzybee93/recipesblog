title: Sweet potato salad with crispy paprika tofu
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.305Z'
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: /images/recipes/sweet-potato-salad-with-crispy-paprika-tofu.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - Crispy paprika tofu (see recipe)
  - '1 large sweet potato, chopped'
  - ½ tbsp olive oil
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - 80g rocket or spinach
  - 120g sweetcorn
  - 'Handful coriander, chopped'
  - Lime and chilli dressing (see recipe)
  - '2 tbsp flaked almonds, toasted'
directions:
  - >-
    Prepare the crispy paprika tofu.
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a baking tray. Add the sweet potato and brush with the olive oil and
    smoked paprika. Roast for 20–25 minutes, until softened and starting to brown.
  - Make the lime and chilli dressing.
  - >-
    In a large bowl, combine the rocket, sweetcorn, coriander, sweet potato and
    tofu. Pour over the dressing and gently mix together.
  - Serve topped with the toasted almonds.
