---
title: "Lemon and courgette dip"
date: "2026-08-18"
categories:
  - snacks
featured_image: "/images/recipes/lemon-and-courgette-dip.jpeg"
servings: "serves 6-8"
ingredients:
  - '2 medium courgettes, sliced'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 3 tbsp tahini
  - 1 tsp lemon zest
  - Juice of 1 lemon
  - '1 tbsp fresh dill, chopped'
  - '¼ tsp salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a baking tray. Add the courgette, brush with the olive oil and roast for 15-20 minutes.
  - 'Add the roasted courgette and remaining ingredients to a food processor and process until smooth. Serve warm, or chill for 1 hour before serving.'
