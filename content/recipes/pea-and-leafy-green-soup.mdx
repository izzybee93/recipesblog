---
title: "Pea and leafy green soup"
date: "2026-08-09"
categories:
  - mains
  - soup
featured_image: "/images/recipes/pea-and-leafy-green-soup.jpeg"
servings: "serves 5"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 onion, chopped'
  - '2 garlic cloves, minced'
  - ¼ tsp chilli powder (optional)
  - '½ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 1L vegetable stock
  - 400g frozen peas
  - 100g spinach
  - '300g leafy greens, stems and leaves separated and chopped'
  - 200ml soya milk
  - Juice of 1 lime
  - Small handful fresh mint or coriander
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '## Any leafy greens:'
  - Pak choi
  - Kale
  - Beetroot greens
  - Chard
directions:
  - 'Heat the olive oil in a wok over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, until softened. Stir in the garlic and chilli, and cook for 1 minute. Add the stems and cook for 5 more minutes.'
  - 'Add the vegetable stock, cover and simmer for 10-15 minutes, until softened.'
  - 'Add the peas and simmer for 3 minutes. Stir in the spinach, leafy greens and soya milk.'
  - Reduce the heat to low and cover for a few minutes until wilted.
  - 'Stir in the lime juice and herbs and blend until smooth. Season to taste and serve topped with ground black pepper.'
---
