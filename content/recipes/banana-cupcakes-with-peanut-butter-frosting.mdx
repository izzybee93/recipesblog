---
title: Banana cupcakes with peanut butter frosting
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.254Z'
categories:
  - cakes
  - treats
featured_image: /images/recipes/banana-cupcakes-with-peanut-butter-frosting.jpeg
servings: makes 12
ingredients:
  - 250g self-raising flour
  - 80g soft brown sugar
  - 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  - ½ tsp sea salt
  - 1 tsp cinnamon
  - 2 overripe bananas
  - 130ml plant milk
  - 70ml vegetable oil
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  - Peanut butter frosting (see recipe)
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Line a cupcake tray with cases.
  - >-
    In a medium bowl, combine the flour, sugar, bicarbonate of soda, salt and cinnamon.
  - >-
    In a large bowl, mash the bananas. Add the milk, oil, vanilla extract and
    apple cider vinegar and mix. Add the dry ingredients and mix until combined. Do not overmix.
  - >-
    Spoon the batter into the prepared tray. Bake for 20 minutes or until a toothpick inserted
    into the centre of one of the cupcakes comes out clean. Transfer the cupcakes to a wire cooling rack to
    cool completely before decorating.
  - >-
    Make the peanut butter frosting. Pipe onto the cupcakes.
