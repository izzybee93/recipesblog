title: Curried chickpea burgers

featured_image: /images/recipes/curried-chickpea-burgers.jpeg
servings: serves 2-3
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 2 tbsp cornflour
  - '1 small onion, finely chopped'
  - '½ red pepper, finely chopped'
  - '15g fresh coriander, chopped'
  - ½ tsp sea salt
  - ½ tsp garlic powder
  - 1 tbsp curry powder
  - 1 tbsp tahini
  - 4-6 tbsp water
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a baking tray.
  - Mash the chickpeas in a large bowl with a fork.
  - >-
    Add the cornflour, onion, pepper, coriander, salt, garlic
    powder, curry powder and tahini to the mashed chickpeas. Stir to combine. Add the
    water and mix until a dough comes together.
  - >-
    Divide and shape into 6 small burgers. Bake for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway.
