---
title: "Tofu and bean chilli"
date: "2025-11-29"
categories: ["mains"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/tofu-and-bean-chilli.jpeg"
servings: "serves 4"
ingredients:
  - '280g extra firm tofu, cubed'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 large onion, finely chopped'
  - '2 garlic cloves, minced'
  - '1 large carrot, chopped'
  - '1 pepper, chopped'
  - '1 courgette, chopped'
  - ½ tsp chilli powder
  - 1 tsp cumin
  - 1½ tsp smoked paprika
  - ¼ tsp turmeric
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 2 tbsp tomato paste
  - 1 400g can chopped tomatoes
  - '1 400g can kidney beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 400ml vegetable stock
  - 'Handful fresh coriander, finely chopped'
directions:
  - >-
    Heat the olive oil in a large pan over a medium-high heat. Add the tofu and
    cook for about 5 minutes, turning to brown each side.
  - >-
    Add the onion and carrot and cook over a medium heat for 5 minutes, until
    softened. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  - >-
    Stir in the spices, salt and tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes. Stir in the
    pepper and courgette until coated.
  - >-
    Add the kidney beans, chopped tomatoes and vegetable stock and bring to the
    boil. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer for 15–20 minutes, stirring
    occasionally, until the veggies are softened and the chilli has slightly
    thickened.
  - >-
    Remove from the heat and stir in ¾ of the coriander. Top with the remaining
    coriander. Serve with tortilla chips and guacamole (see recipe).
---
