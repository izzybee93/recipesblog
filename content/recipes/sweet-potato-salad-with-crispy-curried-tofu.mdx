title: Sweet potato salad with crispy curried tofu

categories:

  - salad
featured_image: /images/recipes/sweet-potato-salad-with-crispy-curried-tofu.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - '2 medium sweet potatoes, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '280g crispy curried tofu (see recipe)'
  - 100g rocket
  - 160g sweetcorn
  - Lime and chilli dressing (see recipe)
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Add the sweet potato and brush with 1 tbsp olive oil. Roast for 20 minutes until softened.
  - Make the crispy curried tofu.
  - Make the lime and chilli dressing.
  - >-
    In a large bowl, combine the rocket, sweetcorn, sweet potato and tofu. Pour
    over the dressing and gently mix together
