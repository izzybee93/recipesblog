categories:

  - salad
featured_image: /images/recipes/sweet-potato-salad-with-crispy-curried-tofu.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 'Crispy curried tofu (see recipe)'
  - '1 large sweet potato, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 80g rocket
  - 120g sweetcorn
  - Lime and chilli dressing (see recipe)
directions:
  - >-
    Prepare the crispy curried tofu.
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Add the sweet potato, brush with 1 tbsp olive oil and roast for 20-25 minutes.
  - Make the lime and chilli dressing.
  - >-
    In a large bowl, combine the rocket, sweetcorn, sweet potato and tofu. Pour
    over the dressing and gently mix together.
