---
title: "Roasted vegetables with sesame tofu sauce"
date: "2026-09-27"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/roasted-vegetables-with-sesame-tofu-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '## Any veggie:'
  - '1 aubergine, chopped'
  - '¼ cauliflower, chopped into florets'
  - '¼ broccoli, chopped into florets'
  - '## Sesame tofu sauce'
  - '150g silken tofu, drained'
  - 1 tbsp tahini
  - 1½ tbsp lemon juice
  - ½ tsp miso paste
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - 'Pinch sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '2 tsp sesame seeds, toasted'
directions:
  - 'Preheat the oven to 200°C and prepare a large baking tray. Add the veggies, brush with olive oil and roast for 20-25 minutes, until soft and slightly browned.'
  - 'To make the sesame tofu sauce, add the tofu, tahini, miso, ½ tbsp lemon juice, garlic powder and salt to a blender and blend until smooth and creamy.'
  - 'In a small pan, add the sauce and gently heat for 2-3 minutes, stirring frequently. Add 1 tsp water at a time if needed. Remove from the heat and stir in the remaining 1 tbsp lemon juice and ground black pepper.'
  - Spoon the sauce into serving bowls. Top with the veggies and toasted sesame seeds.
