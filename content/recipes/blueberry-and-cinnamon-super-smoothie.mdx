---
title: "Blueberry and cinnamon super smoothie"
date: "2026-10-09"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/blueberry-and-cinnamon-super-smoothie.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 banana
  - 100g blueberries
  - 30g spinach
  - 1 tsp cinnamon
  - '2 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water'
  - 1 tbsp hemp seeds
  - 1 tbsp peanut butter or almond butter (optional)
  - 4 tbsp oats
  - 200ml plant milk
directions:
  - Place all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
