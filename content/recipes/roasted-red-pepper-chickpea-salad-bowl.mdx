categories:
  - mains

featured_image: "/images/recipes/roasted-red-pepper-chickpea-salad-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - ⅔ chickpeas with roasted red pepper sauce (see recipe)
  - '80g rocket, lettuce or mixed salad leaves'
  - '¼ cucumber, sliced'
  - '1 avocado, chopped'
  - '150g cherry tomatoes, finely chopped'
  - 'Small handful fresh parsley, chopped'
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - 'Pinch sea salt, to taste'
  - '2 pitta breads, warmed (optional)'
directions:
  - Make the chickpeas with roasted red pepper sauce.
  - In a medium bowl, add the leaves, cucumber, tomatoes, parsley, lemon juice and salt. Toss to combine. Add the avocado and gently mix.
  - Divide the salad between serving bowls. Top with the chickpeas and serve with the pitta bread.
