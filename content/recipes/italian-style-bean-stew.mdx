---
title: "Italian style bean stew"
date: "2026-09-27"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/italian-style-bean-stew.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '½ onion, finely chopped'
  - '1 carrot, finely chopped'
  - '1 celery stick, finely chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '1 red pepper, chopped'
  - 1 tsp dried oregano
  - Pinch chilli flakes (optional)
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 1 tbsp tomato paste
  - 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  - 250g passata
  - '1 400g can cannellini beans or borlotti beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 150ml vegetable stock
  - 60g spinach
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - 'Small handful basil leaves, chopped'
  - Tangy tofu cream (optional, see recipe)
directions:
  - Make the tofu cream and set aside.
  - 'In a large pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion, carrot, celery and pepper and cook for 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened. Add the garlic and cook for 1 more minute. Stir in the oregano, chilli flakes, salt and tomato paste, and cook for 2 minutes.'
  - 'Add the balsamic vinegar, passata, beans and vegetable stock and bring to the boil. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened.'
  - 'Stir in the spinach until wilted and season with ground black pepper. Remove from the heat and stir in the basil.'
  - Serve with crusty bread and top with a drizzle of tofu cream.
---
