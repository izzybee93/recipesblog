---
title: "Chickpea, lemon and herb salad"
date: "2025-10-16"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/chickpea-lemon-and-herb-salad.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2-3"
ingredients:
  - 1½ 400g cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  - 200g red and yellow tomatoes, halved
  - ½ medium cucumber, diced
  - 15-20 kalamata olives, halved
  - Small handful fresh parsley, chopped
  - Small handful fresh dill, chopped
  - Small handful fresh mint, chopped
  - Zingy lemon dressing (see recipe)
directions:
  - In a large bowl, add the chickpeas, tomatoes, cucumber and olives. 
  - Make the dressing. Pour over the salad and toss to coat. Add the herbs and toss again. Season to taste and serve.
---
