---
title: "Creamy beetroot butter beans"
date: "2026-07-26"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/creamy-beetroot-butter-beans.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - Creamy beetroot sauce (see recipe)
  - '1 400g can butter beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 250ml vegetable stock
  - 100g spinach
  - 150g peas (optional)
  - '½ tbsp dill or mint leaves, chopped'
  - 'Pinch sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - 'Make the beetroot sauce, reserving the lemon juice.'
  - 'Add the butter beans and vegetable stock. Simmer for 5-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened slightly. Add the peas, if using, and cook for a few more minutes until heated through.'
  - 'Stir in the salt, spinach and herbs until wilted. Remove from the heat and stir in the lemon juice.'
  - Top with ground black pepper and serve with crusty bread.
---
