---
title: "Creamy beetroot butter beans"
date: "2026-07-26"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/creamy-beetroot-butter-beans.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - Creamy beetroot sauce (see recipe)
  - '1 400g can butter beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 125ml vegetable stock
  - 100g spinach
  - '½ tbsp dill or mint leaves, chopped'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - Make the creamy beetroot sauce.
  - Add the butter beans and vegetable stock. Simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened slightly.
  - Stir in the spinach and herbs until wilted.
  - Season with ground black pepper and serve with crusty bread.
