---
title: 'Parsnip, bean and hazelnut dip'
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.295Z'
categories:
  - snacks
featured_image: /images/recipes/parsnip-bean-and-hazelnut-dip.jpeg
servings: serves 8
ingredients:
  - '500g parsnips, peeled and chopped'
  - 2½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 2 garlic cloves
  - 225g baby plum tomatoes
  - '4 sprigs rosemary, finely chopped'
  - '60g hazelnuts, peeled and toasted'
  - '1 400g can butterbeans, drained and rinsed'
  - Juice of 2 lemons
  - 1 tsp sea salt, to taste
  - Ground black pepper, to taste
  - 2-4 tbsp water
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 190°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Add the parsnips and
    brush with 1 tbsp olive oil. Place the garlic cloves in the middle. Cover loosely 
    with foil and roast for 40 minutes.
  - >-
    Prepare a small baking tray. Add the rosemary and tomatoes and coat with ½
    tbsp oil. Roast for 20 minutes, until soft and starting to caramelise.
  - >-
    Roughly chop 20g of the toasted hazelnuts and set aside.
  - >-
    Add the remaining 40g of hazelnuts to a food processor, along with the parsnips,
    1 tbsp olive oil, butterbeans, lemon juice, garlic, ground black pepper and salt. Process for 30 seconds. 
    Add 2 tbsp water and process until smooth, scraping down the sides and adding a few tbsp water as needed.
  - >-
    Transfer to a serving bowl and spoon over the roasted tomatoes. Top with the remaining chopped hazelnuts and extra rosemary.
