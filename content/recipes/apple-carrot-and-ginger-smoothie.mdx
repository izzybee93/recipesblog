---
title: "Apple, carrot and ginger smoothie"
date: "2026-09-15"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/apple-carrot-and-ginger-smoothie.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '1 apple, chopped'
  - '1 medium carrot, grated (~60g)'
  - '2 tbsp chia seeds, soaked (optional)'
  - 1 tbsp lemon juice
  - ¼ tsp lemon zest
  - ¼ tsp fresh ginger
  - ½ tsp cinnamon
  - 1 tbsp almond butter
  - 4 tbsp oats
  - 200ml plant milk
directions:
  - Place all the ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
