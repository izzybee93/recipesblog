title: Sweet potato soup
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.312Z'
categories:
  - mains
  - soup
featured_image: /images/recipes/sweet-potato-soup.jpeg
servings: serves 4
ingredients:
  - '3 large sweet potatoes, chopped'
  - '3 medium carrots, chopped'
  - 3 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 onion, finely chopped'
  - '2 garlic cloves, minced'
  - 1L vegetable stock
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - >-
    100ml oat or soya cream (optional - alternatively, use less stock for a creamy
    texture)
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 200°C. Place the sweet potatoes and carrots on a large
    roasting tin, drizzled with 2 tbsp oil and season. Roast in the oven for
    25-30 minutes or until tender.
  - >-
    Meanwhile, add the remaining 1 tbsp oil to a large saucepan and fry the
    onion over a medium-low heat for about 10 minutes until softened. Add the
    garlic and cook for 1 more minute.
  - >-
    Add the roasted veggies to the pan. Add the stock and simmer for 5 minutes.
  - >-
    Use a hand blender to process until smooth. Stir in the cream if using, a
    season to taste and reheat. Serve topped with ground black pepper.
