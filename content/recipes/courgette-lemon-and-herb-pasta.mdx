title: 'Courgette, lemon and herb pasta'
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.278Z'
categories:
  - mains
  - pasta
featured_image: /images/recipes/courgette-lemon-and-herb-pasta.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 180g pasta
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - '1 large courgette, sliced'
  - '80g sliced artichoke hearts, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp capers
  - 1 tbsp nutritional yeast
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - Zest of ½ lemon
  - 1 tsp mixed herbs, such as oregano, basil and parsley
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - Pinch sea salt
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - Cook the pasta. Reserve ~200ml pasta water.
  - >-
    In a large pan, heat the olive oil over medium-high heat. Cook the courgette for 8 minutes until
    slightly browned. Add the artichoke, capers, half the lemon zest, herbs, garlic and salt and cook for another
    few minutes.
  - >-
    Add the pasta, 150ml reserved pasta water and nutritional yeast to the pan and toss to combine. Cook for 2 minutes over medium heat, adding a few more tbsp pasta water as needed.
  - >-
    Remove from the heat and stir in the lemon juice. Serve topped with a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil, a sprinkle of lemon zest and ground black pepper.
