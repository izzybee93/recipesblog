title: "Oatmeal chocolate chip cookies"
date: "2025-08-16T15:27:07.275Z"

featured_image: "/images/recipes/oatmeal-chocolate-chip-cookies.jpeg"
servings: "makes 18"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  - 3 tbsp water
  - 110g plant butter
  - 100g soft brown sugar
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - 140g plain flour
  - ½ tsp cinnamon
  - ½ tsp baking powder
  - ¼ tsp sea salt
  - 120g rolled oats
  - '80g dark chocolate, chopped'
directions:
  - 'Whisk together the flaxseed and water in a small bowl and set aside for 15 minutes to thicken.'
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C and prepare 2 large baking trays.
  - 'In a large bowl, cream the butter until light and fluffy. Add the sugar and mix again. Add the flax egg and vanilla extract and mix well.'
  - 'In a medium bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, cinnamon and salt. Add the dry ingredients to the wet and mix until a smooth cookie dough forms.'
  - 'Add the oats and chocolate to the dough and mix well.'
  - 'Scoop out 1 tbsp of cookie dough, roll it into a ball and place it on the baking tray. Space the cookies apart and flatten slightly. Bake for 13-15 minutes, or until golden brown. Transfer to a wire rack. The cookies will be soft but will set as they cool.'
