---
title: "Peanut sauce"
date: "2026-08-18"
categories:
  - sauces
featured_image: "/images/recipes/peanut-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp smooth peanut butter
  - Juice of 1 lime
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - 2 tsp maple syrup or honey
  - 50ml plant milk
  - 2-4 tbsp water
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - 'Small handful coriander, finely chopped'
  - Pinch chilli flakes (optional)
  - '1 tsp fresh ginger, minced (optional)'
directions:
  - Whisk together the sauce ingredients in a small jug. 
  - Gently heat the sauce in a small pan for 5 minutes, stirring frequently, until the sauce thickens. Add a few more tbsp water as needed.
  - Serve with your favourite veggie grain bowl.
