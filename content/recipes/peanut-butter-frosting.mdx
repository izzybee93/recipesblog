---
title: "Peanut butter frosting"
date: "2026-10-01"
categories:
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/peanut-butter-frosting.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1 cake or 12 muffins"
ingredients:
  - 60g plant butter
  - 50g smooth peanut butter
  - 120g icing sugar, sieved, sieved
  - Pinch sea salt
  - 1 tsp plant milk
directions:
  - 'In a medium bowl, beat the plant butter and peanut butter until creamy.'
  - 'Gradually add the icing sugar and salt and beat until light and fluffy.'
  - 'Add the plant milk and beat until smooth and fluffy.'
---
