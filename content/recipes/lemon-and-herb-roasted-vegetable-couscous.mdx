---
title: Lemon and herb roasted vegetable couscous
date: '2021-08-23 22:52 +0000'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/lemon-and-herb-roasted-vegetable-couscous.jpeg
servings: serves 3
ingredients:
  - 150g couscous
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - ~300ml vegetable stock
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - Zest of ½ lemon (optional)
  - ½ tsp turmeric
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - ¼ tsp cumin (optional)
  - ¼ tsp chilli flakes (optional)
  - 1 tsp dried mixed herbs, such as oregano, basil and parsley
  - 2 tbsp pine nuts, toasted (optional)
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '## Any 2 veggies:'
  - '1 pepper, chopped'
  - '1 courgette, chopped'
  - '¼ broccoli, chopped'
  - '¼ cauliflower, chopped'
  - '¼ butternut squash, cubed'
  - ½ aubergine, chopped
  - '## Any additional veggie:'
  - '6 sun-dried tomatoes, chopped'
  - '15 olives, chopped'
  - '80g peas, cooked'
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Add the veggies
    and brush with 1 tbsp olive oil. Add the chickpeas and mix together. Roast for 15–25 minutes, until softened and lightly browned.
  - >-
    In a large bowl, add the couscous and cover with vegetable stock until the water is 0.5cm above the couscous. Set aside for 10 minutes.
  - >-
    Run a fork through the couscous. Add the lemon juice, lemon zest, spices and herbs and mix well. Add
    the veggies, chickpeas and pine nuts and gently mix. Add a drizzle of olive oil and mix again.
    Serve topped with ground black pepper.
