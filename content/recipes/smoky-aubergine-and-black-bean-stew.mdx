---
title: "Smoky aubergine and black bean stew"
date: "2026-09-18"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/smoky-aubergine-and-black-bean-stew.jpeg"
servings: "serves 3"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '½ onion, finely chopped'
  - '1 large aubergine, chopped'
  - '1 red pepper, chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, finely chopped'
  - 1½ tsp smoked paprika
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - 1 tsp ground coriander
  - 1 tsp dried oregano
  - ¼ tsp chilli powder
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 1 tbsp tomato purée
  - '2 large tomatoes, roughly chopped'
  - '1 400g can black beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 250ml vegetable stock
  - Juice of ½ lime
directions:
  - 'Heat the oil in a large pan over a medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, until softened. Add the aubergine and red pepper and cook for 8-10 minutes, until lightly browned.'
  - 'Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Add the smoked paprika, cumin, coriander, oregano, chilli powder, salt and tomato purée and cook for another minute.'
  - 'Stir in the tomatoes, cover and leave to simmer for 5-10 minutes until softened.'
  - 'Add the black beans and vegetable stock and bring to the boil. Reduce to a low heat and simmer for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened. Remove from the heat and stir in the lime juice.'
  - Serve with warmed flatbread or quinoa.
---
