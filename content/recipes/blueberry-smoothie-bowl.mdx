featured_image: /images/recipes/blueberry-smoothie-bowl.jpeg
servings: serves 1
ingredients:
  - '½ frozen banana, chopped'
  - 80g frozen blueberries
  - 1 tbsp almond butter
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 2 tbsp oats
  - ¼ tsp lemon zest
  - 50ml plant milk
  - 1 tbsp granola
  - 1 tbsp almonds, chopped
directions:
  - >-
    Blend the banana, half of the blueberries, almond butter, chia seeds, oats, 
    lemon zest and plant milk until smooth. Transfer to a bowl and top with remaining
    blueberries, granola and almonds.
