servings: makes 18
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  - 3 tbsp water
  - 145g plain flour
  - 35g cocoa powder
  - ½ tsp baking powder
  - ¼ tsp salt
  - 90g plant butter
  - 85g caster sugar
  - Zest of 1 orange
  - ½ tsp vanilla extract
  - '50g dark chocolate, chopped'
directions:
  - Whisk together the flaxseed and water in a small bowl and set aside for 15 minutes to thicken.
  - In a medium bowl, combine the flour, cocoa powder, baking powder and salt.
  - In a large bowl, rub the orange zest and sugar together using your fingers. Add the butter and beat together until light and creamy. Beat in the vanilla extract and flax egg.
  - Add the flour mixture and mix until just combined. Fold in the chocolate. Transfer to the fridge for 30 minutes.
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a large baking tray.
  - Scoop out spoonfuls of dough and roll into a ball. Place on the tray, leaving space between each cookie, then lightly flatten. Bake for 12–15 minutes. Leave to cool slightly before transferring to a wire rack.
