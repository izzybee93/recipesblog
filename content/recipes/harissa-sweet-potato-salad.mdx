featured_image: /images/recipes/harissa-sweet-potato-salad.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - '2 sweet potatoes, chopped'
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - 1 tsp cumin
  - 100g rocket
  - Harissa dressing (see recipee)
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  - >-
    Place the sweet potato and chickpeas on a large baking tray. Brush with 1
    tbsp olive oil, and sprinkle over the paprika and cumin. Cook for 25 minutes
    until the sweet potatoes are soft.
  - Make the harissa dressing.
  - >-
    Transfer to a large bowl. Add the rocket. Pour the dressing over the salad and mix well until coated.
