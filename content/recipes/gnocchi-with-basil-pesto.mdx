---
title: "Gnocchi with basil pesto"
date: "2026-04-28"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/gnocchi-with-basil-pesto.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 300g gnocchi
  - ½ tbsp olive oil
  - ½ basil pesto (see recipe)
  - 2 tbsp lemon juice
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '## Any 2 veggies:'
  - 200g cherry tomatoes
  - 1 courgette, chopped
  - 200g frozen peas
  - 60g spinach
directions:
  - Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the courgette and cook for 5 minutes, until softened. Stir in the peas and cook for 5 more minutes. 
  - Reduce to a medium-low heat and stir in the pesto, tomatoes and spinach.
  - Cook the gnocchi.
  - Add to the pan and toss until coated, adding a few splashes of leftover water as needed. Remove from the heat and stir in the lemon juice.
  - Serve topped with ground black pepper.
