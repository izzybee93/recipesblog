servings: makes 2
ingredients:
  - 2 aubergines
  - ½ tbsp olive oil
  - '2 spring onions, finely sliced'
  - 2 tsp sesame seeds
  - '## Glaze'
  - 4 tsp miso paste
  - 2 tsp maple syrup or honey
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - 2 tsp sesame oil
  - 1 tsp rice vinegar
  - '½ tbsp fresh ginger, minced'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - 2–3 tbsp water
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a baking tray.
  - >-
    Slice the aubergines in half length-ways and score the flesh in a diamond
    pattern, taking care not to cut through the skin. Place on the
    baking tray, cut sides up. Brush with the olive oil and roast for 25 minutes.
  - >-
    In a small bowl, mix the sauce ingredients until a smooth paste forms. Brush over the aubergine
    and roast for 5–10 minutes, until softened and caramelised. 
  - Serve with a sprinkle of spring onions and sesame seeds.
