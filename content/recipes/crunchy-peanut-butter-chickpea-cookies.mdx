---
title: "Crunchy peanut butter chickpea cookies"
date: "2026-08-13"
categories:
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/crunchy-peanut-butter-chickpea-cookies.jpeg"
servings: "makes 24"
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 180g natural peanut butter
  - 80g caster sugar
  - 2 tsp vanilla extract
  - 70g plain flour
  - 1 tsp baking powder
  - '½ tsp sea salt (if peanut butter is unsalted)'
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare two baking trays.
  - 'Add the chickpeas, peanut butter, sugar and vanilla extract to a food processor and blend until smooth. Add 2-4 tbsp water as needed.'
  - 'Add the flour, baking powder and salt (if needed), and pulse until combined.'
  - 'Scoop out 1½ tbsp of dough at a time and roll into a ball. Place on the trays, 2cm apart. Using the back of a fork, flatten in a crisscross pattern.'
  - 'Bake for 15–18 minutes, until golden around the edges. For crunchier cookies, bake for a few minutes longer. Leave to cool for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.'
