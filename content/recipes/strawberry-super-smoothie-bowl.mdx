---
title: "Strawberry super smoothie bowl"
date: "2026-08-18"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/strawberry-super-smoothie-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - 70g frozen strawberries
  - 30g beetroot
  - 2 tbsp oats
  - 1 tsp almond butter
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 50ml plant milk
  - ½ tsp lemon zest
  - '1 kiwi, sliced'
  - '1 tbsp almonds, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp hemp seeds
directions:
  - 'Blend the strawberries, beetroot, almond butter, chia seeds, lemon zest, oats and plant milk until smooth. Transfer to a bowl and top with kiwi slices, almonds and hemp seeds.'
