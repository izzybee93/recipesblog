---
title: Doughnuts
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.282Z'
categories:
  - treats
featured_image: /images/recipes/doughnuts.jpeg
servings: "makes 12"
ingredients:
  - 125g plain flour
  - 70g granulated sugar
  - 1 tsp baking powder
  - ¼ tsp sea salt
  - ½ tsp cinnamon
  - 150ml soya milk
  - 1 tbsp applesauce or 2 tbsp aquafaba
  - 1 tbsp plant butter, melted
  - 1 tbsp vanilla extract
  - '## Glaze'
  - 200g icing sugar
  - 2 tbsp soya milk
  - 1-3 drops of food colouring
  - Sprinkles
  - '## Cinnamon sugar topping'
  - '2 tbsp plant butter, melted'
  - 60g caster sugar
  - 1 tsp cinnamon
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C and brush a doughnut pan with vegetable oil.
  - 'Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, salt and cinnamon in a large bowl.'
  - >-
    In a large measuring jug, add the soy milk, applesauce/aquafaba, melted
    butter and vanilla and mix together. Pour into the dry ingredients and
    mix well to combine.
  - >-
    Spoon the batter into the donut pan, filling each ring ¾ full. Do not overfill.
  - >-
    Bake for 10-12 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden brown. Leave to cool
    in the pan for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack
    with a large piece of parchment paper beneath it.
  - >-
    To make the glaze, whisk together the icing sugar, soya milk and food
    colouring. Spoon over the doughnuts, or dip each doughnut in the glaze. Top
    with sprinkles. Allow to set before serving.
  - >-
    To make the cinnamon sugar topping, whisk together the sugar and cinnamon. Brush
    each doughnut with melted butter, then dip in the cinnamon sugar. Best
    served the same day.
