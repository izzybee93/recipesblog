---
title: "Gnocchi"
date: "2026-09-07"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/gnocchi.jpeg"
servings: "serves 3"
ingredients:
  - 500g potatoes
  - ~90g plain flour
  - 1 tsp sea salt
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a baking tray. Prick the potatoes in several places and bake for 50-60 minutes, until soft.
  - Leave to cool for 10 minutes, then slice open and scoop the flesh into a bowl. Mash until smooth.
  - Mix 70g flour and the salt together, then create a small mound and make a well in the centre. Place the mashed potato in the centre and gently bring together. Add more flour as needed, being careful not to overwork the dough.
  - Dust a surface with flour. Divide the dough evenly into four pieces. Roll each piece into a 2.5cm-thick log.
  - Cut the logs into 2.5cm-long pieces. Press each piece with a fork to create grooves.
  - Bring a large pan of salted water to the boil. Cook the gnocchi for a few minutes, until they rise to the surface.
  - Serve with your favourite pesto or tomato sauce and roasted veggies.
