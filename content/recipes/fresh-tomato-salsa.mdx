---
title: "Fresh tomato salsa"
date: "2026-03-02"
categories:
  - snacks
featured_image: "/images/recipes/fresh-tomato-salsa.jpeg"
servings: "serves 6-8"
ingredients:
  - '¼ onion'
  - '4 plum tomatoes, chopped'
  - Small handful fresh coriander
  - Juice and zest of 1 lime
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - '½ tsp chilli flakes, to taste'
  - ¼ tsp cumin
  - '½ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - ⅛ tsp sugar
directions:
  - Roughly slice the onion and place in a bowl of cold water for 5 minutes.
  - In a food processor, add the onion, tomatoes, coriander, lime juice, lime zest, garlic, chilli, cumin, salt and sugar. Pulse until a chunky mixture forms.
  - If the consistency is too watery, strain half to remove some liquid. Combine the strained mixture with the remaining salsa. Season to taste. 
  - Chill for 20 minutes before serving with tortillas, tacos or fajitas.
