---
title: "Lemon, dill and white bean dip"
date: "2026-07-26"
categories:
  - snacks
featured_image: "/images/recipes/lemon-dill-and-white-bean-dip.jpeg"
servings: "serves 4–6"
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 1 garlic clove
  - '2 tbsp capers, drained'
  - Zest of ¼ lemon
  - ½ tsp Dijon mustard
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - '½ tsp sea salt, or to taste'
  - Ground black pepper, to taste
  - 'Small handful fresh dill, chopped'
  - '1 tbsp capers, finely chopped'
  - 2–3 tbsp water
directions:
  - Finely chop 1 tbsp of the capers and set aside.
  - Add the cannellini beans, garlic, 2 tbsp capers, lemon zest, lemon juice, olive oil, mustard, salt and black pepper to a food processor.
  - Blend until almost smooth.
  - Add the water, 1 tbsp at a time, and blend until smooth.
  - Stir through the dill and reserved chopped capers. Transfer to the fridge for 1 hour before serving.
  - Serve with crunchy vegetables, salad or spread on toasted sourdough.
