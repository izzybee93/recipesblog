---
title: Carrot couscous salad
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.268Z'
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: /images/recipes/carrot-couscous-salad.jpeg
servings: serves 3
ingredients:
  - 150g couscous
  - '2 large carrots, grated'
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 50g sultanas or dried cranberries
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - 1 tsp turmeric
  - 1 tsp cumin
  - ½ tsp ground coriander
  - ¼ tsp ground ginger
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - ½ tsp sea salt
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - Small handful parsley, chopped
directions:
  - >-
    In a large bowl, add the couscous then ~300ml boiling water, pouring until the 
    water is 0.5cm above the couscous. Set aside for 10 minutes.
  - >-
    Heat a large pan over medium heat and add the chickpeas. Sprinkle with half the turmeric and cumin, 
    and toss until coated. Cook for 10 minutes until slightly crispy.
  - >-
    Using a fork, loosen the couscous. Add the lemon juice, spices, seasoning and olive
    oil and mix well. Add the carrot, chickpeas and dried fruit then mix together. Stir in the parsley. Serve chilled.
