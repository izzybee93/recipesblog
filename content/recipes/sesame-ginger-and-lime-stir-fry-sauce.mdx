title: 'Sesame, ginger and lime stir fry sauce'
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.303Z'
categories:
  - sauces
featured_image: /images/recipes/sesame-ginger-and-lime-stir-fry-sauce.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 4 tbsp soy sauce
  - Juice of ½ lime
  - 1 tsp maple syrup or honey
  - 1 tsp sesame oil
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '1 tsp fresh ginger, minced'
  - '½ tsp chilli flakes (optional)'
  - 1 tsp sesame seeds, toasted
  - 125ml water
  - 1 tsp cornflour
directions:
  - >-
    In a small jug, mix all the ingredients together except the cornflour. Stir in the cornflour until dissolved.
  - >-
    Add to the stir fry and cook on a medium-high heat until the sauce thickens.
