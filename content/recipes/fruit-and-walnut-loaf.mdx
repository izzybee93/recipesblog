---
title: "Fruit and walnut loaf"
date: "2026-09-04"
categories:
  - bread
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/fruit-and-walnut-loaf.jpeg"
servings: "makes 1 loaf"
ingredients:
  - 390g strong white bread flour
  - 7g instant yeast
  - 10g sea salt
  - '90g walnuts, chopped'
  - '100g dried fruit, such as cranberries, goji berries, raisins or blueberries'
  - 20g maple syrup or honey
  - 360ml lukewarm water
directions:
  - 'Add the dried fruit to a small bowl, cover with hot water and set aside for 10 minutes, then drain.'
  - 'In a large bowl, combine the flour, yeast, salt, walnuts and fruit. In a measuring jug, combine the maple syrup and water. Add to the dry ingredients and mix until a sticky dough forms.'
  - 'Cover with a damp tea towel and leave for 2 hours, until doubled in size.'
  - Prepare a loaf tin.
  - 'Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and gently shape into a log. Transfer to the tin, cover with a damp tea towel and leave to rise for 1 hour.'
  - Preheat the oven to 220°C.
  - 'Bake for 35-40 minutes, until golden brown and the loaf sounds hollow when tapped.'
  - Transfer to a wire rack and leave to cool for at least 30 minutes before slicing.
---
