---
title: "Miso glazed chickpeas"
date: "2026-08-19"
categories:
  - snacks
featured_image: "/images/recipes/miso-glazed-chickpeas.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 1 tbsp sesame oil
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - 'Small handful coriander, chopped (optional)'
  - 1 tsp sesame seeds (optional)
  - '## Sauce'
  - '1 tsp fresh ginger, minced'
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - 2 tsp maple syrup
  - 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  - 1 tbsp miso paste
  - 2 tsp cornflour
  - 2 tbsp water
directions:
  - Whisk together the sauce ingredients in a small jug.
  - 'Heat the sesame oil in a large pan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Add the sauce and cook for a few minutes, stirring frequently until thickened.'
  - Stir in the chickpeas. Cover and cook over medium heat for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add a few more tbsp water as needed.
  - 'Serve topped with coriander and a sprinkle of sesame seeds, alongside stir-fried veggies, rice or noodles.'
