---
title: "Pasta with peas, spinach and creamy beetroot sauce"
date: "2026-07-26"
categories:
  - mains
  - pasta
featured_image: "/images/recipes/pasta-with-peas-spinach-and-creamy-beetroot-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 160g pasta
  - ¾ creamy beetroot sauce (see recipe)
  - 150g frozen peas
  - 100g spinach
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '1 tsp dill or mint leaves, chopped'
  - ½ tsp lemon zest
directions:
  - Make the creamy beetroot sauce, setting aside the lemon juice.
  - Cook the pasta and peas, reserving 150ml pasta water.
  - 'Add the pasta water to the sauce and cook over medium-low heat for 5 minutes, stirring frequently.'
  - 'Add the ground black pepper and spinach, and stir until wilted. Reduce to a low heat and stir in the herbs and lemon juice.'
  - Add the pasta and peas and toss together until coated. Serve topped with a sprinkle of lemon zest.
