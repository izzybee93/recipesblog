---
title: "Pasta with peas, spinach and creamy beetroot sauce"
date: "2026-07-26"
categories:
  - mains
  - pasta
featured_image: "/images/recipes/pasta-with-peas-spinach-and-creamy-beetroot-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 160g pasta
  - Creamy beetroot sauce (see recipe)
  - 150g frozen peas
  - 100g spinach
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '1 tsp dill or mint leaves, chopped'
  - ½ tsp lemon zest
directions:
  - Cook the pasta and peas, reserving some pasta water.
  - Make the creamy beetroot sauce. Add a few tbsp pasta water as needed.
  - Stir in the spinach and herbs until wilted. Season with ground black pepper.
  - Add the pasta and peas and toss together until coated. Serve topped with lemon zest.
