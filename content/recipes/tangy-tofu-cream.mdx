---
title: "Tangy tofu cream"
date: "2026-09-18"
categories:
  - sauces
featured_image: "/images/recipes/tangy-tofu-cream.jpeg"
servings: "makes 1 small jar"
ingredients:
  - 100g silken tofu
  - 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
  - ¼ tsp sea salt
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - Pinch turmeric
  - ½ tsp Dijon mustard
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - 50ml water
directions:
  - 'Add all the ingredients to a blender and blend until smooth. Transfer to the fridge for at least 2 hours before serving.'
  - 'Drizzle over a stew, soup or grain bowl.'
---
