title: Harissa black bean stew
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.314Z'
categories:

featured_image: /images/recipes/tomato-and-black-bean-stew.jpeg
servings: serves 3
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - ½ onion, finely chopped
  - 1 garlic clove, minced
  - 2 tsp harissa paste
  - 1 tsp cumin
  - 1 tsp dried oregano
  - 1 tbsp tomato paste
  - 1 carrot, chopped
  - 1 pepper, chopped
  - 1 400g can black beans, drained and rinsed
  - 250ml vegetable stock
  - 1 400g can chopped tomatoes
  - Ground black pepper, to taste
  - 2 tsp lemon juice

directions:
- Heat the oil in a large pan over a medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, until softened. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
- Add the harissa paste, cumin, oregano and tomato paste and cook for 1 minute. Add the carrot and pepper and cook for 5 minutes.
- Add the black beans, chopped tomatoes and vegetable stock and bring to the boil. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened.
- Remove from the heat and stir in the lemon juice and ground black pepper.
- Serve with crusty bread.
