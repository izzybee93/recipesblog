---
title: "Creamy pasta sauce"
date: "2026-07-26"
categories:
  - sauces
featured_image: "/images/recipes/creamy-pasta-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1-2"
ingredients:
  - 150ml soya milk
  - 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 1 tsp white miso paste
  - 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - Juice of ½ lemon
directions:
  - Whisk together all the ingredients except the lemon juice in a measuring jug. Add more milk as needed.
  - Heat a pan over medium heat. Add the sauce and stir frequently until slightly thickened. Add a few tbsp pasta water as needed. 
  - Reduce to a low heat and stir in the lemon juice.
---
