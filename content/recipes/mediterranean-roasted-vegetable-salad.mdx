categories:

  - salad
featured_image: /images/recipes/mediterranean-roasted-vegetable-salad.jpeg
servings: serves 3
ingredients:
  - 90g rocket
  - '2 large tomatoes, sliced'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 red pepper, sliced'
  - '1 courgette, sliced'
  - '½ red onion, sliced'
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - Balsamic dressing (see recipe)
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 180°C. Place the red pepper, courgette and red onion on
    a baking tray and drizzle with 1 tbsp olive oil. Bake for 15-20 minutes.
  - >-
    Place the rocket, tomatoes and chickpeas in a large bowl. Add the roasted
    vegetables and gently mix.
  - Make the balsamic dressing.
  - Pour the dressing over the salad and gently mix until coated.
