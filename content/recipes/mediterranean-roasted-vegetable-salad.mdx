servings: serves 3
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 pepper, sliced'
  - '1 courgette, sliced'
  - '½ red onion, quartered'
  - 1 tsp dried oregano
  - 100g rocket
  - '2 large tomatoes, chopped'
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - Balsamic dressing (see recipe)
  - 2 tbsp pine nuts, toasted (optional)
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a large baking tray, add the pepper, courgette and onion. Coat 
    with the olive oil and sprinkle with the oregano. Add the chickpeas and roast for 20 minutes, until starting to brown.
  - Make the balsamic dressing.
  - >-
    In a large bowl, add the rocket, tomatoes, roasted veggies and chickpeas and gently mix. Pour the dressing
    over the salad and gently mix. Serve topped with toasted pine nuts.
