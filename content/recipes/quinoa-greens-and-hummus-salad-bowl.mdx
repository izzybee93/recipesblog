---
title: "Quinoa, greens and hummus salad bowl"
date: "2026-03-30"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/quinoa-greens-and-hummus-salad-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 60g quinoa
  - '¼ broccoli head, chopped'
  - '¼ cucumber, chopped'
  - 120g chickpeas
  - ½ tsp smoked paprika
  - '120g edamame, cooked'
  - '12 olives, chopped'
  - '10g fresh coriander, chopped'
  - '## Any hummus:'
  - 4 tbsp lemon and coriander hummus (see recipe)
  - 4 tbsp chickpea almond hummus (see recipe)
  - 4 tbsp hummus (see recipe)
  - '## Dressing'
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - Pinch sea salt, to taste
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the quinoa.
  - In a small pan over medium heat, add the chickpeas and cook for a few minutes. Add the smoked paprika and toss until coated. Cook for 10 minutes, until slightly crispy.
  - Whisk together the dressing ingredients in a small jug.
  - In a large bowl, add the broccoli, cucumber, edamame, chickpeas, olives, quinoa and coriander. Pour over the dressing and mix well. Serve topped with spoonfuls of lemon and coriander hummus.
