title: "Creamy green pasta with mushroom, tomato and crispy balsamic tofu"
date: "2026-07-24"
categories:
  - mains
  - pasta
featured_image: "/images/recipes/creamy-green-pasta-with-mushroom-tomato-and-crispy-balsamic-tofu.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 160g pasta
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '200g mushrooms, sliced'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - Creamy green sauce (see recipe)
  - 150g cherry tomatoes
  - ½ crispy balsamic tofu bites (see recipe)
directions:
  - Make the crispy balsamic tofu bites.
  - Cook the pasta, reserving some pasta water.
  - In a large pan, heat the olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms and cook for 5 minutes, until lightly browned. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  - Make the creamy green sauce.
  - Add the pasta and toss to combine. Stir in the tomatoes until heated through. Serve topped with the crispy tofu.
