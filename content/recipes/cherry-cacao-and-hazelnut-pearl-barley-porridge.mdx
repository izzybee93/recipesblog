---
title: Cherry, cacao and hazelnut pearl barley porridge
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.269Z'
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: /images/recipes/cherry-cacao-and-hazelnut-pearl-barley-porridge.jpeg
servings: serves 1
ingredients:
  - '120g pearl barley, cooked'
  - 100ml plant milk
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 100g cherries
  - 1 tsp cacoa nibs
  - '1 tsp hazelnuts, chopped'
  - 1 tsp hazelnut butter or dark chocolate hazelnut spread
directions:
  - >-
    In a small pan, add the pearl barley, milk, chia seeds and ¾ of the cherries and cook for 5 minutes over medium heat, stirring frequently until the juices start to release.
  - Serve topped with the remaining cherries, cacao nibs and a drizzle of hazelnut butter or dark chocolate hazelnut spread.
---
