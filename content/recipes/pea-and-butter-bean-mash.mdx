title: Pea and butter bean mash
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.295Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/pea-and-butter-bean-mash.jpeg
servings: serves 3
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can butter beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 150g frozen peas
  - Juice of ½ lemon 
  - Zest of ½ lemon
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - 'Large handful fresh parsley, chopped'
  - '½ tsp sea salt, to taste'
directions:
  - Cook the peas for 1 minute.
  - >-
    Add all the ingredients to a food processor and blend until almost smooth.
    Season to taste.
  - >-
    Gently heat the mash in a pan on a medium-low heat for 5-10 minutes, then
    serve.
