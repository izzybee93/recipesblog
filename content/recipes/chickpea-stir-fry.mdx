title: Chickpea stir fry
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.272Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/chickpea-stir-fry.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 100g rice or noodles
  - 1 tbsp sesame oil
  - '½ onion, chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - 60g spinach
  - '## Any 2 veggies:'
  - '120g broccoli, chopped'
  - '1 pepper, chopped'
  - '150g mushrooms, sliced'
  - '120g baby corn, chopped'
  - '## Sauce'
  - Almond stir fry sauce (see recipe)
  - '## Garnish'
  - 2 tsp sesame seeds
directions:
  - Cook the rice or noodles.
  - >-
    In a large pan, heat the sesame oil over a medium heat. Add the onion and
    cook for a few minutes until softened. Add the garlic and veggies and cook
    for 5 minutes, followed by the chickpeas and cook for another 5 minutes,
    stirring occasionally.
  - >-
    Make the almond stir fry sauce. Add the sauce to the pan and cook for 5 minutes, until heated through. Add a few tbsp water as needed.
  - >-
    Stir through the spinach until wilted. Serve with the rice or noodles and topped with sesame seeds.
