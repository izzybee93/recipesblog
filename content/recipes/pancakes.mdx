  - breakfast
featured_image: /images/recipes/pancakes.jpeg
servings: makes 5
ingredients:
  - 125g plain flour
  - 2 tsp baking powder or 8g Paneangeli Lievito Vanigliato
  - 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  - 225ml plant milk
  - Plant butter for frying
directions:
  - >-
    Add the apple cider vinegar milk to a jug and set aside for 2
    minutes without stirring.
  - Whisk together the flour and baking powder.
  - 'Heat a pan over medium-high heat, then add ½ tsp of butter.'
  - >-
    Pour the milk mixture into the flour and gently whisk together - do not
    overmix.
  - >-
    Spoon a ladle of batter into the pan and cook for a few minutes, until
    bubbles stop forming on the top. Flip the pancake and cook for another few
    minutes, until golden brown on both sides.
  - Serve with your favourite toppings.
