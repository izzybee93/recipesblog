---
title: "Pickled radishes"
date: "2026-04-15"
categories:
  - salad
  - snacks
featured_image: "/images/recipes/pickled-radishes.jpeg"
servings: "makes 1 jar"
ingredients:
  - '200g radishes, thinly sliced'
  - 180ml apple cider vinegar or rice vinegar
  - 180ml water
  - 1 tbsp caster sugar
  - 1 tsp sea salt
  - '## Optional flavours'
  - 1 tsp mustard seeds
  - 1 tsp coriander seeds
  - ¼ tsp chilli flakes
  - ½ tsp black peppercorns
  - 1 sprig fresh dill
directions:
  - Put the radishes in a mason jar, pushing down until lightly packed.
  - In a small pan, add the water and vinegar. Bring to a simmer, then stir in the sugar and salt until dissolved. Add the optional flavourings.
  - Pour into the jar, ensuring all the radishes are covered. Loosely cover with the lid and set aside for 1 hour. Seal tightly and transfer to the fridge for up to 3 weeks.
  - Serve as a side with a veggie grain bowl or tossed in a salad.
