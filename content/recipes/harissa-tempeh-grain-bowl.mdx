---
title: "Harissa tempeh grain bowl"
date: "2026-06-30"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/harissa-tempeh-grain-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '100g tempeh, sliced'
  - 100g quinoa or rice
  - '120g edamame, cooked'
  - '½ cucumber, chopped'
  - 'Small handful mint leaves, chopped (optional)'
  - '## Marinade'
  - 1 tsp harissa paste
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - Juice of ½ lime
  - '## Any 1-2 veggies:'
  - '1 medium aubergine, chopped and roasted'
  - '1 pepper, chopped and roasted'
  - ¼ broccoli, chopped and roasted
  - '## Dressing'
  - Harissa dressing (see recipe)
directions:
  - Whisk the marinade ingredients together in a small bowl. Place the tempeh in a shallow dish and pour over the marinade. Set aside for 10 minutes.
  - Rinse and cook the quinoa or rice.
  - Heat a pan over medium heat. Add the tempeh and cook for 5-10 minutes, turning to brown each side.
  - Make the harissa dressing.
  - Assemble the bowls with the quinoa or rice, then top with tempeh, edamame, cucumber and veggies. Pour over the dressing. Serve with a garnish of mint leaves.
