---
title: "Passion fruit and raspberry smoothie bowl"
date: "2026-08-08"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/passion-fruit-and-raspberry-smoothie-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - '½ frozen banana, chopped'
  - 2 passion fruit
  - 1 tbsp almond butter
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 2 tbsp oats
  - 50ml plant milk
  - 25g raspberries
  - 1 tbsp granola
directions:
  - 'Blend the banana, passion fruit, almond butter, chia seeds, oats and plant milk until smooth. Transfer to a bowl and top with raspberries and granola.'
