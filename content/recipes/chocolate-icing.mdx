---
title: Chocolate icing
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.276Z'
categories:
  - treats
featured_image: /images/recipes/chocolate-icing.jpeg
servings: serves 1 cake
ingredients:
  - 60g plant butter
  - 180g icing sugar, sieved
  - 30g cocoa powder
  - Pinch sea salt
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - 1–2 tbsp plant milk
directions:
  - In a medium bowl, beat the plant butter until creamy. Gradually add the icing sugar, cocoa powder, salt and vanilla and beat until light and fluffy.
  - Add the plant milk, 1 tbsp at a time, and beat until smooth and spreadable.
