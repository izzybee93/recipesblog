title: "Pasta with peas, spinach and creamy green sauce"
date: "2026-07-26"
categories:
  - mains
  - pasta
featured_image: "/images/recipes/pasta-with-peas-spinach-and-creamy-green-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 160g pasta
  - Creamy green sauce (see recipe)
  - 150g frozen peas
  - 100g spinach
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - Cook the pasta and peas, reserving some pasta water.
  - Make the creamy green sauce. Add a few tbsp pasta water as needed.
  - Stir in the spinach until wilted.
  - Add the pasta and peas and toss together until coated. Serve topped with ground black pepper.
