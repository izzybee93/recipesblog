title: Sesame tofu salad bowl
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.314Z'
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: /images/recipes/sesame-tofu-salad-bowl.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - '200g extra firm tofu, cubed'
  - '150g edamame, cooked'
  - '2 carrots, grated'
  - '¼ cucumber, chopped'
  - '## Any 2 veggies:'
  - 80g sugar snap peas
  - ½ avocado, sliced
  - 6 tbsp pickled red cabbage (see recipe)
  - 4 radishes, sliced
  - '## Dressing'
  - Sesame salad dressing (see recipe)
  - 4 tsp sesame seeds
directions:
  - >-
    Heat a pan over medium heat. Add the tofu and cook for 10-15 minutes, turning to brown each side.
  - >-
    Make the sesame salad dressing. Stir in 2 tsp sesame seeds. Add half the dressing to the tofu and cook for 5 more minutes. 
  - >-
    Assemble the salads, drizzle with the dressing, and sprinkle with the remaining sesame seeds.
