title: "Stuffed courgettes"
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.311Z'
categories:

featured_image: "/images/recipes/stuffed-courgettes.jpeg"
servings: "serves 3"
ingredients:
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '1 small red onion, finely chopped'
  - 3 large courgettes
  - '1 aubergine, chopped'
  - '1 yellow pepper, chopped'
  - 2 tbsp tomato paste
  - 1 400g can chopped tomatoes
  - 1 tsp dried oregano
  - ½ tsp dried thyme
  - ¼ tsp chilli flakes
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  - 'Small handful fresh basil, chopped'
  - '## Topping'
  - 3 tbsp nutritional yeast
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - 3 tsp extra virgin olive oil
directions:
  - 'Preheat the oven to 200°C. Chop the courgettes in half lengthways and scoop out the middle and set aside. Place the halves on a prepared baking tray, brush with a little olive oil and roast for 10 minutes.'
  - 'Heat the olive oil in a large pan. Add the onion, aubergine and yellow pepper and cook for a few minutes. Add the garlic, remaining courgette middles, tomato paste, oregano, thyme, chilli flakes and salt. Cook for 2 minutes, then stir in the chopped tomatoes. Simmer for 10-15 minutes, until the vegetables begin to soften and the sauce has thickened. Remove from the heat, then stir in the balsamic vinegar and basil.'
  - 'Fill the courgettes with the ratatouille mixture. Sprinkle over the nutritional yeast and ground black pepper. then drizzle with extra virgin olive oil. Bake for 10-20 minutes, until the topping has lightly toasted. Serve any leftover ratatouille as a side, or store in the fridge for up to 5 days.'
  - 'Optionally serve alongside lemon and herb chickpea couscous (see recipe) or creamy lemon and herb white beans (see recipe).'
