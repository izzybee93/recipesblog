---
title: "Carrot and quinoa salad"
date: "2025-11-12"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/carrot-and-quinoa-salad.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 70g quinoa
  - 2 large carrots, grated
  - 150g cherry tomatoes, chopped
  - ¼ cucumber, chopped
  - 80g chickpeas
  - Balsamic dressing (see recipe)
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the quinoa.
  - Make the balsamic dressing.
  - In a medium bowl, combine the carrots, quinoa, cucumber, tomatoes and chickpeas. Pour over the dressing and mix well.
