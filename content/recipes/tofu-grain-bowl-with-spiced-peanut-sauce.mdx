---
title: "Tofu grain bowl with spiced peanut sauce"
date: "2025-08-17"
categories: ["mains"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/tofu-grain-bowl-with-spiced-peanut-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g rice or quinoa
  - 280g extra firm tofu, cubed
  - ¼ cucumber, sliced
  - 2 tsp sesame seeds
  - '## Marinade'
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 tbsp rice vinegar or lime juice
  - 1 tsp sesame oil
  - '## Any 2 veggies:'
  - 100g edamame, cooked
  - 1 pepper, chopped
  - ¼ broccoli, chopped
  - 100g mushroom, chopped
  - 1 small aubergine, chopped
  - '## Sauce'
  - Spiced peanut sauce (see recipe)
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the rice or quinoa.
  - Whisk the marinade ingredients together in a small bowl. Place the tofu in a shallow dish and pour over the marinade. Set aside for 10 minutes.
  - 'Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a baking tray. Place the tofu and veggies on the tray and bake for 20-25 minutes, until the tofu is golden brown and crispy.'
  - Make the spiced peanut sauce.
  - Assemble the bowls with rice or quinoa, tofu, veggies and cucumber slices. Drizzle over the peanut sauce, and top with sesame seeds.
