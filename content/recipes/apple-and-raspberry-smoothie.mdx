---
title: "Apple and raspberry smoothie"
date: "2026-10-06"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/apple-and-raspberry-smoothie.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '1 apple, chopped'
  - 100g raspberries
  - '2 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 1 tbsp lemon juice
  - ½ tsp lemon zest
  - 1 tbsp almond butter (optional)
  - 4 tbsp oats
  - 200ml plant milk
directions:
  - Place all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
