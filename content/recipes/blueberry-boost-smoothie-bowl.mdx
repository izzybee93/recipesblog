title: Blueberry boost smoothie bowl
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.264Z'
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: /images/recipes/blueberry-boost-smoothie-bowl.jpeg
servings: serves 1
ingredients:
  - 75g blueberries
  - '1 kiwi, sliced'
  - 15g spinach
  - 1 tsp lime juice
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water'
  - ½ tbsp almond butter
  - 2 tbsp oats
  - 50ml plant milk
  - 1 tbsp granola
  - 1 tbsp hemp seeds
  - 1 tbsp pomegranate seeds
directions:
  - Set aside 1 tbsp of blueberries and a few kiwi slices for the topping.
  - >-
    Blend the remaining blueberries and kiwi with the spinach, lime juice, chia seeds, almond butter, oats and plant milk
    until smooth. Transfer to a bowl and top with the remaining kiwi slices, blueberries,
    granola, hemp seeds and pomegranate seeds.
