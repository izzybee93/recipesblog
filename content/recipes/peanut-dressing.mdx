ingredients:

  - 2 tsp maple syrup or honey
  - 2-4 tbsp plant milk
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - 'Small handful coriander, finely chopped'
  - Pinch chilli flakes (optional)
  - '1 tsp fresh ginger, minced (optional)'
