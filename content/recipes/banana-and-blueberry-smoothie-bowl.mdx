title: Banana and blueberry smoothie bowl
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.253Z'
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: /images/recipes/banana-and-blueberry-smoothie-bowl.jpeg
servings: serves 1
ingredients:
  - '1 banana, sliced'
  - 75g blueberries
  - 1 tbsp almond butter
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 2 tbsp oats
  - 50ml plant milk
  - ¼ tsp cinnamon
  - 1 tbsp granola
  - 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds
directions:
  - >-
    Blend ½ of the banana, ¾ of the blueberries, almond butter, chia seeds, oats, cinnamon and plant milk
    until smooth. Transfer to a bowl and top with remaining banana, blueberries, granola and pumpkin seeds.
