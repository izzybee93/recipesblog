---
title: "Raspberry frosting"
date: "2025-11-18"
categories: ["treats"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/raspberry-frosting.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1 cake or 12 muffins"
ingredients:
  - 60g frozen raspberries
  - 75g plant butter
  - 140g icing sugar, sieved, sieved
  - ½ tsp vanilla extract
  - Pinch sea salt
directions:
  - In a small pan, add the raspberries and cook over medium-low heat for 5–10 minutes, stirring frequently, until slightly thickened. Strain over a bowl to remove the seeds.
  - In a medium bowl, beat the plant butter until creamy. Gradually add the icing sugar and beat until light and fluffy. Beat in the vanilla and salt.
  - Add the raspberry mixture, 1 tbsp at a time, and beat until smooth and fluffy.
---
