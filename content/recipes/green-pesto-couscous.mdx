date: "2026-10-13"

servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - ⅓ green pesto (see recipe)
  - ½ tbsp olive oil
  - 100g couscous
  - '150g cherry tomatoes, chopped'
  - '160g edamame, cooked'
  - Juice of ¼ lemon
  - 'Pinch sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '## Any veggie:'
  - '1 courgette, chopped'
  - '¼ broccoli, chopped into small florets'
  - '1 aubergine, chopped'
directions:
  - Make the green pesto.
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a baking tray. Add the veggies, brush with the olive oil and roast for 15–25 minutes, until softened and starting to brown.
  - In a large bowl, add the couscous, then pour in the vegetable stock until it is 0.5cm above the couscous. Set aside for 10 minutes.
  - Using a fork, loosen the couscous. Stir in pesto and mix well. Stir in the lemon juice and salt. Add the tomatoes, edamame and roasted veggies, and gently mix.
  - Serve topped with ground black pepper.
