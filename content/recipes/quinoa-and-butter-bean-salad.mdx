---
title: "Quinoa and butter bean salad"
date: "2025-08-17"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/quinoa-and-butter-bean-salad.jpeg"
servings: "serves 3"
ingredients:
  - 150g quinoa
  - 1 400g can butter beans
  - ½ cucumber, chopped
  - 200g cherry tomatoes, chopped
  - 120g sweetcorn
  - 12 sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
  - Small handful basil leaves, chopped
  - Balsamic dressing (see recipe)
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the quinoa.
  - Make the balsamic dressing.
  - Add the quinoa, butter beans, cucumber, tomatoes, sweetcorn and basil to a large bowl. Pour over the dressing, toss gently and serve chilled.
