---
title: "Lemon and herb couscous salad"
date: "2026-08-27"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/lemon-and-herb-couscous-salad.jpeg"
servings: "serves 3-4"
ingredients:
  - 120g giant couscous
  - '½ cucumber, chopped'
  - '300g cherry tomatoes, chopped'
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - '16 olives, chopped'
  - Zingy lemon dressing (see recipe)
  - '## Any 2 herbs:'
  - '2 tbsp parsley, finely chopped'
  - '1 tbsp dill, finely chopped'
  - '1 tbsp basil, finely chopped'
  - '1 tbsp mint leaves, finely chopped'
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the couscous.
  - 'In a large bowl, add the chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes, olives and couscous.'
  - 'Make the dressing. Pour over the salad and toss to coat. Add the herbs and toss again. Season to taste and serve as a main, or alongside falafel, hummus or roasted vegetables. Store in the fridge for up to 2 days.'
