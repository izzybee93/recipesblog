---
title: "Pasta with mushrooms and creamy green sauce"
date: "2026-08-02"
categories:
  - mains
  - pasta
featured_image: "/images/recipes/pasta-with-mushrooms-and-creamy-green-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 160g pasta
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '300g mushrooms, sliced'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - Creamy green sauce (see recipe)
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - Cook the pasta, reserving some pasta water.
  - In a large pan, heat the olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms and cook for 5 minutes, until lightly browned. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  - Make the creamy green sauce. Add to the pan with the mushrooms.
  - Add the pasta and toss to combine. Serve topped with ground black pepper.
