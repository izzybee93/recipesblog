---
title: "Butternut squash and black bean tacos"
date: "2026-02-27"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/butternut-squash-and-black-bean-tacos.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '250g butternut squash, cut into 2cm cubes'
  - '1 small onion, chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, finely chopped'
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ¼ tsp ground coriander
  - 120g black beans
  - 150ml vegetable stock
  - 'Small handful fresh coriander, chopped'
  - 4-6 taco wraps, warmed
  - '## Sauce'
  - 1 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted
  - 20g pumpkin seeds
  - ¼ tsp smoked paprika
  - 2-3 tsp harissa paste (see recipe)
  - Juice of ½ lime
  - ½ tbsp maple syrup
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
directions:
  - 'Heat the olive oil in a large pan. Add the butternut squash and cook over medium heat for 5-8 minutes, until browned and lightly charred. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes. Stir in the garlic, cumin and ground coriander and cook for 2 more minutes.'
  - 'Add the beans and vegetable stock. Bring to a simmer, cover with a lid and cook for 10 minutes, until the squash has softened and the liquid has reduced. Stir in the fresh coriander.'
  - 'Add all the sauce ingredients to a food processor and process until almost smooth.'
  - 'Assemble the tacos with the veggies and top with the harissa sauce.'
---
