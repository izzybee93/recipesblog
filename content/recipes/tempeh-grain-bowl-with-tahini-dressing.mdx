---
title: "Tempeh grain bowl with tahini dressing"
date: "2026-01-20"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/tempeh-grain-bowl-with-tahini-dressing.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g tempeh, sliced
  - 100g quinoa or rice
  - 120g edamame, cooked
  - ½ cucumber, chopped
  - Tahini dressing (see recipe)
  - '## Any 1–2 veggies:'
  - '2 small beetroot, chopped and cooked'
  - '1 carrot, finely sliced'
  - 150g cherry tomatoes, chopped
  - '½ aubergine, chopped and roasted'
  - '4 radishes, finely sliced'
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the quinoa.
  - Heat a pan over medium heat. Add the tempeh and cook for 5–10 minutes, turning to brown each side.
  - Make the tahini dressing.
  - Assemble the bowls with quinoa, edamame, tempeh and veggies. Drizzle with tahini dressing and serve.
