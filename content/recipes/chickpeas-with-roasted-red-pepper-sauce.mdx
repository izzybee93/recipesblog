---
title: "Chickpeas with roasted red pepper sauce"
date: "2026-10-06"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/chickpeas-with-roasted-red-pepper-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 3"
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 'Small handful fresh parsley, chopped'
  - '## Sauce'
  - '1 large roasted red pepper, roughly chopped'
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - 2 tsp soy sauce
  - 1 garlic clove
  - ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - ¼ tsp smoked paprika
  - ¼ tsp dried oregano
  - Pinch chilli flakes
  - 1–2 tbsp water
directions:
  - Add all the sauce ingredients to a blender and blend until smooth. Add water, 1 tbsp at a time, as needed until the sauce reaches the desired consistency.
  - In a medium bowl, add the chickpeas and pour over the sauce. Mix well, then stir in the parsley.
  - Serve with a refreshing salad, grain bowl or wrap, spoon over roasted aubergine. Optionally, serve with roasted red pepper chickpea salad bowl (see recipe).
