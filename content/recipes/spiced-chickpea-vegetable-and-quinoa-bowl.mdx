---
title: "Spiced chickpea, vegetable and quinoa bowl"
date: "2025-09-20"
categories: ["mains"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/spiced-chickpea-vegetable-and-quinoa-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g quinoa
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  - ½ tsp smoked paprika
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ¼ tsp chilli powder
  - ¼ tsp ground coriander
  - '## Any 2 veggies:'
  - 1 pepper, chopped
  - 1 courgette, chopped
  - ½ aubergine, chopped
  - 150g mushrooms, sliced
  - '## Any additional veggie:'
  - 6 sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
  - 10 olives, sliced
  - 80g sweetcorn
  - '## Dressing'
  - 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - ¼ tsp sea salt, to taste
  - Small handful coriander or parsley, finely chopped
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the quinoa, then transfer to a bowl.
  - Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pan. Cook the veggies for 10–15 minutes on a medium heat, until slightly browned. Stir in the additional veggie and cook for 2 more minutes.
  - Heat a separate pan over medium heat. Add the chickpeas and cook for a few minutes. Add the spices, toss until coated. Cook for 10 minutes, until slightly crispy.
  - Mix together the dressing ingredients in a small bowl. Pour half over the quinoa and mix to combine.
  - Divide the quinoa between the serving bowls. Add the vegetables and chickpeas, then serve with a drizzle of the remaining dressing.
