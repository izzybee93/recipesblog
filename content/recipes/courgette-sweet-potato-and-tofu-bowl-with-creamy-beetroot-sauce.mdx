featured_image: "/images/recipes/courgette-sweet-potato-and-tofu-bowl-with-creamy-beetroot-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '225g extra firm tofu, sliced'
  - '1 sweet potato, chopped'
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 large courgette, sliced'
  - Creamy beetroot sauce (see recipe)
  - 'Small handful fresh coriander, chopped'
  - '1 tbsp pine nuts or flaked almonds, toasted'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - Make the creamy beetroot sauce.
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a baking tray. Add the sweet potato, brush with 1 tbsp olive oil and roast for 25 minutes.
  - Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a grill pan over medium-high heat. Add the tofu and courgette and grill until lightly browned on each side.
  - Spoon the sauce into serving bowls. Top with the veggies and tofu. Sprinkle with the toasted nuts and coriander.
