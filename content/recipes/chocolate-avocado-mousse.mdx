---
title: Chocolate avocado mousse
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.274Z'
categories:
  - desserts
  - treats
featured_image: /images/recipes/chocolate-avocado-mousse.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 1 ripe avocado
  - 12g cocoa powder
  - 20g dark chocolate, melted
  - ½ tsp vanilla extract
  - 20g maple syrup
  - 3 tbsp plant milk
  - Pinch sea salt
  - 2 tsp cacao nibs
directions:
  - >-
    Add all ingredients except the cacao nibs to a food processor and process until 
    smooth. Spoon into serving glasses and refrigerate for at least 3 hours before serving.
  - Serve topped with a sprinkle of cacao nibs.
