---
title: "Sweet potato and edamame salad bowl with tahini dressing"
date: "2026-01-09"
categories: ["mains", "salad"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/sweet-potato-and-edamame-salad-bowl-with-tahini-dressing.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 2 sweet potatoes, chopped
  - ½ tbsp olive oil
  - ½ tsp smoked paprika
  - 120g edamame, cooked
  - ½ small cucumber, chopped
  - Tahini dressing (see recipe)
  - '## Any 1–2 veggies:'
  - '½ aubergine, chopped and roasted'
  - '½ red pepper, sliced and roasted'
  - '60g pickled red cabbage'
  - '1 avocado, chopped'
  - '120g cherry tomatoes, chopped'
  - '4 radishes, finely sliced'
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C and prepare a large baking tray. Add the sweet potato and brush with the oil and smoked paprika. Roast for 20–25 minutes, until softened.
  - Assemble the bowls with the sweet potato, edamame, and veggies. Drizzle with tahini dressing and serve.
