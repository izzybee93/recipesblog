featured_image: /images/recipes/sweet-potato-and-chickpea-grain-bowl-with-tahini-sauce.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - '100g rice or quinoa, rinsed'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 large sweet potato, sliced'
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 1 tsp paprika
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ½ tsp turmeric
  - Pinch chilli flakes
  - 50g mixed salad leaves
  - '1 avocado, sliced'
  - Tahini sauce (see recipe)
  - Small handful of fresh coriander or parsley, chopped
directions:
  - Cook the rice or quinoa.
  - >-
    Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the sweet potato, toss
    to coat in the oil, and cook for 2 minutes until lightly browned. Add ~100ml water, cover and cook for 10 minutes,
    until softened. Add more water as needed. Remove the lid and simmer until the water has evaporated.
  - >-
    Heat a pan over medium heat. Add the chickpeas and cook on a medium heat for a
    5 minutes. Add the spices and toss to coat. Cook for 10 minutes, until slightly crunchy.
  - >-
    Assemble the bowls with the quinoa or rice, then add the sweet potato, chickpeas, salad leaves
    and avocado. Serve with a drizzle of tahini sauce and top with fresh herbs.
