---
title: "Chickpeas with miso, mango and lime sauce"
date: "2026-07-31"
categories:
  - salad
  - snacks
featured_image: "/images/recipes/chickpeas-with-miso-mango-and-lime-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 'Small handful coriander, chopped'
  - '## Sauce'
  - '200g mango, chopped'
  - Juice of 1 lime
  - ½ tsp lime zest
  - 2 tsp miso paste
  - 2 tsp soy sauce
  - 1 tsp maple syrup
  - 1 tsp sesame oil
  - Pinch chilli flakes
  - '1 tsp ginger, grated'
  - 2 tbsp water
directions:
  - Add all the sauce ingredients to a blender and blend until smooth. Add water, 1 tbsp at a time, as needed until the sauce reaches the desired consistency.
  - In a medium bowl, add the chickpeas and pour over the sauce. Mix well, then stir in the coriander.
  - Serve in a refreshing salad, quinoa bowl or wrap with veggies such as cucumber, rocket, edamame and avocado.
