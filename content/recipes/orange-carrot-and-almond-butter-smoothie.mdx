---
title: "Orange, carrot and almond butter smoothie"
date: "2026-06-29"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/orange-carrot-and-almond-butter-smoothie.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '1 medium carrot, grated (~60g)'
  - '1 orange, peeled'
  - 1 tbsp almond butter
  - '2 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - ½ tsp orange zest
  - ½ tsp cinnamon
  - ½ tsp vanilla extract
  - 4 tbsp oats
  - 200ml plant milk
directions:
  - Place all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
