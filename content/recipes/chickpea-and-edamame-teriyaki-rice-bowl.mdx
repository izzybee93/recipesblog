title: Chickpea and edamame teriyaki rice bowl

categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/chickpea-and-edamame-teriyaki-rice-bowl.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - '100g rice, rinsed'
  - 1 tbsp sesame oil
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 1 tsp paprika
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ½ tsp chilli powder
  - ½ tsp turmeric
  - '100g edamame, cooked'
  - '2 medium carrots, finely sliced'
  - '1 avocado, sliced'
  - 4-6 tbsp teriyaki sauce
directions:
  - 'Cook the rice.'
  - >-
    Heat a pan over medium heat. Add the chickpeas and cook on a medium heat for a
    few minutes. Add the spices and toss until coated. Cook for 10 minutes, until slightly crunchy.
  - >-
    Divide the rice between 2 bowls, and top with chickpeas, edamame, carrot and
    avocado. Drizzle with teriyaki sauce.
