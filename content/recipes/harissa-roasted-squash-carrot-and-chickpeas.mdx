title: 'Harissa roasted squash, carrot and chickpeas'

  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/harissa-roasted-squash-carrot-and-chickpeas.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - '200g carrots, peeled, and sliced into strips'
  - '½ butternut squash, deseeded and sliced'
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - ½ tbsp harissa paste
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 25g pumpkin seeds
  - 'Handful fresh parsley, chopped'
  - Tahini sauce (optional, see recipe)
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Add the squash and carrots,
    drizzle with olive oil and harissa paste and mix until coated. Roast for 25 minutes.
  - >-
    Remove the tray from the oven and add the chickpeas and pumpkin seeds. Roast for another 10-15 minutes, until the
    vegetables have softened. 
  - Make the tahini sauce.
  - Serve with the tahini sauce a sprinkle of parsley.
