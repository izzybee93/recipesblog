title: Chickpea and hummus pittas

featured_image: /images/recipes/chickpea-and-hummus-pittas.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '½ red onion, chopped'
  - '1 pepper, chopped'
  - 1 400g can chickpeas
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - 'Pinch sea salt, to taste'
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - Small handful coriander, chopped
  - 2 wholemeal pittas, warmed
  - 50g rocket
  - '## Any hummus:'
  - 4 tbsp hummus (see recipe)
  - 4 tbsp sun-dried tomato hummus (see recipe)
  - 4 tbsp lemon and coriander hummus (see recipe)
directions:
  - >-
    Heat the olive oil in a pan over a medium heat. Add the onion, and cook
    for 5 minutes, until softened. Add the pepper amnd cook for 5 more minutes. 
  - >-
    Stir in chickpeas, paprika and salt. Cook for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
    Remove from the heat and stir in the lemon juice and coriander.
  - >-
     Spread the hummus in each pitta. Fill with rocket and the chickpea mixture.
