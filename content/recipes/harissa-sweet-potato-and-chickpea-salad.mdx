servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - '2 sweet potatoes, chopped'
  - ½ tbsp olive oil
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - 1 tsp cumin
  - 80g rocket
  - Harissa dressing (see recipe)
  - '2 tbsp flaked almonds, toasted'
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a baking tray. Add the sweet potato and brush with the
    olive oil. Add the chickpeas, smoked paprika and cumin, and mix until coated. Roast for 20–25 minutes.
  - Make the harissa dressing.
  - >-
    In a large bowl, combine the rocket, sweet potato and chickpeas. Pour over the dressing and gently mix together.
  - Serve topped with the toasted almonds.
