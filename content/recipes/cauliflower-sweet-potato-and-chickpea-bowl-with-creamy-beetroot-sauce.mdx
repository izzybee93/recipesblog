servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '½ small cauliflower, chopped'
  - '1 large sweet potato, chopped'
  - 120g chickpeas
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - Creamy beetroot sauce (see recipe)
  - '2 tsp fresh chives, finely chopped'
  - 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - Make the creamy beetroot sauce to serve chilled.
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a large baking tray.
  - Place the sweet potato, cauliflower and chickpeas on a large tray and brush with olive oil. Mix until coated. Bake for 25 minutes.
  - Spoon the sauce into serving bowls. Top with the roasted veggies. Garnish with chives, pumpkin seeds and ground black pepper.
