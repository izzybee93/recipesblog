servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '½ small cauliflower, chopped'
  - '1 large sweet potato, chopped'
  - 120g chickpeas
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - Creamy beetroot sauce (see recipe)
  - '2 tsp fresh chives, finely chopped'
  - 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds, toasted
directions:
  - Make the creamy beetroot sauce.
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Add the sweet potato, cauliflower and chickpeas, brush with the olive oil and roast for 25 minutes.
  - Spoon the sauce into serving bowls. Top with the roasted veggies. Sprinkle with toasted pumpkin seeds and chives.
