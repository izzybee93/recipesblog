---
title: "Peanut noodle stir fry"
date: "2026-05-05"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/peanut-noodle-stir-fry.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g rice noodles
  - 1 tbsp sesame oil
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '120g edamame, cooked'
  - '## Any 2-3 veggies:'
  - '120g broccoli, chopped'
  - '1 pepper, chopped'
  - '150g mushrooms, sliced'
  - '120g baby corn, chopped'
  - '## Sauce'
  - Peanut stir fry sauce (see recipe)
  - '## Garnish'
  - 2 tsp sesame seeds
directions:
  - Cook the rice noodles. Drain and rinse, then set aside.
  - 'Heat the sesame oil in a wok over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Add the veggies and cook for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally. Stir in the edamame.'
  - Make the peanut stir fry sauce. Add half the sauce to the pan and stir until heated. Add a few tbsp more water as needed.
  - Add the noodles to the pan and toss to combine. Add the remaining sauce and stir until coated and heated through.
  - Serve topped with sesame seeds.
