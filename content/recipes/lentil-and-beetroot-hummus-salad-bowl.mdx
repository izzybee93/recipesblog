servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can green lentils, drained and rinsed'
  - '2 carrots, grated'
  - '¼ cucumber, chopped'
  - '4 radishes, sliced'
  - 4 tbsp beetroot hummus (see recipe)
  - '## Dressing'
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 'Small handful fresh dill, chopped'
  - 'Pinch sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - Whisk together the dressing ingredients in a small jug.
  - 'To assemble the bowls, add the lentils, carrot, cucumber and radish. Pour over the dressing and top with spoonfuls of hummus.'
