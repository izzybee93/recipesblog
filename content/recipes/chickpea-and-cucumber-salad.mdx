---
title: 'Chickpea and cucumber salad'
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.291Z'
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: /images/recipes/chickpea-and-cucumber-salad.jpeg
servings: serves 3
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - '½ cucumber, chopped'
  - '200g cherry tomatoes, chopped'
  - 'Large handful fresh parsley, chopped'
  - 'Small handful fresh mint, chopped'
  - '## Dressing'
  - Juice of 1 lemon
  - Zest of ½ lemon
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - ¼ tsp cumin (optional)
  - '½ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - >-
    In a large bowl, combine the chickpeas, cucumber, tomato, parsley
    and mint.
  - >-
    Whisk together the dressing ingredients in a small jug. Pour over the salad and gently toss together.
---
