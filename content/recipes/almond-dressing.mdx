featured_image: "/images/recipes/almond-dressing.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp almond butter
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  - Juice of ½ lime
  - 2 tsp maple syrup or honey
  - '1 tsp fresh ginger, minced (optional)'
directions:
  - Whisk all ingredients together in a small measuring jug. Serve with a rainbow salad.
