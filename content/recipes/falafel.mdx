---
title: Falafel
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.253Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/falafel.jpeg
servings: makes 12
ingredients:
  - '1½ 400g cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - '½ onion, chopped'
  - 1 garlic clove
  - Large handful fresh coriander
  - Small handful fresh parsley
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - Zest of ½ lemom
  - ½ tbsp plain flour
  - ¼ tsp baking powder
  - 1 tsp cumin
  - ½ tsp ground coriander
  - Pinch chilli flakes (optional)
  - ½ tsp sea salt
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Line a large baking tray with baking paper.
  - >-
    In a food processor, combine all ingredients except the olive oil and pulse until finely chopped. Transfer to the fridge for 30 minutes.
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C and prepare a large baking tray with parchment paper.
  - 'Take 1 tbsp of falafel mixture at a time and form small patties. Brush with oil and place on the tray. Bake for 25 minutes, flipping halfway.'
  - 'Serve with salad, hummus, pitta and tahini sauce. Store in the fridge for up to 3 days or freeze for up to 3 months.'
