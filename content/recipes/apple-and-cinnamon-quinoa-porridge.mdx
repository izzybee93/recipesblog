---
title: "Apple and cinnamon quinoa porridge"
date: "2026-07-29"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/apple-and-cinnamon-quinoa-porridge.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - 50g white quinoa
  - 100ml plant milk
  - 1 apple
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - ½ tsp cinnamon
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the quinoa. Transfer to the fridge if cooking in advance.
  - Halve the apple. Cut one half into thin slices and set aside. Chop or grate the remaining half.
  - Add the quinoa, milk, chia seeds and apple to a pan and cook for 5 minutes on a medium heat, stirring frequently. Stir in the cinnamon.
  - Serve topped with apple slices and an extra sprinkle of cinnamon.
