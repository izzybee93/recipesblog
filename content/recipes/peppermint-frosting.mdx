title: Peppermint frosting
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.298Z'
categories:
  - treats
featured_image: /images/recipes/peppermint-frosting.jpeg
servings: serves 1 cake or 12 muffins
ingredients:
  - 75g plant butter
  - 140g icing sugar, sieved, sieved
  - Pinch sea salt
  - 1 tsp peppermint extract
  - 1–2 drops green food colouring
directions:
  - 'In a medium bowl, beat the plant butter until creamy.'
  - >-
    Gradually add the icing sugar and beat until light and fluffy. Beat in the
    salt, peppermint extract and food colouring until smooth and fluffy.
