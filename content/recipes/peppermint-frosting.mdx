---
title: Peppermint frosting
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.298Z'
categories:
  - treats
featured_image: /images/recipes/peppermint-frosting.jpeg
servings: serves 1 cake or 12 muffins
ingredients:
  - 100g plant butter
  - '220g icing sugar, sieved'
  - Pinch of sea salt
  - 1 tsp peppermint extract
  - 1-2 drops green food colouring
directions:
  - 'Using a mixer, beat the butter until light and fluffy.'
  - >-
    Gradually beat in the sugar. Add the salt, peppermint extract and food colouring.
    Blend until whipped and creamy.
  - >-
    Spread or pipe on top of the cake, and add your favourite mint chocolate
    toppings to decorate.
