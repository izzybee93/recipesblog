title: Butternut squash and chickpea curry
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.265Z'

  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/butternut-squash-and-chickpea-curry.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 100g rice or quinoa
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '½ onion, finely chopped'
  - '1 tsp fresh ginger, minced'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '15g fresh coriander, stalks and leaves separated'
  - 1 tsp curry powder
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ½ tsp turmeric
  - ½ tsp ground coriander
  - ¼ tsp chilli flakes
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 1 tbsp tomato paste
  - 250ml vegetable stock
  - ½ 400g can chopped tomatoes
  - '¼ large butternut squash, cubed'
  - '120g chickpeas'
  - '50g soya chunks (optional)'
  - 80g fresh spinach
  - 100g silken tofu cream (see recipe)
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the rice or quinoa.
  - 'In a small bowl, add the soya chunks and enough boiling water or vegetable stock to just cover them. Leave to sit for 5-10 minutes.'
  - 'Heat the oil in a large pan. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, until softened. Add the soya chunks and cook for a few more minutes, until slightly browned.'
  - 'Finely chop the coriander stalks. Add the stalks to the pan and cook for 2-3 minutes. Stir in the garlic, ginger, chilli powder, turmeric, cumin, ground coriander, curry powder, salt and tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes. Pour in the vegetable stock.'
  - 'Add the butternut squash and chickpeas. Toss until coated, then simmer for 25-30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the curry is reduced by a third and the butternut squash is cooked.'
  - 'Make the "bright and fresh" flavour silken tofu cream.'
  - Add the spinach and stir through until wilted. Reduce to a low heat and stir in the cream and coriander.
  - Serve with the rice or quinoa.
