categories:

  - pasta
featured_image: "/images/recipes/creamy-green-pasta-with-tomato-and-crispy-balsamic-tofu.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 160g pasta
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - Creamy green sauce (see recipe)
  - 300g cherry tomatoes
  - ½ crispy balsamic tofu bites (see recipe)
directions:
  - Make the crispy balsamic tofu bites.
  - Cook the pasta, reserving some pasta water.
  - Make the creamy green sauce.
  - Add the pasta to the sauce and toss to combine. Stir in the tomatoes until heated through. Serve topped with the crispy tofu.
