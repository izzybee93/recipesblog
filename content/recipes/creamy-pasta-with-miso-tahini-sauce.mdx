title: Creamy pasta with miso tahini sauce

categories:

featured_image: /images/recipes/creamy-pasta-with-miso-tahini-sauce.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 180g dry pasta
  - '## Any 1-2 veggies:'
  - 1 large courgette, chopped
  - 250g mushrooms, chopped
  - '½ broccoli, chopped and roasted'
  - '## Any 1-2 additional veggies:'
  - 150g peas, cooked
  - 60g spinach
  - '## Sauce'
  - 2 tbsp tahini
  - 1 tsp miso paste
  - 1 garlic clove
  - ¼ tsp sea salt, to taste
  - 4 tbsp nutritional yeast
  - 150ml water
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - >-
    Cook the pasta, reserving a few tbsp of pasta water.
  - >-
    Heat the olive oil in a large pan. Add the raw veggies and cook on a medium heat for 5-10 minutes, until softened and lightly browned.
  - >-
    To make the sauce, add all the ingredients except the lemon juice to a blender and blend until smooth. 
    Add to the pan over medium-low heat, stirring frequently until slightly thickened. Add a few tbsp 
    of pasta water as needed. Stir in the lemon juice.
  - >-
    Add the pasta and additional veggies, and stir until combined and heated through. Stir in the additional veggies. Serve topped with ground black pepper.
