---
title: "Mashed cannellini beans and mushrooms on toast"
date: "2026-09-01"
categories:
  - breakfast
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/mashed-cannellini-beans-and-mushrooms-on-toast.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '250g mushrooms, sliced'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '180g cannellini beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 1 tbsp lemon juice
  - 1 tbsp nutritional yeast
  - ½ tsp Dijon mustard
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '2 slices sourdough, toasted'
directions:
  - 'In a large pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the mushrooms and cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly browned.'
  - 'In a medium bowl, add the cannellini beans, olive oil and lemon juice. Mash together until creamy but slightly chunky.'
  - 'Add the nutritional yeast, mustard, garlic powder, salt and ground black pepper. Mix well, adding 1–2 tsp water if needed to reach a spreadable consistency.'
  - Serve the mashed beans on the toasted sourdough and top with mushrooms and ground black pepper.
