---
title: Roasted butternut squash pasta
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.301Z'
categories:
  - mains
  - pasta
featured_image: /images/recipes/roasted-butternut-squash-pasta.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - '½ butternut squash, cubed'
  - '½ onion, roughly chopped'
  - 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  - ¼ tsp nutmeg
  - '½ broccoli, chopped'
  - 140g pasta
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - 100ml vegetable stock
  - 50ml soya milk
  - 1 tsp dried sage
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Line 2 baking trays with parchment paper.
  - >-
    Place the butternut squash and onion on a tray and coat with 1 tbsp olive
    oil. Sprinkle over the nutmeg. Roast for 25-30 minutes, until tender.
  - >- 
    Place the broccoli on the other tray and brush with ½ tbsp olive oil. Roast for 10-15 minutes. 
  - Cook the pasta.
  - >-
    Transfer the roasted squash and onion to a large bowl. Add the vegetable
    stock and soya milk, then blend until smooth.
  - >-
    In a large pan, heat the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Add the garlic and sage and cook for 1 minute.
    Add the blended sauce, salt and ground black pepper. Bring to a simmer, stirring occasionally, until heated through. Stir in
    the pasta and broccoli. Serve topped with ground black pepper.
