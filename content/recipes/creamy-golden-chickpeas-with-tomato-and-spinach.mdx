---
title: "Creamy golden chickpeas with tomato and spinach"
date: "2026-10-17"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/creamy-golden-chickpeas-with-tomato-and-spinach.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 200g cherry tomatoes
  - '½ onion, chopped'
  - '1 carrot, chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '½ thumb-sized piece of fresh ginger, minced'
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ½ tsp turmeric
  - ¼ tsp cinnamon
  - Pinch nutmeg
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - '400g cooked chickpeas, with their stock'
  - 150ml vegetable stock
  - 60g spinach
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - 'Small handful fresh basil leaves, chopped, plus extra to serve'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '## Sauce'
  - 150g silken tofu
  - 4 tbsp nutritional yeast
  - 2 tsp miso paste
  - 200ml soya milk
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Add the tomatoes and brush with ½ tbsp olive oil. Roast for 20 minutes.
  - Add all the sauce ingredients to a blender and blend until smooth and creamy. Set aside.
  - In a large pan, heat the remaining ½ tbsp olive oil over a medium heat. Add the onion and carrot and cook for 5 minutes, until starting to soften. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 1 minute. Stir in the spices and cook for 2 more minutes.
  - Stir in the sauce and cook for 2 minutes. Add the chickpeas and vegetable stock, then simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until heated through and slightly thickened. Stir in the spinach and cook over low heat until wilted.
  - Remove from the heat and stir in the lemon juice and ¾ of the basil.
  - Divide between bowls and top with the roasted tomatoes, remaining basil and ground black pepper. Serve with a side of crusty bread, pitta or quinoa.
