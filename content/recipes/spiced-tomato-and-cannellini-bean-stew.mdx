---
title: "Spiced tomato and cannellini bean stew"
date: "2026-03-24"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/spiced-tomato-and-cannellini-bean-stew.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '½ onion, finely chopped'
  - '1 pepper, chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, finely chopped'
  - 1 tsp cumin
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - ¼ tsp chilli powder
  - 1 tbsp tomato purée
  - 1 400g can chopped tomatoes
  - 200ml vegetable stock
  - '1 400g can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 100g spinach
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '## Tofu cream'
  - 100g silken tofu
  - 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
  - ¼ tsp sea salt
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - ¼ tsp ground turmeric
  - ½ tsp Dijon mustard
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - 50ml water
directions:
  - To make the tofu cream, add all the ingredients to a blender and blend until smooth. Set aside.
  - 'In a large pan, heat the olive oil over a medium heat. Add the onion and pepper and cook for 5-7 minutes, until softened. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.'
  - 'Add the cumin, smoked paprika, chilli powder and tomato purée and cook for 1 minute.'
  - 'Add the chopped tomatoes, vegetable stock and cannellini beans and bring to the boil. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened.'
  - Add the spinach and stir through until wilted. Season with black pepper to taste.
  - Serve with crusty bread and top with a drizzle of tofu cream.
