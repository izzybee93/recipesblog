---
title: "Raspberry, cacao and hazelnut porridge"
date: "2026-08-23"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/raspberry-cacao-and-hazelnut-porridge.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - 50g oats
  - 250ml plant milk or water
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 75g raspberries
  - 1 tbsp hemp seeds (optional)
  - 1 tsp cacao nibs
  - '1 tsp hazelnuts, chopped'
  - 1 tsp hazelnut butter or dark chocolate hazelnut spread
directions:
  - 'In a small pan, add the oats, plant milk and chia seeds. Cook for a few minutes over medium heat, stirring frequently. Stir in ⅔ of the raspberries.'
  - 'Serve topped with the remaining raspberries, hemp seeds, cacao nibs, hazelnuts and a drizzle of hazelnut butter or dark chocolate hazelnut spread.'
---
