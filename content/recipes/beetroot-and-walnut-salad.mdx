---
title: "Beetroot and walnut salad"
date: "2026-10-05"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/beetroot-and-walnut-salad.jpeg"
servings: "serves 3"
ingredients:
  - '2 medium cooked beetroot, chopped'
  - '2 celery sticks, sliced'
  - '1 small apple, sliced into matchsticks'
  - '1 x 400g can cannellini beans or chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 80g mixed salad leaves
  - 'Small handful fresh parsley, chopped'
  - '25g walnuts, chopped and toasted'
  - '## Dressing'
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - ½ tsp Dijon mustard
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - 'In a large bowl, add the salad leaves, beetroot, celery, apple, legumes, parsley and walnuts.'
  - 'To make the dressing, whisk all the dressing ingredients together in a small jug.'
  - 'Add the dressing and gently toss to combine.'
