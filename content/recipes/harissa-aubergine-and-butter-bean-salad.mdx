---
title: "Harissa aubergine and butter bean salad"
date: "2026-08-27"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/harissa-aubergine-and-butter-bean-salad.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '1 aubergine, chopped'
  - '1 pepper, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 80g rocket or spinach
  - '1 400g can butter beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 'Small handful fresh coriander, chopped'
  - '2 tbsp flaked almonds, toasted'
  - Harissa dressing (see recipe)
directions:
  - 'Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a baking tray. Add the aubergine, brush with ½ tbsp olive oil and roast for 10 minutes. Add the pepper to the tray, brush with ½ tbsp olive oil and roast for 15 more minutes.'
  - 'In a large bowl, add the rocket or spinach, butter beans, coriander, aubergine and pepper.'
  - 'Make the dressing, pour over the salad and toss to combine. Serve topped with the toasted flaked almonds.'
