title: Harissa roasted vegetable couscous
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.288Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/harissa-roasted-vegetable-couscous.jpeg
servings: serves 2–3
ingredients:
  - 150g dry couscous
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - ~300ml vegetable stock
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - 3 tsp harissa paste
  - Small handful parsley, chopped
  - 2 tbsp flaked almonds, toasted
  - '## Any 2 veggies:'
  - '1 pepper, chopped'
  - '1 courgette, chopped'
  - '¼ broccoli, chopped'
  - '¼ cauliflower, chopped'
  - '¼ butternut squash, cubed'
  - ½ aubergine, chopped
  - '## Any additional veggie:'
  - '6 sun-dried tomatoes, chopped'
  - '15 olives, chopped'
  - '80g peas, cooked'
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Add the veggies
    and brush with 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tsp harissa paste. Add the chickpeas and mix together.
    Roast for 15–25 minutes, until softened and lightly browned.
  - >-
    In a large bowl, add the couscous, then pour in the vegetable stock until it
    is 0.5cm above the couscous. Set aside for 10 minutes.
  - >-
    Using a fork, loosen the couscous. Add the lemon juice and remaining 2 tsp harissa paste and mix well. Add
    the veggies, chickpeas and parsley and gently mix. Add a drizzle of olive oil and mix again. Serve topped with the
    toasted flaked almonds.
