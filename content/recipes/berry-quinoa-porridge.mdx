---
title: "Berry quinoa porridge"
date: "2026-07-29"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/berry-quinoa-porridge.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - '120g quinoa, cooked'
  - 100ml plant milk
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 75g mixed berries
  - ½ tsp cinnamon
  - 1 tsp almond butter (optional)
directions:
  - Add the quinoa, milk, chia seeds and ⅔ of the berries to a pan and cook for 5 minutes on a medium heat, stirring frequently until the juices start to release. Stir in the cinnamon.
  - Serve topped with the remaining berries and a drizzle of almond butter.
