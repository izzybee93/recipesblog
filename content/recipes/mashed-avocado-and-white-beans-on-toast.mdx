categories:
  - breakfast

featured_image: "/images/recipes/mashed-avocado-and-white-beans-on-toast.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 medium ripe avocado
  - '120g cannellini beans, drained and rinsed'
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - 1 tbsp nutritional yeast
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '2 slices sourdough, toasted'
  - '## Optional toppings'
  - '4 sun-dried tomatoes, chopped'
  - 2 tbsp pickled radishes (see recipe)
  - 2 tbsp hemp seeds
directions:
  - 'In a medium bowl, add the avocado and beans and mash together. Mix in the lemon juice, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, salt and pepper.'
  - 'Serve on toasted sourdough. Top with sun-dried tomatoes or pickled radishes, with a sprinkle of hemp seeds.'
