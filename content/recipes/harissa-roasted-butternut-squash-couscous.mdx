title: Harissa roasted butternut squash couscous
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.285Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/harissa-roasted-butternut-squash-couscous.jpeg
servings: serves 3
ingredients:
  - '½ butternut squash, cubed'
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 3 tsp harissa paste
  - 150g couscous
  - ~300ml vegetable stock
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - '3 tbsp flaked almonds, toasted'
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - '## Any veggie:'
  - 1 pepper, chopped
  - 150g broccolini, chopped
  - ¼ cauliflower, chopped into florets
  - ½ aubergine, chopped
  - '## Any additional veggie:'
  - '80g peas, cooked'
  - '8 sundried-tomatoes, chopped'
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 190°C. Prepare a large baking tray and add the butternut
    squash and veggies. Drizzle with 1 tbsp olive oil and 2 tsp harissa paste. Toss until
    evenly coated. Roast for 20 minutes, until almost softened.
  - >-
    In a large bowl, add the couscous then vegetable stock, pouring until the
    water is 0.5cm above the couscous. Set aside for 10 minutes.
  - >-
    Add the chickpeas to the roasted butternut squash and continue to roast for
    10 minutes.
  - >-
    Using a fork, loosen the couscous. Add the lemon juice, 1 tsp harissa paste
    and 1 tbsp olive oil, and mix well. Add the roasted veggies, additional veggie,
    chickpeas and toasted almonds, and gently mix together. Serve warm or cold.
