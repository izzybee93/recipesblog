featured_image: "/images/recipes/edamame-and-roasted-aubergine-grain-bowl-with-peanut-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g rice or quinoa
  - '150g edamame, cooked'
  - '1 medium aubergine, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '½ cucumber, diced'
  - 4 tbsp pickled radishes (see recipe)
  - Peanut sauce (see recipe)
  - 2 tsp sesame seeds
directions:
  - 'Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a baking tray. Add the aubergine, brush with the olive oil and roast for 25 minutes.'
  - Rinse and cook the rice or quinoa.
  - Make the peanut sauce.
  - 'Assemble the bowls with rice or quinoa and top with the edamame, aubergine, cucumber and radishes. Drizzle over the peanut sauce and top with sesame seeds.'
