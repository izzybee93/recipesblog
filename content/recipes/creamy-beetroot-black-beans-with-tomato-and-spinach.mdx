---
title: "Creamy beetroot black beans with tomato and spinach"
date: "2026-03-20"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/creamy-beetroot-black-beans-with-tomato-and-spinach.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 200g cherry tomatoes
  - '1 400g can black beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 200ml vegetable stock
  - 60g spinach
  - Juice of 1 lemon
  - 'Small handful fresh coriander, chopped'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '## Sauce'
  - '2 beetroot, cooked and chopped'
  - 1 garlic clove
  - 1 tbsp tahini
  - 100g silken tofu
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ¼ tsp sea salt
  - 3 tbsp water
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Add the beetroot, tomatoes and garlic, and brush with the olive oil. Roast for 20 minutes.
  - Add all the sauce ingredients and half of the lemon juice to a blender and blend until smooth and creamy. Set aside.
  - Add the sauce to a large pan over medium heat. Stir in the black beans and vegetable stock, then simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until heated through and slightly thickened. Stir in the spinach and cook over low heat until wilted.
  - Remove from the heat and stir in the remaining lemon juice and coriander.
  - Divide between bowls and top with the roasted tomatoes and ground black pepper. Serve with a side of crusty bread, quinoa or roasted sweet potato.
