---
title: "Crispy miso tofu"
date: "2026-08-19"
categories:
  - snacks
featured_image: "/images/recipes/crispy-miso-tofu.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2-4"
ingredients:
  - '280g extra-firm tofu, cubed'
  - 1 tbsp miso paste
  - 2 tsp maple syrup
  - 2 tsp soy sauce
  - 1 tsp rice vinegar
  - 1 tsp sesame oil
  - '½ tsp ginger, minced'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - 2 tsp water
  - 1 tbsp cornflour
  - 2 tsp sesame seeds
directions:
  - Place the tofu in a shallow dish.
  - 'In a small bowl, whisk together the miso paste, maple syrup, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, ginger, garlic and water. Pour over the tofu, cover and transfer to the fridge for 2-12 hours.'
  - 'Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Drain off any excess marinade, then toss the tofu with the cornflour until lightly coated. Add to the baking tray and bake for 30-35 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crispy. Alternatively, cook in an air fryer for 15 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crispy.'
  - 'In a small pan, heat any leftover marinade for a few minutes until slightly thickened.'
  - 'Serve the tofu with a drizzle of extra marinade and topped with sesame seeds, alongside salad, rice or roasted veggies.'
---
