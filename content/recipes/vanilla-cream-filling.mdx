---
title: "Vanilla cream filling"
date: "2026-02-26"
categories:
  - sauces
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/vanilla-cream-filling.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1 cake"
ingredients:
  - 150g silken tofu, drained
  - 1 tbsp maple syrup
  - 1 tsp lemon juice
  - 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
  - Pinch sea salt
directions:
  - 'Add all the ingredients to a blender and blend for 1 minute, scraping down the sides as needed.'
  - Chill for at least 2 hours in the fridge to firm up slightly.
---
