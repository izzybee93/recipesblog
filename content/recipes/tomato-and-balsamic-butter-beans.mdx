---
title: "Tomato and balsamic butter beans"
date: "2026-06-17"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/tomato-and-balsamic-butter-beans.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '½ onion, finely chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '200g cherry tomatoes, halved'
  - '6 sun-dried tomatoes, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp tomato paste
  - 1½ tbsp balsamic vinegar
  - '1 400g can butter beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 150ml vegetable stock
  - ¼ tsp sea salt, to taste
  - Ground black peper, to taste
  - 'Small handful parsley, chopped'
  - Tangy tofu cream (optional, see recipe) 
directions:
  - Make the tofu cream and set aside.
  - Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5-8 minutes, until softened. Add the garlic and cook for another minute. Add the tomatoes and cook for 2 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  - Stir in the sun-dried tomatoes, tomato paste, balsamic vinegar and salt and cook for 2 minutes. Stir in the butter beans and vegetable stock, then simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. Stir in the ground black pepper.
  - Serve the butter beans with toasted sourdough, and top with a drizzle of tofu cream and fresh parsley.
