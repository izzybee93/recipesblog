title: Caponata
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.268Z'
categories:

featured_image: /images/recipes/caponata.jpeg
servings: serves 6
ingredients:
  - 5 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 red onion, chopped'
  - '2 aubergines, cut into 2cm chunks'
  - '2 garlic cloves, minced'
  - '1 red pepper, chopped'
  - '1 yellow pepper, chopped'
  - '2 celery sticks, chopped'
  - 20 black olives
  - 2 tbsp capers
  - 1 tbsp pine nuts
  - 500g passata
  - ½ tbsp apple cider vinegar
  - 1 tbsp sugar
  - '1 tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 2 400g cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed (optional)
  - Ground black pepper, to taste
directions:
  - >-
    Heat the oil in a large saucepan. Add the onion and cook until softened. Add
    the garlic and cook for another minute.
  - "Add the aubergine, peppers and celery, and gently cook for about 10 minutes until softened."
  - >-
    Add the olives, passata, capers, pine nuts, vinegar, sugar and salt. Stir together and bring to a gentle simmer. Cover and cook over low heat for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  - Stir in the chickpeas and simmer until heated through.
  - Season with ground black pepper and serve with crusty bread or stirred through pasta.'
