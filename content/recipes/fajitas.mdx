title: Fajitas

categories:

featured_image: /images/recipes/fajitas.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 small red onion, sliced'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '1 red pepper, chopped'
  - '1 400g can black beans or kidney beans, drained and rinsed'
  - '50g soya chunks (optional)'
  - '2 tomatoes, chopped'
  - 1 tsp turmeric
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - ½ tsp chilli powder
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ¼ tsp sea salt, to taste
  - Juice of ½ lime
  - 'Handful fresh coriander, chopped'
  - '4 tortilla wraps, warmed'
directions:
  - 'In a small bowl, add the soya chunks and enough boiling water or vegetable stock to just cover them. Leave to sit for 5-10 minutes.'
  - >-
    In a large pan, heat the oil. Add the onion and cook for 5
    minutes, until softened. Add the the garlic, red pepper and soya chunks. Cook for 5 minutes, until lightly browned. 
    Stir in the spices and cook for another minute.
  - >-
    Add the beans and tomatoes, and cook on a medium-low heat for 5-10 minutes. Add a few tbsp water as needed.
  - Remove from the heat and stir in the lime juice and fresh coriander.
  - >-
    Assemble the fajitas in the tortilla wraps, and serve with guacamole and salsa.
