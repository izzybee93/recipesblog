---
title: "Mashed cannellini beans and pesto on toast"
date: "2026-09-01"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/mashed-cannellini-beans-and-pesto-on-toast.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 2 tbsp basil pesto (see recipe)
  - '180g cannellini beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 1 tbsp lemon juice
  - 1–2 tbsp water
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '2 slices sourdough, toasted'
  - '200g cherry tomatoes, chopped'
directions:
  - Make the basil pesto.
  - 'In a medium bowl, add the cannellini beans and lemon juice. Mash together until almost smooth.'
  - 'Add the pesto and mix until spreadable. Add 1 tbsp water at a time as needed. Season with ground black pepper.'
  - Serve on the toasted sourdough and top with the cherry tomatoes and ground black pepper.
