---
title: "Sweet potato and lentil shepherd's pie"
date: "2026-10-05"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/sweet-potato-and-lentil-shepherds-pie.jpeg"
servings: "serves 4"
ingredients:
  - '600g sweet potato, chopped'
  - 2–3 tbsp plant milk
  - 50g soya mince
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 large onion, finely chopped'
  - '2 garlic cloves, minced'
  - '150g chestnut mushrooms, chopped'
  - '1 400g can green or brown lentils, drained and rinsed'
  - 1 tbsp tomato paste
  - 1 tsp dried thyme
  - ½ tsp dried rosemary
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - 250ml vegetable stock
  - 1 400g can chopped tomatoes
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - Boil the sweet potato for 15–20 minutes, until tender. Drain, then mash with the plant milk and a pinch of salt. Set aside.
  - In a small bowl, add the soya mince and enough vegetable stock to just cover it. Leave to sit for 5–10 minutes.
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C.
  - Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5–8 minutes, until softened. Add the garlic and mushrooms and cook for 5 minutes, until softened and starting to brown.
  - Stir in the tomato paste, thyme and rosemary and cook for 1 minute.
  - Add the soya mince, lentils, chopped tomatoes, soy sauce and vegetable stock. Season with salt and black pepper, and simmer for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened.
  - Spoon the filling into a baking dish and spread the sweet potato mash evenly over the top. Bake for 15 minutes, then grill for 5 minutes until lightly browned and crispy on top.
