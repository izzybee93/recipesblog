---
title: "Creamy green sauce"
date: "2026-07-26"
categories:
  - sauces
featured_image: "/images/recipes/creamy-green-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 80g rocket, spinach or kale
  - 100g silken tofu
  - 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 20g fresh basil
  - 1 tsp lemon zest
  - 2 tbsp hemp seeds
  - 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
  - 1 tsp miso paste
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - Juice of ½ lemon
directions:
  - Pour boiling water over the greens until wilted. Add all the ingredients except the lemon juice to a blender. Blend until smooth. Add 1–2 tbsp water as needed.
  - Add the sauce to a pan and stir over medium-low heat until smooth and slightly thickened. Add a few tbsp water (or pasta water) as needed. Stir in the lemon juice.
---
