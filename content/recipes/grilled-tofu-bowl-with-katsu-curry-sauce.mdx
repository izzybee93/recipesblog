title: "Grilled tofu bowl with katsu curry sauce"
date: "2026-10-13"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/grilled-tofu-bowl-with-katsu-curry-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - Katsu curry sauce (see recipe)
  - '225g extra firm tofu, sliced'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '## Any roasted veggies:'
  - '¼ butternut squash, chopped'
  - '1 sweet potato, chopped'
  - '## Any grilled veggies:'
  - '1 courgette, sliced'
  - '1 aubergine, sliced'
  - '## Any toppings:'
  - 'Small handful coriander, chopped'
  - '1 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted'
  - 1 tbsp crispy onion
directions:
  - Prepare the katsu curry sauce.
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a baking tray. Add the roasted veggies, brush with ½ tbsp olive oil and roast for 20–30 minutes, until softened and starting to brown.
  - Heat ½ tbsp olive oil in a grill pan over medium-high heat. Add the tofu and grilled veggies, and grill until lightly browned on each side.
  - Gently reheat the sauce for 5 minutes. Spoon the sauce into serving bowls. Top with the veggies and tofu. Sprinkle with coriander, toasted sesame seeds and crispy onion.
