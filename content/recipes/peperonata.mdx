---
title: "Peperonata"
date: "2026-08-28"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/peperonata.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2–3"
ingredients:
  - 1½ tbsp olive oil
  - '½ red onion, sliced'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '1 red pepper, sliced'
  - '1 yellow pepper, sliced'
  - '1 orange pepper, sliced'
  - 250g passata
  - ½ tbsp apple cider vinegar
  - ½ tsp sugar (optional)
  - '½ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed (optional)
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - 'Small handful fresh basil, chopped'
directions:
  - 'Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, until softened. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.'
  - 'Add the peppers and cook for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften.'
  - 'Add the passata, vinegar, sugar and salt. Stir together and bring to a gentle simmer. Cover and cook over low heat for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally.'
  - Stir in the chickpeas and simmer until heated through.
  - 'Remove from the heat and stir in the basil. Season with ground black pepper and serve with crusty bread or stirred through pasta.'
