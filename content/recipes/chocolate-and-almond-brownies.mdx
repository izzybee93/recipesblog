servings: makes 16
ingredients:
  - 2 tbsp ground flaxseed
  - 115g brown sugar
  - 90g vegetable oil
  - 70ml water
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract or vanilla bean paste
  - 60g cocoa powder
  - 110g almond flour
  - ½ tsp sea salt
  - ¼ tsp baking powder
  - 50g dark chocolate chips
  - 50g flaked almonds
directions:
  - >-
    Whisk together the flaxseed and 6 tbsp water in a small bowl and set aside for 15 minutes to thicken.
  - Preheat the oven to 160°C. Line an 8x8 dish with parchment.
  - >-
    In a large bowl, add the flax egg, sugar and oil and mix until fully
    incorporated. Add the water and the vanilla and mix for another 2 minutes.
  - >-
    In a separate bowl, combine the cocoa powder, almond flour, baking powder
    and sea salt. Add to the wet mixture and stir until fully combined, with no
    lumps. Stir in the chocolate chips and 35g of the flaked almonds.
  - >-
    Pour into the pan and sprinkle the remaming almonds on top. Bake for 35-45 minutes. Allow to cool completely
    before slicing.
