---
title: Mashed peas on toast
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.292Z'
categories:
  - breakfast
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/mashed-peas-on-toast.jpeg
servings: serves 1
ingredients:
  - '100g peas, cooked'
  - 1 tbsp tahini
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - 'Pinch sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - 'Small handful mint leaves, finely chopped'
  - 2 slices bread, toasted
  - 1 tsp sesame seeds, toasted
directions:
  - >-
    Add the peas, tahini, lemon, garlic, salt and pepper to a small bowl and mash until a chunky paste forms. Stir in the herbs and season to taste.
  - >-
    Serve on toasted bread and top with a sprinkle of sesame seeds.
