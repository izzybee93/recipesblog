---
title: "Roasted sweet potato, chickpeas and crispy kale"
date: "2025-12-07"
categories: ["mains"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/roasted-sweet-potato-chickpeas-and-crispy-kale.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 large or 2 medium sweet potatoes
  - 1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  - 2 tsp cumin
  - 1 tsp ground coriander 
  - ¼ tsp chilli powder
  - 125g crispy kale (see recipe)
  - 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds
  - 2 tbsp tahini sauce (see recipe)
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare 2 baking trays.
  - Slice the sweet potato in half lengthwise and place it cut-side down on a tray. Bake for 45 minutes.
  - Add the chickpeas with the cumin, coriander and chilli powder to a tray. Bake for 25 minutes.
  - Make the crispy kale.
  - Place the sweet potato in serving bowls. Top with the roasted chickpeas, crispy kale, pumpkin seeds and a drizzle of tahini sauce.
