---
title: "Roasted sweet potato, chickpeas and crispy kale"
date: "2025-12-07"
categories: ["mains"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/roasted-sweet-potato-chickpeas-and-crispy-kale.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 2 medium sweet potatoes, chopped
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  - 1 tsp cumin
  - 100g crispy kale (see recipe)
  - 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds
  - '## Any sauce:'
  - ½ harissa aubergine and bean dip (see recipe)
  - 2 tbsp tahini sauce (see recipe)
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare 2 baking trays.
  - Add the sweet potato to a tray and brush with the olive oil. Roast for 20-25 minutes.
  - Add the chickpeas and cumin to a tray and mix until coated. Roast for 10 minutes, then add the pumpkin seeds and roast for another 10 minutes, until the chickpeas are crispy.
  - Make the harissa aubergine and bean dip or the tahini sauce.
  - Make the crispy kale.
  - Spoon the harissa aubergine dip into serving bowls and top with the sweet potato, kale, chickpeas and pumpkin seeds. Alternatively top with a drizzle of tahini sauce.
