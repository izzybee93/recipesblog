---
title: "Roasted sweet potato, chickpeas and crispy kale"
date: "2025-12-07"
categories: ["mains"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/roasted-sweet-potato-chickpeas-and-crispy-kale.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 2 medium sweet potatoes, chopped
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  - 1 tsp cumin
  - 100g crispy kale (see recipe)
  - 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds
  - ½ harissa aubergine and bean dip (optional, see recipe)
  - 2 tbsp tahini sauce (optional, see recipe)
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare 2 baking trays.
  - Add the sweet potato to a tray and brush with the olive oil. Roast for 20-25 minutes.
  - Add the chickpeas and cumin to a tray. Bake for 20 minutes until crispy.
  - Make the harissa aubergine and bean dip or the tahini sauce.
  - Make the crispy kale.
  - Spoon the aubergine dip into serving bowls. Top with the sweet potato, kale, chickpeas, pumpkin seeds and a drizzle of tahini sauce.
