title: Creamy pasta
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.279Z'
categories:
  - mains
  - pasta
featured_image: /images/recipes/creamy-pasta.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 180g dry pasta
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - Ground black pepper, to taste
  - '## Any veggie:'
  - '2 courgettes, chopped'
  - '½ broccoli, chopped and roasted'
  - '## Any additional veggie:'
  - 150g peas, cooked
  - 60g spinach
  - '## Sauce'
  - Creamy pasta sauce (see recipe)
directions:
  - Cook the pasta, reserving some of the pasta water.
  - In a large pan, heat the oil on a medium heat. Add the raw veggie and cook for 5-10 minutes, until softened and lightly browned.
  - Make the creamy pasta sauce. Add to the pan and gently heat through. Stir in a few tbsp of pasta water, adding more as needed.
  - Add the pasta, any roasted veggie and additional veggie. Stir until combined and heated through. Serve topped with ground black pepper.
