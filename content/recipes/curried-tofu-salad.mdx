title: Curried tofu salad

servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - '1 large sweet potato, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 pepper, chopped'
  - '1 courgette, sliced'
  - '280g extra firm tofu, cubed'
  - 80g rocket
  - '## Curry paste'
  - ½ tbsp tomato paste
  - 2 heaped tsp curry powder
  - ½ tbsp apple cider vinegar
  - ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - '¼ tsp sea salt'
  - '## Dressing'
  - ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - Juice of ½ lime
  - 'Pinch sea salt, to taste'
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare 2 baking trays.
  - >-
    Place the sweet potato on a tray and brush with ½ tbsp olive oil. Roast for 20–25
    minutes, until softened and starting to brown.
  - >-
    In a small bowl, add all the curry paste ingredients and mix well. Toss the tofu in
    the curry paste until coated, then place on a baking tray. Add the pepper and brush with the remaining curry
    paste. Roast for 15 minutes or, alternatively, cook the tofu and pepper in an air fryer for 12–14 minutes.
  - >-
    Heat the remaining ½ tbsp olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the courgette
    slices and cook until slightly browned on one side, then flip each slice and cook for another 5
    minutes.
  - Whisk together all the dressing ingredients in a small jug.
  - >-
    In a large bowl, add the rocket, veggies and tofu. Add the dressing,
    gently toss together and serve.
