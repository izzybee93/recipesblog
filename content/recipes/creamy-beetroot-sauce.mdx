---
title: "Creamy beetroot sauce"
date: "2026-07-26"
categories:
  - sauces
featured_image: "/images/recipes/creamy-beetroot-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '1 large beetroot, cooked and chopped'
  - 200g silken tofu
  - ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 1 tsp miso paste
  - 1 tbsp fresh dill
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - ¼ tsp cumin (optional)
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - Juice of ½ lemon
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a tray with baking paper.
  - Place the beetroot on the tray, coat with the olive oil and roast for 20 minutes. Leave to cool for 10 minutes.
  - Add all the ingredients except the lemon juice to a blender and blend until smooth.
  - If serving warm, add the sauce to a pan and stir over medium-low heat until smooth and slightly thickened. Add a few tbsp water (or pasta water) as needed. Stir in the lemon juice.
  - If serving cold with roasted veggies or a grain bowl, stir in the lemon juice.
