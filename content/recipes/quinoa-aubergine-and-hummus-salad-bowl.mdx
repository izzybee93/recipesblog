servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 60g quinoa
  - '½ large aubergine, chopped'
  - ½ tbsp olive oil
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - ½ tsp smoked paprika
  - '150g cherry tomatoes, chopped'
  - '60g artichoke hearts, chopped'
  - 'Small handful fresh basil, chopped'
  - '## Any hummus'
  - 4 tbsp sun-dried tomato hummus (see recipe)
  - 4 tbsp red pepper hummus (see recipe)
  - '## Dressing'
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - ½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - Pinch sea salt, to taste
directions:
  - Cook the quinoa.
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a large baking tray, add the aubergine and brush with the olive oil. Add the chickpeas and smoked paprika and brush to coat. Roast for 20–25 minutes.
  - Whisk together the dressing ingredients in a small jug.
  - 'In a large bowl, add the quinoa, tomatoes, artichoke, aubergine, chickpeas and basil. Pour over the dressing and mix well. Serve topped with spoonfuls of hummus and a sprinkle of paprika.'
