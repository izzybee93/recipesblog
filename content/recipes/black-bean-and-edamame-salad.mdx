date: "2026-08-13"
categories:
  - mains

featured_image: "/images/recipes/black-bean-and-edamame-salad.jpeg"
servings: "serves 3"
ingredients:
  - '1 large sweet potato, chopped'
  - ½ tbsp olive oil
  - '1 400g can black beans, drained and rinsed'
  - '200g edamame, cooked'
  - 100g sweetcorn
  - '3 medium tomatoes, chopped'
  - 'Small handful coriander, chopped'
  - '## Dressing'
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - Juice of 1 lime
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ½ tsp smoked paprika
  - Pinch chilli flakes
  - ¼ tsp sea salt, to taste
directions:
  - 'Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a baking tray. Add the sweet potato, brush with the olive oil and roast for 20-25 minutes.'
  - Whisk all the dressing ingredients together in a small jug.
  - 'In a large bowl, add the beans, vegetables and coriander. Pour over the dressing and gently mix.'
