title: Veggie paella
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.318Z'
categories:

featured_image: /images/recipes/veggie-paella.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 1L vegetable stock
  - 2 saffron threads (sub with 2 tsp turmeric)
  - '½ onion, chopped'
  - '1 red pepper, chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '1 large tomato, diced'
  - 150g arborio rice
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - ½ tsp sweet paprika
  - Pinch sea salt, to taste
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - ½ tsp dried thyme
  - 250g frozen peas
  - 2 lemon wedges
  - Small handful fresh parsley, chopped
directions:
  - Break up the saffron and add it to the vegetable stock.
  - >-
    Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion
    and pepper and cook for 5 minutes until softened. Add
    the garlic and cook for another minute.
  - >-
    Stir in the tomato, smoked paprika, sweet paprika, thyme and salt, and cook for
    2 minutes.
  - "Add the rice and cook for 1 minute until coated."
  - >-
    Slowly pour in half of the vegetable stock. Do not stir after this point or the rice 
    will release starches and become creamier. Increase to medium-high heat and cook for 2 minutes.
  - >-
    Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer for 15-20 minutes until rice is al dente, adding the
    rest of the stock as needed, but do not over-stir.
  - "Gently stir in the peas. Pay attention to the socarrat forming on the bottom of the pan (the golden rice crust), which will crackle and pop, and create a nutty aroma. This should take 1-2 minutes to form."
  - 'Serve topped with parsley, ground black pepper and squeeze of lemon juice.'
