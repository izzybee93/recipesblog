---
title: "Blackberry, apple and almond butter smoothie"
date: "2026-10-05"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/blackberry-apple-and-almond-butter-smoothie.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 120g blackberries
  - '1 small apple, chopped'
  - '2 tbsp chia seeds, soaked (optional)'
  - 1 tbsp lemon juice
  - ½ tsp lemon zest
  - 1 tsp cinnamon
  - ¼ tsp vanilla extract (optional)
  - 1 tbsp almond butter
  - 4 tbsp oats
  - 200ml plant milk
directions:
  - Place all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
---
