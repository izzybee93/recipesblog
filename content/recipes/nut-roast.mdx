title: Nut roast
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.294Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/nut-roast.jpeg
servings: serves 8–12
ingredients:
  - '300g mixed nuts (cashews, almonds, walnuts, pecans, brazil nuts)'
  - 150g breadcrumbs
  - 2 tbsp ground flaxseed
  - '100g chestnut mushrooms'
  - 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  - '1 onion, finely chopped'
  - '2 garlic cloves, minced'
  - '2 celery stalks, finely chopped'
  - '2 large carrots, grated'
  - 65g tomato paste
  - 2 tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 tbsp miso paste

  - Handful chopped thyme and rosemary
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a loaf tin.
  - >-
    Add the nuts to a food processor and until finely
    chopped. Transfer to a large bowl, and stir in the breadcrumbs and flaxseed. Set aside.
  - Add the mushrooms to the food processor and pulse until finely chopped.
  - >-
    Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion, celery and carrot and cook for 5 minutes, until softened.
    Add the garlic and mushrooms and cook for 2 minutes. 
  - >-
    Stir in the tomato paste, soy sauce and miso paste and cook for 2 minutes.
  - >-
    Str in the nut mixture and roasted veggies until a thick dough forms.
  - Transfer to the tin and bake for 50 minutes.
  - >-
    Leave to cool for 10 minutes in the tin before slicing.
