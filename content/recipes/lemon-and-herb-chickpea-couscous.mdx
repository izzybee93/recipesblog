---
title: "Lemon and herb chickpea couscous"
date: "2026-08-09"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/lemon-and-herb-chickpea-couscous.jpeg"
servings: "serves 3-4"
ingredients:
  - 150g couscous
  - ~300ml vegetable stock
  - '6 sun-dried tomatoes, chopped'
  - '1 x 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - Juice of 1 lemon
  - 1 tsp dried oregano
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 'Small handful fresh parsley or basil, chopped'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - 'In a large bowl, add the couscous, then pour in the vegetable stock until the liquid is 0.5cm above the couscous. Set aside for 10 minutes.'
  - 'Run a fork through the couscous. Add the lemon juice and oregano and mix well. Add the sun-dried tomatoes and chickpeas and gently mix.'
  - 'Add the olive oil, fresh herbs and ground black pepper and mix again. Serve warm or cold.'
---
