featured_image: "/images/recipes/aubergine-sweet-potato-and-tofu-bowl-with-katsu-curry-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '1 aubergine, sliced'
  - '1 sweet potato, chopped'
  - 1½ tbsp olive oil
  - '225g extra firm tofu, sliced'
  - Katsu curry sauce (see recipe)
  - '1 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted'
  - 'Small handful coriander or Thai basil, chopped'
directions:
  - Make the katsu curry sauce.
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a baking tray. Add the aubergine and sweet potato, brush with 1 tbsp olive oil and roast for 25 minutes, until softened.
  - Heat ½ tbsp olive oil in a grill pan over medium-high heat. Add the tofu and grill until lightly browned on each side.
  - Spoon the sauce into serving bowls. Top with the veggies and tofu. Sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds and herbs.
