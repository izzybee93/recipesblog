---
title: Berry pearl barley porridge
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.260Z'
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: /images/recipes/berry-pearl-barley-porridge.jpeg
servings: serves 1
ingredients:
  - '120g pearl barley, cooked'
  - 100ml plant milk
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 100g mixed berries
  - ½ tsp cinnamon
  - 1 tsp almond butter (optional) 
directions:
  - >-
    Add the pearl barley, milk, chia seeds and ¾ of the berries to a pan and cook for 5 minutes on
    a medium heat, stirring frequently until the juices start to release. Stir
    in the cinnamon. 
  - Serve topped with the remaining berries and a drizzle of almond butter.
---
