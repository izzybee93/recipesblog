---
title: "Strawberry and blueberry smoothie bowl"
date: "2026-10-02"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/strawberry-and-blueberry-smoothie-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - '½ frozen banana, chopped'
  - 60g strawberries
  - 25g blueberries
  - 1 tbsp almond butter
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 2 tbsp oats
  - ¼ tsp lemon zest
  - ¼ tsp vanilla extract
  - 50ml plant milk
  - 1 tbsp granola
  - '1 tbsp almonds, chopped'
directions:
  - Slice a few strawberries and set aside for topping.
  - 'Blend the banana, remaining strawberries, almond butter, chia seeds, oats, lemon zest, vanilla and plant milk until smooth. Transfer to a bowl and top with strawberry slices, blueberries, granola and almonds.'
