---
title: Grissini
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.284Z'
categories:
  - bread
featured_image: /images/recipes/grissini.jpeg
servings: makes 30
ingredients:
  - '380g strong white bread flour, divided'
  - 4g instant yeast
  - 6g sea salt
  - 180ml water
  - 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
directions:
  - >-
    In a large bowl, combine 180g of flour, yeast, salt, water and oil. Mix well.
  - >-
    Add the remaining flour and mix until the dough is smooth.
  - >-
    Transfer the dough to a floured surface and knead for 15 minutes, until soft and stretchy. Pat into a flat rectangle. 
    Fold along the longest side, folding the top third of the dough inwards, then
    overlap with the bottom third, to form a long rectangle. Cover with a damp
    tea towel and leave to rise for 1-1½ hours.
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C and line a large baking tray with parchment paper.
  - From the shorter side of the dough, cut into 30 equal strips. Roll each piece
    gently, starting from the center and pulling towards the
    ends until it is the length of the tray. Alternatively, twist two sticks
    together in a spiral. Place on the tray and repeat with the remaining
    dough pieces, spacing 2cm apart.
  - >-
    Bake for 13-15 minutes, or until golden, turning the tray around in the last few minutes of baking. Transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
