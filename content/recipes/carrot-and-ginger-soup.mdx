---
title: "Carrot and ginger soup"
date: "2026-08-31"
categories:
  - mains
  - soup
featured_image: "/images/recipes/carrot-and-ginger-soup.jpeg"
servings: "serves 4"
ingredients:
  - '450g carrots, chopped into medium chunks'
  - '1 large sweet potato, chopped into large chunks'
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 onion, chopped'
  - '2 garlic cloves, minced'
  - '1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced'
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - 1L vegetable stock
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - 'Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Drizzle the carrots and sweet potato with 1 tbsp of oil and roast for 15-20 minutes.'
  - 'Heat the remaining oil in a large pan, add the onion and cook for 3 minutes until softened. Add the garlic and cook for another minute. Stir in the ginger and cumin, and cook for 1 more minute.'
  - 'Add the carrots, sweet potato and stock and bring to the boil, then reduce the heat. Cover and cook for 10 minutes until the carrots are tender.'
  - 'Remove from the heat and blend until smooth. Stir in the lemon juice, season to taste and reheat to serve.'
