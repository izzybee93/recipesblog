---
title: "Green pesto"
date: "2025-12-29"
categories: ["sauces"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/green-pesto.jpeg"
servings: "serves 6"
ingredients:
  - 40g frozen edamame
  - 40g frozen peas
  - 30g spinach
  - 10g fresh basil
  - 18g pumpkin seeds or sunflower seeds, toasted
  - 1 tbsp nutritional yeast
  - 1½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - ½ tsp sea salt, to taste
  - 3–4 tbsp water
directions:
  - Cook the peas and edamame for 2 minutes. 
  - Place all the ingredients in a food processor and process until almost smooth. Add more water, a tbsp at a time, until the pesto reaches the desired consistency.
  - Serve with pasta or gnocchi, or spread on toasted sourdough with your favourite veggies.
