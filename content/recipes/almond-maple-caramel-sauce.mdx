---
title: "Almond maple caramel sauce"
date: "2026-09-07"
categories:
  - sauces
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/almond-maple-caramel-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2-4"
ingredients:
  - 60g almond butter
  - 35g maple syrup
  - ½ tsp vanilla extract
  - Zest of ½ orange (optional)
  - Pinch sea salt
  - 2-4 tbsp plant milk
directions:
  - Add the almond butter and maple syrup to a small pan and heat over a low heat until smooth.
  - Remove from the heat and stir in the vanilla extract, orange zest and salt.
  - Stir in the milk, adding 1 tbsp at a time until smooth and pourable. Serve warm with pancakes, waffles or crepes, topped with banana, blueberries or orange slices, or drizzle over ice cream.
---
