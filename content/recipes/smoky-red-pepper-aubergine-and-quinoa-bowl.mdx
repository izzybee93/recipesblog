---
title: "Smoky red pepper, aubergine and quinoa bowl"
date: "2026-02-20"
categories: ["mains"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/smoky-red-pepper-aubergine-and-quinoa-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 80g quinoa
  - 1 aubergine, chopped
  - 1 Romano pepper, chopped
  - 1½ tbsp olive oil
  - 1 400g can black beans, drained and rinsed
  - ½ smoky red pepper sauce (see recipe)
  - Small handful fresh coriander, chives or parsley, chopped
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the quinoa.
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Add the aubergine, brush with 1 tbsp olive oil and roast for 10–15 minutes. 
  - Add the pepper to the tray and brush with ½ tbsp olive oil. Roast for another 8–10 minutes.
  - Make the smoky red pepper sauce.
  - Assemble the bowls with the quinoa, black beans, aubergine and pepper. Drizzle with the red pepper sauce and top with fresh herbs.
