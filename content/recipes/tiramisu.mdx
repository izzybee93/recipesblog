---
title: "Tiramisu"
date: "2026-04-16"
categories:
  - desserts
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/tiramisu.jpeg"
servings: "serves 8"
ingredients:
  - 150ml espresso
  - 350g silken tofu
  - 40g caster sugar
  - 2 tsp lemon juice
  - 1½ tsp vanilla extract
  - Pinch sea salt
  - 80ml oat or soya cream
  - 12 savoiardi biscuits (see recipe)
  - 1 tbsp cocoa powder
directions:
  - In a blender, add the tofu, sugar, lemon juice, vanilla extract and salt, and blend until smooth. Gradually blend in the cream until fully combined.
  - Spoon ⅓ of the creamy mixture into the base of a dish. Quickly dip the biscuits in the coffee on each side, then layer on top. Repeat with another layer of creamy mixture then biscuits, then top with the remaining ⅓ cream mixture.
  - Smooth the top and dust with sifted cocoa powder. Transfer to the fridge for at least 4 hours, or overnight, before serving.
