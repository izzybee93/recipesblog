---
title: "Seeded rye bread"
date: "2026-06-06"
categories:
  - bread
featured_image: "/images/recipes/seeded-rye-bread.jpeg"
servings: "makes 1 loaf"
ingredients:
  - 255g wholemeal rye flour
  - 205g strong white bread flour
  - 3g instant yeast
  - 340ml water
  - 125g bulgur wheat
  - 40g flaxseeds
  - 85g sunflower seeds
  - 85g pumpkin seeds
  - 7g sea salt
  - 30g dark brown soft sugar
directions:
  - 'The evening before baking, make the dough. In a large bowl, mix together the flour and yeast. Add the water and mix until a rough, sticky dough forms. Knead briefly, then cover with a damp tea towel and lid. Leave overnight to prove for 8–12 hours at room temperature.'
  - 'The next morning, rinse the bulgur wheat. In a medium bowl, combine the bulgur wheat and seeds. Stir in 260ml boiling water, then cover and leave to soak for 30 minutes.'
  - Prepare a large loaf tin.
  - 'Add the soaked grain mixture, salt and sugar to a large bowl. Mix well, then add the dough and mix again until everything is evenly combined. The mixture will be sticky and quite heavy.'
  - Transfer to the tin and press firmly into the corners. Smooth the top with a wet spoon. Cover loosely with a damp tea towel and leave for 2-3 hours, until risen close to the top of the tin.
  - Preheat the oven to 220°C. Bake for 15 minutes.
  - Reduce the temperature to 200°C and bake for another 15 minutes.
  - Reduce the temperature to 160°C and bake for 45 minutes.
  - 'For a crispy crust, remove the loaf from the tin and bake for another 10 minutes.'
  - 'Transfer to a wire rack and leave to cool completely, for at least 4 hours, or overnight, before slicing into about 16 thin slices.'
  - 'Serve toasted with your favourite toppings. Store for 3-4 days, or freeze slices separated by a sheet of baking paper for up to 4 months.'
