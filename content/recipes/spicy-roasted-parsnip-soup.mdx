title: Spicy roasted parsnip soup
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.310Z'
categories:

  - soup
featured_image: /images/recipes/spicy-roasted-parsnip-soup.jpeg
servings: serves 4
ingredients:
  - '680g parsnips, diced'
  - '1 large onion, quartered'
  - 2 garlic cloves, peeled
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 tsp ground coriander
  - 1 tsp cumin
  - 1 tsp chilli powder, to taste
  - 900ml vegetable stock
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 220°C. Prepare a large baking tray.
  - >-
    Place the onion, garlic and parsnips on the baking tray. Add the olive oil and spices and mix well. Roast for 30 minutes until tender.
  - >-
    Transfer to a large pan, then add 600ml vegetable stock. Blend with a hand
    blender until smooth.
  - >-
    Add the remaining vegetable stock as needed, then season and heat until
    simmering. Stir in the lemon juice. Serve topped with ground black pepper.
