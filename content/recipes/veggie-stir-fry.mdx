---
title: "Veggie stir fry"
date: "2026-09-14"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/veggie-stir-fry.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g rice
  - 1 tbsp sesame oil
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '120g edamame, cooked'
  - 50g soya chunks (optional)
  - '## Any 2 veggies:'
  - '2 carrots, chopped'
  - '1 courgette, chopped'
  - '1 pepper, chopped'
  - '¼ broccoli, chopped into florets'
  - '150g baby corn, chopped'
  - 150g sugar snap peas
  - 150g beansprouts
  - '## Any sauce:'
  - Almond stir fry sauce (see recipe)
  - 'Lemon, ginger and ponzu stir fry sauce (see recipe)'
  - Miso and ginger stir fry sauce (see recipe)
  - 'Sesame, ginger and lime stir fry sauce (see recipe)'
  - '## Toppings'
  - 'Handful fresh coriander, chopped'
  - 2 tsp sesame seeds
  - 2 tbsp crispy onion (optional)
directions:
  - Cook the rice.
  - 'In a small bowl, add the soya chunks and enough boiling water or vegetable stock to just cover them. Leave to sit for 5-10 minutes.'
  - 'Heat the sesame oil in a large wok. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Add the soya chunks and cook for a few more minutes, until slightly browned. Add the edamame and veggies and cook for 5 minutes, stirring frequently.'
  - Make the sauce. Add to the wok and cook for 5-10 minutes until heated through and slightly thickened. Add a few tbsp water as needed. Stir in the coriander.
  - Serve with the rice and top with a sprinkle of sesame seeds and crispy onion.
