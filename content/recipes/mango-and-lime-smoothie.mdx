---
title: "Mango and lime smoothie"
date: "2026-10-01"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/mango-and-lime-smoothie.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 150g frozen mango
  - 50g pineapple
  - 1 tbsp shredded coconut (optional)
  - '2 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 4 tbsp oats
  - ½ tsp lime zest
  - 1 tsp lime juice
  - 2 tsp almond butter (optional)
  - 200ml plant milk
directions:
  - Place all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
