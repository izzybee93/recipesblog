---
title: Chocolate ganache
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.276Z'
categories:
  - treats
featured_image: /images/recipes/chocolate-ganache.jpeg
servings: serves 1 cake
ingredients:
  - 180g dark chocolate
  - 40g plant butter
  - 70ml oat or soya cream
  - ½ tsp vanilla extract
  - Pinch sea salt
directions:
  - 'Melt the chocolate and plant butter in a bowl over a pan of boiling water.'
  - 'Remove from the heat and stir in the cream, vanilla and salt until smooth and glossy.'
  - >-
    Leave to cool until slightly thickened, or transfer to the fridge for 5–10 minutes, until the ganache is spreadable but not completely set.
