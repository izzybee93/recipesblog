---
title: Chocolate ganache
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.276Z'
categories:
  - treats
featured_image: /images/recipes/chocolate-ganache.jpeg
servings: makes 2 layers
ingredients:
  - 180g dark chocolate
  - 70g plant butter
  - 50ml soya or oat cream
  - ½ tsp vanilla extract
  - Pinch sea salt
directions:
  - 'Melt the chocolate in a bowl over a pan of boiling water. Stir in the cream, vanilla and salt until the ganache is smooth and glossy.'
  - >-
    Leave to cool until slightly thickened, or transfer to the fridge for 5-10 minutes, until the ganache is spreadable but not completely set before decorating your cake.
