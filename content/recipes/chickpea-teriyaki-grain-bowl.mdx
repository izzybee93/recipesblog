title: Chickpea teriyaki grain bowl
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.271Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/chickpea-teriyaki-grain-bowl.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - '100g rice or quinoa, rinsed'
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - ½ tsp smoked paprika
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ¼ tsp chilli powder
  - ½ tsp turmeric
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '## Any 1-2 roasted veggies:'
  - 1 small aubergine, chopped
  - 1 pepper, chopped
  - ¼ broccoli, chopped into florets
  - 200g mushrooms, sliced
  - 150g baby corn, chopped
  - '## Any 1-2 fresh veggies:'
  - 2 carrots, finely sliced
  - ¼ cucumber, chopped
  - 150g sugar snap peas
  - 1 avocado, sliced
  - '## Sauce'
  - 4-6 tbsp teriyaki sauce
directions:
  - Cook the rice or quinoa.
  - >-
    In a large pan, add the chickpeas and spices. Toss together and cook on a medium-high heat until crispy.
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Add the veggies to the tray and brush with the olive oil. Roast for 15-25 minutes, or
    alternatively, cook in an air fryer for 10-15 minutes, until softened and slightly browned.
  - >-
    Assemble the bowls with the rice or quinoa, then top with roasted veggies, fresh veggies and chickpeas.
    Serve with a drizzle of teriyaki sauce.
