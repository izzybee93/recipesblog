---
title: "Orange and berry boost porridge"
date: "2026-09-17"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/orange-and-berry-boost-porridge.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - 50g oats
  - 250ml plant milk or water
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - '1 orange, peeled and chopped'
  - ½ tsp orange zest
  - ½ tsp cinnamon
  - 1 tsp dried cranberries
  - 1 tbsp hemp seeds
  - 1 tsp goji berries
directions:
  - 'In a small pan, add the oats, plant milk or water and chia seeds. Cook for a few minutes over medium heat, stirring frequently. Stir in half of the orange slices, orange zest, cranberries and cinnamon.'
  - Serve topped with the remaining orange slices, hemp seeds and goji berries.
