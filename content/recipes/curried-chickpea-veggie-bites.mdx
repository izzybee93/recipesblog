title: "Curried chickpea veggie bites"
date: "2025-08-16"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/curried-chickpea-veggie-bites.jpeg"
servings: "makes 12"
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 2 tbsp oats
  - 1 tbsp cornflour
  - '1 small onion, finely chopped'
  - '½ red pepper, finely chopped'
  - '15g fresh coriander, chopped'
  - ½ tsp sea salt
  - ½ tsp garlic powder
  - 1 tbsp curry powder
  - 1 tbsp tahini
  - 1 tbsp lemon juice
  - 1-2 tbsp water
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a baking tray.
  - Mash the chickpeas in a large bowl with a fork.
  - 'Add the oats, cornflour, onion, pepper, coriander, garlic powder, curry powder, salt, tahini and lemon juice. Mix well, adding 1-2 tbsp water as needed, until the mixture comes together when pressed.'
  - 'Divide and shape into 12 balls or patties. Bake for 25-30 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and crispy. Alternatively, cook in an air fryer for 15 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and crispy.'
  - Serve with roasted veggies or salad.
