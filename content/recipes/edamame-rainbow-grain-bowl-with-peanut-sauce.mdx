title: "Edamame rainbow grain bowl with peanut sauce"
date: "2026-07-21"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/edamame-rainbow-grain-bowl-with-peanut-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g rice or quinoa
  - '150g edamame, cooked'
  - '½ cucumber, sliced'
  - 2 tsp sesame seeds
  - '## Any 1-2 veggies:'
  - '½ red pepper, finely sliced'
  - 1 medium aubergine, chopped and roasted
  - 6 tbsp pickled red cabbage (see recipe)
  - 4 tbsp pickled radishes (see recipe)
  - '## Sauce'
  - Peanut sauce (see recipe)
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the rice or quinoa.
  - Make the peanut sauce.
  - Assemble the bowls with the rice or quinoa, then top with edamame, cucumber, and veggies. Drizzle over the peanut sauce and top with sesame seeds.
