---
title: "Roasted butternut squash and coriander hummus"
date: "2026-04-13"
categories:
  - snacks
featured_image: "/images/recipes/roasted-butternut-squash-and-coriander-hummus.jpeg"
servings: "serves 8"
ingredients:
  - '1 butternut squash, halved'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 garlic clove
  - 3 tbsp tahini
  - 'Juice of 1 lemon'
  - 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - ½ tsp sea salt, to taste
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - '25g coriander, roughly chopped'
  - 2-4 tbsp water
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Chop one half of the butternut squash into cubes.
  - 'Place the whole half of the squash on the tray and coat with ½ tbsp olive oil. Turn the squash face down and roast for 20 minutes.'
  - 'Add the chopped squash and garlic to the tray. Coat with ½ tbsp olive oil and roast for another 20 minutes, until softened.'
  - Combine the tahini and lemon juice in a food processor and process for 30 seconds. Scrape the sides of the bowl then process for 30 more seconds.
  - 'Add the extra virgin olive oil, and salt. Process for 30 seconds, scrape down the sides, then process another 30 seconds or until well blended.'
  - 'Scoop the flesh from the roasted half and transfer to a food processor. Add the garlic, chickpeas and coriander, reserving a few leaves for garnish. Process until smooth, adding a few tbsp water as needed.'
  - Taste for salt and adjust as needed. Transfer to a large serving bowl, top with the roasted squash and a few extra coriander leaves. Store in an airtight container and refrigerate for up to one week.
