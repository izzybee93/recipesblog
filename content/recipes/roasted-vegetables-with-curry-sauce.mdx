title: "Roasted vegetables with curry sauce"
date: "2026-09-27"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/roasted-vegetables-with-curry-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '2 tbsp flaked almonds, toasted'
  - '## Any veggie:'
  - '¼ cauliflower, chopped into florets'
  - '¼ broccoli, chopped into florets'
  - '½ butternut squash, chopped'
  - '1 aubergine, chopped'
  - '## Curry sauce'
  - '120g cannellini beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 1 tbsp tomato paste
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 1 tsp cumin
  - 1 tsp garam masala
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - ½ tsp ground coriander
  - ½ tsp garlic powder
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - 2-4 tbsp water
directions:
  - 'To make the curry sauce, add all the sauce ingredients to a blender and blend until smooth and creamy. Add more water as needed.'
  - 'Preheat the oven to 200°C and prepare a large baking tray. Add the veggies, coat with the olive oil and season with ground black pepper. Roast for 20-30 minutes, until soft and slightly browned.'
  - 'Add the sauce to a small pan and gently heat for 3-5 minutes, stirring frequently. Add a splash of water if needed.'
  - Spoon the sauce into serving bowls. Top with the roasted veggies and toasted flaked almonds.
