title: Roasted cauliflower and sweet potato bowl with lime and chilli dressing
date: '2025-11-02'
categories:

  - salad
featured_image: /images/recipes/roasted-cauliflower-and-sweet-potato-bowl-with-lime-and-chilli-dressing.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - '2 sweet potatoes, chopped'
  - '¼ cauliflower, chopped into florets'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - ¾ tsp cumin
  - ½ tsp smoked paprika
  - ¼ tsp ground coriander
  - 1 tsp water
  - '¼ cucumber, chopped'
  - 'Handful coriander, chopped'
  - Lime and chilli dressing (see recipe)
  - 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds, toasted
  - '## Any legume:'
  - 1 400g can black beans, drained and rinsed
  - 1 400g can green lentils, drained and rinsed
  - 150g edamame, cooked
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a large baking tray and add the sweet potato. Brush with ½ tbsp olive oil and roast for 5 minutes.
  - >-
    Whisk the remaining ½ tbsp olive oil, spices and water together in a small bowl.
  - Remove the tray from the oven and add the cauliflower. Drizzle the spice mixture on top and brush until coated. Roast for 15–20 minutes or until softened and lightly browned.
  - >-
    Make the lime and chilli dressing.
  - >-
    In a large bowl, add the cucumber, legumes, coriander and roasted veggies. Pour over the dressing and gently toss to combine. Serve topped with toasted pumpkin seeds.
