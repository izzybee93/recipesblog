---
title: "Butternut squash, bean and almond dip"
date: "2026-09-12"
categories:
  - snacks
featured_image: "/images/recipes/butternut-squash-bean-and-almond-dip.jpeg"
servings: "serves 6-8"
ingredients:
  - '600g butternut squash, peeled and chopped'
  - 2½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 2 garlic cloves
  - 225g baby plum tomatoes
  - 4 sprigs thyme
  - '3 sage leaves, chopped'
  - '60g almonds, toasted'
  - '1 400g can butter beans, drained and rinsed'
  - Juice of 1 lemon
  - '1 tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - 2-4 tbsp water
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Add the butternut squash and brush with 1 tbsp olive oil. Place the garlic cloves in the middle and scatter over the sage. Cover loosely with foil and roast for 30 minutes.
  - Prepare a small baking tray. Add the thyme leaves and tomatoes and coat with ½ tbsp oil. Roast for 20 minutes, until soft and starting to caramelise.
  - Roughly chop 20g of the toasted almonds and set aside.
  - Add the remaining 40g of almonds to a food processor, along with the butternut squash, 1 tbsp olive oil, butter beans, lemon juice, garlic, ground black pepper and salt. Process for 30 seconds. Add 2 tbsp water and process until smooth, scraping down the sides and adding a few tbsp water as needed. Season to taste, adding more lemon juice or salt as needed.
  - Transfer to a serving bowl and spoon over the roasted tomatoes. Top with the chopped almonds and extra herbs. Serve with grissini, crackers or crunchy veggies.
