---
title: "Teriyaki tofu grain bowl"
date: "2025-12-04"
categories: ["mains"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/teriyaki-tofu-grain-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - 50g quinoa or rice
  - 1 tsp sesame oil 
  - 50g extra firm tofu, cubed
  - 50g edamame, cooked
  - 1 small beetroot, cooked and chopped 
  - ¼ cucumber, chopped
  - 2 tbsp pickled radishes (see recipe)
  - 2-4 tbsp teriyaki sauce
  - 1 tsp sesame seeds
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the quinoa or rice.
  - Heat the sesame oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add the tofu and cook for a few minutes on each side until browned.
  - Assemble the bowl with the quinoa or rice, topped with tofu, edamame, cucumber, beetroot and radish. Serve with a drizzle of teriyaki sauce and a sprinkle of sesame seeds.
