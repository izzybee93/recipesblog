---
title: "Teriyaki tofu grain bowl"
date: "2025-12-04"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/teriyaki-tofu-grain-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g quinoa or rice
  - 2 tsp sesame oil
  - '100g extra firm tofu, cubed'
  - '100g edamame, cooked'
  - '¼ cucumber, sliced'
  - 4-6 tbsp teriyaki sauce
  - 2 tsp sesame seeds
  - '## Any 1-2 veggies:'
  - '1 avocado, sliced'
  - '2 small beetroot, chopped and cooked'
  - '1 small aubergine, chopped and roasted'
  - '1 carrot, finely sliced'
  - '2 large radishes, finely sliced'
  - 4 tbsp pickled radishes (see recipe)
  - 6 tbsp pickled red cabbage (see recipe)
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the quinoa or rice.
  - Heat the sesame oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add the tofu and cook for a few minutes on each side until browned.
  - Assemble the bowl with the quinoa or rice, then top with tofu, edamame, cucumber and veggies. Serve with a drizzle of teriyaki sauce and a sprinkle of sesame seeds.
