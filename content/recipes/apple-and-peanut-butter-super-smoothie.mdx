  - '½ celery stick, chopped'
  - '2 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water'
  - 1 tbsp hemp seeds

  - 4 tbsp oats
  - ½ tsp cinnamon
  - Small handful spinach
  - 200ml plant milk
