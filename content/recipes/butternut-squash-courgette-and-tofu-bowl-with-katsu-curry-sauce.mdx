date: "2026-10-09"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/butternut-squash-courgette-and-tofu-bowl-with-katsu-curry-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '¼ butternut squash, chopped'
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - '225g extra firm tofu, sliced'
  - '1 large courgette, sliced'
  - Katsu curry sauce (see recipe)
  - '1 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted'
  - 1 tbsp crispy onion (optional)
directions:
  - Make the katsu curry sauce.
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a baking tray. Add the butternut squash, brush with 1 tbsp olive oil and roast for 30 minutes, until softened.
  - Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a grill pan over medium-high heat. Add the tofu and courgette and grill until lightly browned on each side.
  - Spoon the sauce into serving bowls. Top with the veggies and tofu. Sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds and crispy onion.
