title: "Aubergine and chickpea salad with tahini dressing"
date: "2026-07-28"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/aubergine-and-chickpea-salad-with-tahini-dressing.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '1 aubergine, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 80g rocket
  - '200g tomatoes, chopped'
  - Tahini dressing (see recipe)
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a baking tray. Add the aubergine, brush with the olive oil and roast for 15 minutes. Add the chickpeas and roast for another 10 minutes.
  - In a large bowl, add the rocket, tomatoes, aubergine and chickpeas.
  - Make the dressing, pour over the salad and toss to combine.
