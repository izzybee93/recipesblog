---
title: "Roasted red pepper pesto"
date: "2026-01-08"
categories: ["sauces"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/roasted-red-pepper-pesto.jpeg"
servings: "serves 4"
ingredients:
  - 1 red pepper, chopped
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 1 garlic clove
  - 15g pine nuts, toasted
  - 10g fresh basil
  - 1 tbsp nutritional yeast
  - 2 tsp lemon juice
  - ¼ tsp sea salt, to taste
  - Ground black pepper, to taste
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Place the peppers on a tray, coated with 1 tbsp olive oil. Roast for 15 minutes, until starting to brown at the edges. Remove from the oven and allow to cool.
  - In a food processor, add the pepper, garlic, toasted pine nuts, basil, nutritional yeast, lemon juice, salt, ground black pepper and remaining olive oil.
  - Process for 1 minute, until a smooth, thick pesto forms.
  - Serve with pasta or gnocchi, and your favourite veggies. Store refrigerated for up to 5 days, or freeze small portions in ice cube trays for up to 3 months.
