---
title: "Beetroot, carrot and lentil salad"
date: "2026-09-19"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/beetroot-carrot-and-lentil-salad.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 50g quinoa or giant couscous (optional)
  - '2 large beetroot, cooked and chopped'
  - '2 carrots, grated'
  - '1 400g can green lentils, drained and rinsed'
  - '2 tbsp walnuts, roughly chopped and toasted'
  - 'Small handful parsley, finely chopped'
  - 'Small handful dill, finely chopped'
  - Zingy lemon dressing (see recipe)
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the quinoa or couscous.
  - 'In a large bowl, mix together the beetroot, carrot, lentils, herbs and grains.'
  - Make the zingy lemon dressing. Pour over the salad and mix well. Serve topped with toasted walnuts.
