---
title: "Lemon and herb dressing"
date: "2026-08-18"
categories:
  - sauces
featured_image: "/images/recipes/lemon-and-herb-dressing.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2-3"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - ½ tsp maple syrup or honey (optional)
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - '2 tsp dried mixed herbs, such as oregano, basil and parsley'
directions:
  - Whisk the ingredients together in a small jug.
