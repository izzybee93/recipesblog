---
title: "Creamy harissa chickpea couscous"
date: "2026-08-28"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/creamy-harissa-chickpea-couscous.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - ½ tbsp olive oil
  - '½ onion, finely chopped'
  - '1 pepper, chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - ½ tbsp tomato paste
  - 1 tbsp harissa paste
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ½ tsp smoked paprika
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 90g giant couscous
  - 300ml vegetable stock
  - 150g cherry tomatoes
  - 50ml soya or oat cream
  - 60g spinach
  - 'Small handful fresh coriander, chopped'
  - Juice of ½ lemon
directions:
  - In a large pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion and pepper and cook for 5 minutes, until softened. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Stir in the tomato paste, harissa, cumin, smoked paprika and salt, and cook for 2 more minutes. Stir in the chickpeas.
  - Stir in the couscous until coated. Add the vegetable stock, bring to a simmer and cook for 4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the couscous is almost cooked through. Add a few tbsp water as needed.
  - Stir in the tomatoes and cream. Cook for 3 minutes, until heated through and the couscous is cooked. Stir in the spinach until wilted.
  - Remove from the heat and stir in the lemon juice and coriander. Serve topped with extra coriander.
