title: French toast with blueberry maple sauce
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.284Z'
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: /images/recipes/french-toast-with-blueberry-topping.jpeg
servings: serves 3
ingredients:
  - '6 slices sourdough bread, sliced about 1.5cm thick'
  - 230ml soya milk
  - 2 tsp maple syrup
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - 4 tbsp whole wheat plain flour
  - 1 tsp ground flaxseed
  - 1 tsp cinnamon
  - '1½ tsp plant butter'
  - 1½ blueberry maple sauce (see recipe)
directions:
  - >-
    In a shallow bowl, whisk together the milk, maple syrup, vanilla, flour,
    flaxseed and cinnamon.
  - Heat the butter in a frying pan over medium-high heat.
  - >-
    Whisk the batter again. Dip each side of the bread slices in the batter, let it soak for 10
    seconds, then add to the pan and cook for 2-3 minutes on each side, until golden brown.
  - Make the blueberry maple sauce.
  - 'Serve topped with the blueberry sauce, fruit and an extra drizzle of maple syrup.'
