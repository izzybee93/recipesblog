---
title: "Gnocchi with roasted butternut squash"
date: "2026-08-11"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/gnocchi-with-roasted-butternut-squash.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '½ butternut squash, cubed'
  - '½ onion, roughly chopped'
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - ¼ tsp nutmeg
  - '½ broccoli, chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - ~100ml vegetable stock
  - 50ml soya milk
  - 1 tsp dried sage
  - 200g gnocchi
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare 2 baking trays.
  - 'Place the butternut squash and onion on a tray and coat with 1 tbsp olive oil. Sprinkle over the nutmeg. Roast for 25-30 minutes, until tender.'
  - 'Place the broccoli on the other tray and brush with ½ tbsp olive oil. Roast for 10-15 minutes.'
  - 'Transfer the roasted squash and onion to a large bowl. Add the vegetable stock and soya milk, then blend until smooth. Add a splash more vegetable stock as needed.'
  - 'In a large pan, heat the remaining ½ tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Add the garlic and sage and cook for 1 minute.'
  - 'Add the blended sauce, salt and ground black pepper. Bring to a simmer, stirring occasionally, until heated through. Stir in the broccoli.'
  - Cook the gnocchi. Stir into the sauce until coated. Serve topped with ground black pepper.
