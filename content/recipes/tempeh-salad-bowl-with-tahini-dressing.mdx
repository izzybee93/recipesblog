---
title: "Tempeh salad bowl with tahini dressing"
date: "2026-01-20"
categories:
  - mains
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/tempeh-salad-bowl-with-tahini-dressing.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - 30g quinoa
  - 50g tempeh, sliced
  - 60g edamame, cooked
  - ¼ small cucumber, chopped
  - 60g cherry tomatoes, chopped
  - Tahini dressing (see recipe)
directions:
  - Rinse and cook the quinoa.
  - Heat a pan over medium heat. Add the tempeh and cook for 5-10 minutes, turning to brown each side.
  - Assemble the bowls with quinoa, edamame, tempeh, cucumber and tomatoes. Drizzle with tahini dressing and serve.
