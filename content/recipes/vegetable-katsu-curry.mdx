---
title: "Vegetable katsu curry"
date: "2026-04-15"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/vegetable-katsu-curry.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g rice
  - 4 tbsp plain flour
  - ¼ tsp sea salt
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - 100ml water
  - 100g panko breadcrumbs
  - 1 tsp sesame seeds (optional)
  - ½ tbsp olive oil
  - '## Any veggies:'
  - '1 medium aubergine, sliced 1cm thick'
  - '1 large courgette, sliced 1cm thick'
  - '½ butternut squash, sliced 0.5cm thick'
  - '1 medium sweet potato, sliced 0.5cm thick'
  - '## Sauce'
  - Katsu curry sauce (see recipe)
  - '## Sides'
  - 4 tbsp pickled radishes (see recipe)
  - 50g salad leaves
directions:
  - 'Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a large baking tray.'
  - 'In a shallow bowl, mix the flour, salt, garlic and water to make a thin paste. Sprinkle the breadcrumbs and sesame seeds on a plate. Coat the vegetable slices in the paste, shake off excess, then press into the breadcrumbs. Place each slice on the tray and drizzle with the olive oil.'
  - 'Bake the butternut squash for 30 minutes, sweet potato for 25 minutes, aubergine for 20 minutes, and courgette for 10 minutes, turning halfway. Increase heat to 220°C and bake for 10 more minutes, or until crispy. Alternatively, cook in an airfryer - cook the butternut squash for 20 minutes, sweet potato for 18 minutes, aubergine for 15 minutes, and courgette for 10 minutes, turning halfway, or until crispy.'
  - Cook the rice.
  - Make the curry sauce.
  - 'When the veggies are almost ready, gently reheat the sauce for 5 minutes. Assemble the bowls with rice and vegetable slices. Pour the sauce over the vegetables. Serve with the pickled radishes and salad leaves.'
