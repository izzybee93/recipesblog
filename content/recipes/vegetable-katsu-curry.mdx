title: "Vegetable katsu curry"
date: "2026-04-15"

servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 100g rice
  - ½ tbsp olive oil
  - 4 tbsp plain flour
  - 90ml water
  - ¼ tsp sea salt
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder (optional)
  - 100g panko breadcrumbs
  - 1 tsp sesame seeds (optional)
  - '## Any veggies:'
  - '1 medium aubergine, sliced 1cm thick'
  - '1 large courgette, sliced 1cm thick'
  - '½ butternut squash, sliced 0.5cm thick'
  - '1 medium sweet potato, sliced 0.5cm thick'
  - '## Sauce'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '½ onion, chopped'
  - '2 medium carrots, diced'
  - '1 garlic clove, sliced'
  - '1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced'
  - 1 tbsp curry powder
  - 1 tbsp plain flour
  - 250ml vegetable stock
  - Pinch sea salt, to taste
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  - '## Sides'
  - 4 tbsp pickled radishes (see recipe)
  - 50g salad leaves
directions:
  - 'Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a large baking tray.
  - In a shallow bowl, mix the flour, salt, garlic and water to make a thin paste. Put the breadcrumbs and sesame seeds on a plate. Coat the vegetable slices in the paste, shake off excess, then press into the breadcrumbs. Place on the tray and drizzle with ½ tbsp olive oil. Bake the butternut squash for 30 minutes, sweet potato for 25 minutes, aubergine for 20 minutes, and courgette for 10 minutes, turning halfway. Increase heat to 220°C and bake for 10 more minutes, or until crispy.'
  - Cook the rice.
  - 'To make the sauce, heat the oil in a large pan. Add the onion, carrots and sweet potato, and cook for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and ginger, and cook for 1 more minute. Reduce the heat and cover, leaving to cook for 5 more minutes.'
  - 'Stir in the curry powder and cook for 2 minutes. Stir in the flour, then add the stock and bring to the boil. Add the salt, soy sauce and ketchup. Remove from the heat and blend until smooth. If making ahead of time, store in the fridge for up to 3 days.'
  - 'When the veggies are almost ready, gently reheat the sauce for 5 minutes. Assemble the bowls with rice and vegetable slices. Pour the sauce over the vegetables. Serve with the pickled radishes and salad leaves.'
