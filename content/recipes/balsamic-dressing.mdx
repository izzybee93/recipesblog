title: "Balsamic dressing"
date: "2026-08-18"
categories:
  - sauces
featured_image: "/images/recipes/balsamic-dressing.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2-3"
ingredients:
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  - Juice of ½ lemon or 1 additional tbsp balsamic vinegar
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - 'Pinch sea salt, to taste'
  - ½ tsp dried oregano (optional)
  - 'Small handful parsley or basil leaves, finely chopped (optional)'
directions:
  - Whisk the ingredients together in a small jug.
