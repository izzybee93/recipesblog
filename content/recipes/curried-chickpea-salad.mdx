servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - '1 large sweet potato, chopped'
  - '1 pepper, chopped'
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - '1 courgette, sliced'
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 1 tsp curry powder
  - 80g rocket
  - '## Dressing'
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ¼ tsp chilli powder
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - ¼ tsp sea salt, to taste
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare 2 baking trays.
  - >-
    Place the sweet potato on a tray and brush with ½ tbsp olive oil. Sprinkle with the smoked paprika and
    mix until coated. Roast for 15 minutes. Remove from the oven, add the pepper to the tray,
    and brush with ½ tbsp olive oil. Roast for another 10-15 minutes until softened and starting to brown.
  - >-
    Place the chickpeas on a tray, sprinkle with the curry poweder and mix until coated. Roast for 20 minutes, until crispy.
  - >-
    Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the courgette
    slices and cook until slightly browned on one side, then flip each slice and cook for another 5
    minutes.
  - >-
    Whisk together all the dressing ingredients in a small jug.
  - >-
    In a large bowl, add the rocket, veggies and chickpeas. Add the dressing,
    gently toss together and serve.
