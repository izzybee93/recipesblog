---
title: "Blueberry maple sauce"
date: "2026-09-07"
categories:
  - sauces
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/blueberry-maple-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 150g blueberries
  - 1 tbsp maple syrup
  - 1 tsp lemon juice (optional)
directions:
  - Gently heat the maple syrup and blueberries in a saucepan until the berries start to release their juices. Simmer for 5 minutes over a low heat until slightly thickened, stirring frequently. Remove from the heat and stir in the lemon juice.
  - Serve with French toast, pancakes, waffles or crepes.
