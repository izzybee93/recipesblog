---
title: 'Pasta with tomato, spinach and aubergine'
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.295Z'
categories:
  - mains
  - pasta
featured_image: /images/recipes/pasta-with-tomato-sauce-spinach-and-aubergine.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 aubergine, chopped'
  - 180g pasta
  - 1 garlic clove
  - 200g passata
  - A few basil leaves
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - 80g spinach
  
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 180°C. Place the aubergine on a baking tray and brush
    with 1 tbsp olive oil. Roast the aubergine for 15 minutes, until soft.
  - Cook the pasta.
  - >-
    Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pan. Chop the garlic in
    half. Add to the pan and cook on a medium-high heat for a few minutes. Add
    the passata and continue to cook on a medium heat, stirring frequently. Stir in
    the salt and ground black pepper. Stir in the spinach until wilted.
  - >-
    Add the pasta and auberginem and toss together until coated. Remove from the heat 
    and stir in the basil. Serve topped with ground black pepper.
