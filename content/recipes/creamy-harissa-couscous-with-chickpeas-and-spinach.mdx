---
title: "Creamy harissa couscous with chickpeas and spinach"
date: "2026-08-28"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/creamy-harissa-couscous-with-chickpeas-and-spinach.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - ½ tbsp olive oil
  - '½ onion, finely chopped'
  - '1 red pepper, chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - ½ tbsp tomato paste
  - 1 tbsp harissa paste
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - ½ tsp smoked paprika
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 100g giant couscous
  - 250ml vegetable stock
  - 50ml soya or oat cream
  - '1 400g can chickpeas, drained and rinsed'
  - 150g cherry tomatoes (optional)
  - 60g spinach
  - Juice of ½ lemon
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - 'In a large pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion and red pepper and cook for 5 minutes, until softened. Add the garlic and cook for another minute. Stir in the tomato paste, harissa, cumin, smoked paprika and salt.'
  - 'Add the couscous and vegetable stock. Stir together and bring to a simmer. Cook for 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the couscous is cooked through and the sauce has thickened.'
  - 'Add the cream, chickpeas and tomatoes and cook for 5 more minutes, until heated through. Stir in the spinach until wilted.'
  - 'Remove from the heat and stir in the lemon juice. Serve topped with ground black pepper.'
---
