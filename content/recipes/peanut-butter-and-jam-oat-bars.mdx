---
title: "Peanut butter and jam oat bars"
date: "2025-09-21"
categories: 
  - treats
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/peanut-butter-and-jam-oat-bars.jpeg"
servings: "makes 12"
ingredients:
  - 200g peanut butter
  - 45g maple syrup
  - 25g vegetable oil
  - 140g oats
  - 70g oat flour
  - ¼ tsp baking powder
  - ¼ tsp sea salt
  - 4-6 tbsp plant milk
  - '160g jam, such as raspberry, strawberry, blueberry or blackcurrant'
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Line an 8x8 dish with baking paper.
  - 'Set aside 50g of the peanut butter. In a large bowl, combine the remaining peanut butter, maple syrup, vegetable oil and 50ml plant milk.'
  - 'Add the oats, oat flour, baking powder and salt and mix well to form a soft, slightly sticky dough. Add another tbsp milk as needed until the mixture holds together.'
  - 'Place ¾ of the dough into the baking tin and press down firmly with the back of a spoon. Bake the layer for 7-8 minutes, then remove from the oven.'
  - Spread the remaining peanut butter evenly on top of the baked layer. Spread the jam on top.
  - 'Crumble the remaining dough evenly over the top. Bake for 18-22 minutes, until golden. Leave to cool completely before slicing into bars.'
