---
title: "Tempeh and vegetable stir fry"
date: "2026-01-17"
categories: ["mains"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/tempeh-and-vegetable-stir-fry.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 200g tempeh, cut into strips
  - 100g rice
  - 1 tbsp sesame oil
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '## Any 2-3 veggies:'
  - 100g broccolini, chopped
  - 1 pepper, chopped
  - 100g mushrooms, sliced
  - 1 courgette, chopped
  - 100g baby corn, chopped
  - 100g sugar snap peas
  - '## Marinade'
  - 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  - 2 tbsp soy sauce
  - 2 tbsp almond butter
  - 1 tsp sesame oil
  - 1 tsp maple syrup
  - 1 tsp fresh ginger, minced
  - ¼ tsp chilli flakes
  - '## Toppings'
  - 1 tsp sesame seeds
  - 2 tbsp crispy onion (optional)
directions:
  - Place the tempeh in a shallow dish. In a small bowl, whisk together the marinade ingredients. Pour over the tempeh, cover and transfer to the fridge for 2–12 hours.'
  - Cook the rice.
  - In a large pan, heat the sesame oil over a medium heat. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Add the veggies and cook for 5–10 minutes.
  - Add the tempeh, leftover marinade and a splash of water. Cook for 5–10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  - Serve with the rice and top with a sprinkle of sesame seeds and crispy onion.
