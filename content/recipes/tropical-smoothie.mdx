---
title: "Tropical smoothie"
date: "2026-08-08"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/tropical-smoothie.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 frozen banana
  - 100g frozen mango
  - 3 passion fruit
  - '2 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 4 tbsp oats
  - ½ tsp lime zest (optional)
  - 200ml plant milk
directions:
  - Place all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
