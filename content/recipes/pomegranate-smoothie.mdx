---
title: "Pomegranate smoothie"
date: "2026-10-12"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/pomegranate-smoothie.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '½ banana, chopped'
  - 75g pomegranate seeds
  - '25g blueberries, raspberries or strawberries'
  - '2 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - ½ tsp lemon zest (optional)
  - 4 tbsp oats
  - 200ml plant milk
directions:
  - Place all ingredients except ½ tbsp pomegranate seeds in a blender and blend until smooth. Top with a sprinkle of pomegranate seeds.
---
