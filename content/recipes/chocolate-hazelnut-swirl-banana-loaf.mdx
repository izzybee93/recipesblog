---
title: "Chocolate hazelnut swirl banana loaf"
date: "2026-09-27"
categories:
  - bread
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/chocolate-hazelnut-swirl-banana-loaf.jpeg"
servings: "makes 1 loaf"
ingredients:
  - 500g strong white bread flour
  - 7g instant yeast
  - 1 tsp sea salt
  - 2 tbsp caster sugar
  - '2 overripe bananas, mashed'
  - 40ml vegetable oil
  - 150–200ml soya milk
  - 200g dark chocolate hazelnut spread
directions:
  - In a large bowl, combine the flour, yeast, salt and sugar.
  - Add the mashed banana, vegetable oil and soya milk. Mix until a rough dough forms.
  - Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and knead for 10 minutes, until smooth and elastic. Transfer to a lightly greased bowl, cover and leave in a warm place for 2 hours, or until doubled in size. Alternatively, refrigerate overnight.
  - Prepare a loaf tin. Punch down the dough and turn it out onto a lightly floured surface. Roll into a rectangle approximately 30 x 45cm.
  - Spread the chocolate hazelnut spread evenly over the dough, leaving a small border around the edges. Roll up tightly from one of the long edges into a log.
  - Using a sharp knife, slice the log in half lengthways. Turn the cut sides upwards and twist the two halves together. Tuck the ends underneath and carefully transfer to the loaf tin.
  - Loosely cover and leave to rise for 45–60 minutes, or until puffy.
  - Preheat the oven to 190°C. Bake for 45–55 minutes. Cover loosely with foil during the final 10 minutes if browning too quickly.
  - Leave to cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack. Leave to cool completely before slicing.
