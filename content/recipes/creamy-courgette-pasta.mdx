title: Creamy courgette pasta
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.279Z'
categories:
  - mains
  - pasta
featured_image: /images/recipes/creamy-courgette-pasta.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 180g dry pasta
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '2 courgettes, chopped'
  - Creamy pasta sauce (see recipe)
directions:
  - Cook the pasta, reserving some pasta water.
  - >-
    In a large pan, heat the olive oil on a medium heat. Cook the courgette
    for 8-10 minutes until softened and slightly browned.
  - Make the creamy sauce and heat in the pan with the courgette.
  - >-
    Add the pasta to the pan and stir until heated through. Season to
    taste. Serve topped with ground black pepper.
