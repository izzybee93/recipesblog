---
title: "Coffee frosting"
date: "2026-10-01"
categories:
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/coffee-frosting.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1 cake or 12 muffins"
ingredients:
  - 75g plant butter
  - 140g icing sugar, sieved, sieved
  - ½ tsp vanilla extract
  - 2 tsp instant coffee
  - Pinch sea salt
  - 1–2 tsp plant milk
directions:
  - 'Dissolve the instant coffee in 1 tsp warm plant milk, then leave to cool slightly.'
  - 'In a medium bowl, beat the plant butter until creamy. Gradually add the icing sugar and beat until light and fluffy.'
  - 'Add the coffee mixture, vanilla and salt and beat until well combined. Add the remaining plant milk as needed and beat until smooth and fluffy.'
