---
title: "Coffee frosting"
date: "2026-10-01"
categories:
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/coffee-frosting.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1 cake or 12 muffins"
ingredients:
  - 100g plant butter
  - 200g icing sugar
  - ½ tsp vanilla extract
  - 1 tbsp instant coffee
  - Pinch of sea salt
  - 1 tbsp plant milk
directions:
  - 'Dissolve the instant coffee in 1 tbsp warm plant milk, then leave to cool slightly.'
  - 'In a medium bowl, beat the plant butter until light and creamy. Gradually add the icing sugar and beat until smooth and fluffy.'
  - 'Add the coffee mixture, vanilla extract and salt, then beat again until well combined. Add a little more plant milk as needed, until the frosting reaches a smooth, spreadable consistency.'
