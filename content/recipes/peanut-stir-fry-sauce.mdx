---
title: "Peanut stir fry sauce"
date: "2026-08-17"
categories:
  - sauces
featured_image: "/images/recipes/peanut-stir-fry-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1½ tbsp peanut butter
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 tbsp rice vinegar or lime juice
  - 1 tsp maple syrup or honey
  - Pinch chilli flakes (optional)
  - 50ml plant milk
  - 2-4 tbsp water
directions:
  - 'Whisk all ingredients together in a small jug until smooth. Add to a pan of stir fried veggies, and stir until slightly thickened and heated through. Add a few tbsp more water as needed.'
