---
title: "Creamy green butter beans"
date: "2026-08-03"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/creamy-green-butter-beans.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - Creamy green sauce (see recipe)
  - ½ tbsp olive oil
  - 200g cherry tomatoes
  - '1 400g can butter beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 100ml vegetable stock or water
  - 160g frozen peas (optional)
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C. Prepare a large baking tray. Add the tomatoes and brush with the olive oil. Roast for 15–20 minutes.
  - Make the creamy green sauce.
  - Add the butter beans and vegetable stock or water. Simmer for 5–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened slightly. Add the peas and cook for a few more minutes, until heated through.
  - Divide between bowls and top with the roasted tomatoes and ground black pepper. Serve with crusty bread or quinoa.
