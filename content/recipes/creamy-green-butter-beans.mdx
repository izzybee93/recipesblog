servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - Creamy green sauce (see recipe)
  - '1 400g can butter beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 100ml vegetable stock or water
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - '## Optional veggies'
  - 200g cherry tomatoes
  - 160g peas
directions:
  - Make the creamy green sauce.
  - Add the butter beans and vegetable stock or water. Simmer for 5–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened slightly. Add the tomatoes or peas and cook for a few more minutes until heated through.
  - Top with ground black pepper and serve with crusty bread.
