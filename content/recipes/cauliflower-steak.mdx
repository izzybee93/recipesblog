---
title: Cauliflower steak
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.269Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/cauliflower-steak.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 1 cauliflower
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - 2 tsp turmeric
  - 500ml vegetable stock
  - 'Handful fresh coriander, chopped'
  - '## Sauce'
  - Juice of 1 lime
  - ½ tsp harissa paste
  - 2 tsp maple syrup
  - ¼ tsp sea salt, to taste
directions:
  - >-
    Preheat the oven to 180°C. Slice the middle of the cauliflower into 2 steaks
    about 2cm.
  - >-
    In a large dish, combine the olive oil and the turmeric. Place each cauliflower in the dish until coated.  
  - >-
    Heat a large pan over medium-high heat. Place the cauliflower steaks in the pan and sear until
    lightly browned on each side.
  - >-
    Place the cauliflower in a large dish. Pour in the vegetable stock, up to 3cm. Roast for 20–25 minutes, until softened.
  - >-
    Combine all the sauce ingredients in a small bowl and mix well. Pour over the
    cauliflower steaks and serve.
