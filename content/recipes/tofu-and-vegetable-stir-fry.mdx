title: Tofu and vegetable stir fry
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.313Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/tofu-and-vegetable-stir-fry.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 100g rice
  - 1 ½ tbsp sesame oil
  - '200g extra firm tofu, chopped'
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '## Any 2 veggies:'
  - 2 medium carrots, chopped
  - 1 large courgette, chopped
  - 1 pepper, chopped
  - ¼ broccoli, chopped into florets
  - 150g baby corn, chopped
  - 150g sugar snap peas
  - 150g beansprouts
  - '## Any sauce'
  - Almond stir fry sauce (see recipe)
  - Lemon, ginger and ponzu stir fry sauce (see recipe)
  - Miso and ginger stir fry sauce (see recipe)
  - Sesame, ginger and lime stir fry sauce (see recipe)
  - '## Toppings'
  - 1 tsp sesame seeds
  - 2 tbsp crispy onion (optional)
directions:
  - Cook the rice.
  - >-
    In a medium pan, heat ½ tbsp sesame oil. Add the tofu and soy sauce. Toss to
    coat the tofu and cook on a medium heat for 10 minutes, turning to brown each side.
  - >-
    In a large wok, heat 1 tbsp sesame oil. Add the garlic and cook for 1
    minute. Add the veggies and cook for 5-10 minutes. Stir in
    the tofu.
  - >-
    Make the sauce. Add to the wok and cook for 5-10 minutes until heated through
    and thickened slightly. Add a few tbsp water as needed.
  - Serve with the rice and top with a sprinkle of sesame seeds.
