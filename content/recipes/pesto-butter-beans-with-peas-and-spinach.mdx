servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - ⅓ basil pesto (see recipe)
  - '160g butter beans, drained and rinsed'
  - ½ tbsp olive oil
  - 80g frozen peas
  - 60g spinach
  - 1 tbsp lemon juice
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - Make the basil pesto.
  - 'In a large pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the butter beans and peas. Cook for 5-10 minutes until heated through, adding a few tbsp water as needed. Stir in the spinach until wilted.'
  - 'Add the pesto and stir through until coated. Remove from the heat and stir in the lemon juice.'
  - 'Serve on toasted sourdough or stir through pasta. Top with ground black pepper.'
