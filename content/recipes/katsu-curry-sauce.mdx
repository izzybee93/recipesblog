---
title: "Katsu curry sauce"
date: "2026-08-18"
categories:
  - sauces
featured_image: "/images/recipes/katsu-curry-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '½ onion, chopped'
  - '2 medium carrots, diced'
  - '1 garlic clove, sliced'
  - '1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced'
  - 1 tbsp curry powder
  - 1 tbsp plain flour
  - 250ml vegetable stock
  - 'Pinch sea salt, to taste'
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
directions:
  - 'Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion and carrots and cook for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 1 more minute. Cover and cook for 5 more minutes over low heat.'
  - 'Stir in the curry powder and cook for 2 minutes. Stir in the flour, then add the stock and bring to the boil. Add the salt, soy sauce and ketchup. Remove from the heat and blend until smooth.'
  - 'Reheat for 5 minutes before serving. Add a few tbsp water as needed. Serve with vegetable katsu curry (see recipe), roasted vegetables, tofu, or your favourite veggie grain bowl. Store in the fridge for up to 3 days.'
