---
title: "Lime and chilli dressing"
date: "2026-08-18"
categories:
  - sauces
featured_image: "/images/recipes/lime-and-chilli-dressing.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2-3"
ingredients:
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - Juice of 1 lime
  - ½ tsp maple syrup or honey (optional)
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Pinch chilli flakes, to taste'
  - ½ tsp cumin (optional)
directions:
  - Whisk the ingredients together in a small jug.
