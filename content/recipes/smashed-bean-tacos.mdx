---
title: "Smashed bean tacos"
date: "2026-09-02"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/smashed-bean-tacos.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 small onion, chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '1 pepper, chopped'
  - '1 400g can black beans or kidney beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 1 tsp turmeric
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - ½ tsp chilli powder
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - Juice of ½ lime
  - 'Handful fresh coriander, chopped'
  - 4-6 taco wraps
directions:
  - 'In a large pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for a few minutes, followed by the garlic and red pepper. Cook for a few more minutes until softened.'
  - 'Add the beans, turmeric, smoked paprika, chilli powder and cumin. Cook over medium heat for 5–10 minutes, stirring occasionally. Lightly mash the beans, leaving some texture. Add a few tbsp water if it becomes too dry.'
  - Remove from the heat and stir in the lime juice and fresh coriander.
  - Spread the bean mixture over one half of each tortilla. Fold the other half over the filling and gently press down.
  - 'Heat a large pan over medium heat. Add the tacos and cook for 2–3 minutes on each side, until golden and crisp.'
