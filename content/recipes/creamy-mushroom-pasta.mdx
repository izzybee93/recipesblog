title: Creamy mushroom pasta
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.261Z'
categories:
  - mains
  - pasta
featured_image: /images/recipes/creamy-mushroom-pasta.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 180g dry pasta
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '250g mushrooms, sliced'
  - Creamy pasta sauce (see recipe)
  - 40g rocket (optional)
directions:
  - Cook the pasta.
  - >-
    In a large pan, heat the olive oil and cook the garlic on a medium heat
    for 1 minute. Add the mushrooms and cook for 5-10 minutes.
  - Make the creamy sauce and heat in the pan with the mushrooms.
  - Add the pasta. Stir until combined and heated through. Season to taste.
  - Serve topped with ground black pepper and rocket leaves.
