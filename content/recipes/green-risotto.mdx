---
title: "Green risotto"
date: "2026-09-28"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/green-risotto.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 140g risotto rice
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '½ onion, finely chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - ~800ml vegetable stock
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - 100ml oat or soya cream
  - 'Small handful basil or parsley, chopped'
  - Juice of ¼ lemon
  - '## Any 1-2 veggies'
  - '1 courgette, chopped'
  - '½ broccoli, chopped into florets'
  - 150g frozen peas
directions:
  - Heat the oil in a pan over a medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, until softened. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Add the courgette and cook for a few more minutes, until lightly browned.
  - Add the rice and cook for 2 minutes. Gradually add the stock and cook for 20 minutes, stirring frequently.
  - Once the rice is almost cooked, stir in the broccoli and peas and cook for 5 more minutes. Stir in the cream until heated through.
  - Remove from the heat and stir in the herbs and lemon juice. Serve topped with ground black pepper.
