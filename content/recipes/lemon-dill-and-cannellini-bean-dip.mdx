---
title: "Lemon, dill and cannellini bean dip"
date: "2026-07-26"
categories:
  - snacks
featured_image: "/images/recipes/lemon-dill-and-cannellini-bean-dip.jpeg"
servings: "serves 4"
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed'
  - 2 tbsp capers
  - 1 garlic clove
  - Juice of 1 lemon
  - Zest of ½ lemon
  - ½ tsp Dijon mustard
  - 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - Ground black pepper, to taste
  - 'Small handful fresh dill, chopped'
  - 2–3 tbsp water
directions:
  - Finely chop 1 tbsp of the capers and set aside.
  - >- 
    Add the cannellini beans, garlic, 1 tbsp capers, lemon juice, lemon zest, olive oil, mustard, 
    salt and ground black pepper to a food processor. Blend for 30 seconds, scraping down the sides 
    as needed. Add 1 tbsp water at a time and blend until smooth.
  - Stir in the dill and chopped capers. Transfer to the fridge for 1 hour before serving.
  - Serve with crunchy vegetables, salad or spread on toasted sourdough.
