---
title: "Berry and super seed porridge"
date: "2026-02-22"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/berry-and-super-seed-porridge.jpeg"
servings: "serves 1"
ingredients:
  - 50g oats
  - 250ml plant milk or water
  - '1 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water'
  - 100g mixed berries, such as rapsberry, blueberry and blackberry
  - 1 tbsp hemp seeds
  - 1 tsp flax seeds
  - 1 tsp pumpkin seeds
  - 1 tsp sunflower seeds
directions:
  - Add the oats, milk, chia seeds and ¾ of the berries to the pan and cook for a few minutes on a medium heat, stirring frequently until the juices start to release.
  - Serve topped with the remaining berries and seeds.
---
