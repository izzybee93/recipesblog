---
title: "Pasta with tomato sauce"
date: "2026-09-09"
categories:
  - mains
  - pasta
featured_image: "/images/recipes/pasta-with-tomato-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 180g pasta
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - '1 garlic clove, halved'
  - 250g passata
  - 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar (optional)
  - '¼ tsp sea salt, to taste'
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - 60g spinach (optional)
  - Small handful fresh basil
  - '## Any 1-2 veggies:'
  - '1 courgette, chopped'
  - '1 pepper, chopped'
  - '200g portobellini mushrooms, chopped'
  - '1 medium aubergine, chopped'
  - '## Any 1-2 additional veggies:'
  - '200g cherry tomatoes, halved'
  - '6 sun-dried tomatoes, chopped'
  - '10 olives, chopped'
  - '80g artichoke hearts, cooked and chopped'
  - 1 tbsp capers
directions:
  - 'Cook the pasta, reserving a few tbsp of pasta water.'
  - 'Heat the olive oil in a large pan. Add the garlic and cook on a medium-high heat for a few minutes to flavour the oil. Add the raw veggies and cook on a medium heat for 8-10 minutes, until starting to soften. Stir in the additional veggies.'
  - 'Add the passata and continue to cook on a medium heat for a few minutes, stirring frequently. Stir in the balsamic vinegar and season with salt and ground black pepper. Stir in the spinach and basil leaves until wilted. Add a few tbsp pasta water as needed.'
  - Add the pasta and toss together until coated. Serve topped with ground black pepper.
