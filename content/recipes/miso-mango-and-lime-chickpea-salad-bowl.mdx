title: "Miso, mango and lime chickpea salad bowl"
date: "2026-09-02"
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: "/images/recipes/miso-mango-and-lime-chickpea-salad-bowl.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '⅔ chickpeas with miso, mango and lime sauce (see recipe)'
  - 50g rocket, lettuce or mixed salad leaves
  - '¼ cucumber, sliced'
  - '½ avocado, chopped'
  - '½ red pepper, finely chopped'
  - Juice of ½ lime
  - Pinch sea salt, to taste
  - '2 pitta breads, warmed (optional)'
directions:
  - 'Make the chickpeas with miso, mango and lime sauce.'
  - 'In a medium bowl, add the leaves, cucumber, pepper and lime juice and salt. Toss to combine. Add the avocado and gently mix.'
  - Divide the salad between serving bowls. Top with the chickpeas and serve with the pitta bread.
