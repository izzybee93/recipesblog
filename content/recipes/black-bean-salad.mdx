featured_image: /images/recipes/black-bean-salad.jpeg
servings: serves 2-3
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can black beans, drained and rinsed'
  - '1 bell pepper, chopped'
  - '150g cherry tomatoes, chopped'
  - '¼ cucumber, chopped'
  - '1 avocado, chopped'
  - 80g sweetcorn
  - 'Handful coriander, chopped'
  - Lime and chilli dressing (see recipe)
directions:
  - Combine the beans, pepper, tomatoes, cucumber, sweetcorn and coriander in a medium bowl.
  - Make the lime and chilli dressing.
  - Pour the dressing over the salad and mix again.
  - >-
    Gently stir in the avocado. Serve topped with a garnish of coriander.
