servings: serves 2-3
ingredients:
  - '1 400g can black beans, drained and rinsed'
  - '1 bell pepper, chopped'
  - '150g cherry tomatoes, chopped'
  - '¼ cucumber, chopped'
  - '1 avocado, chopped'
  - 80g sweetcorn
  - Handful coriander chopped
  - '## Dressing'
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - Juice of 1 lime
  - ½ tsp maple syrup or honey (optional)
  - ¼ tsp sea salt
  - ¼ tsp garlic powder
  - ½ tsp cumin
  - Pinch chilli flakes
directions:
  - Combine the beans, pepper, tomatoes, cucumber, sweetcorn and coriander in a medium bowl.
  - >-
    In a small measuring jug, whisk together the dressing ingredients. Pour over
    the salad and mix again.
  - >-
    Gently stir in the avocado. Serve topped with a garnish of coriander.
