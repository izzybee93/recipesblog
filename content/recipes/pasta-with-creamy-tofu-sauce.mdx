---
title: "Pasta with creamy tofu sauce"
date: "2026-02-15"
categories:
  - mains
  - pasta
featured_image: "/images/recipes/pasta-with-creamy-tofu-sauce.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - 180g dry pasta
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 garlic clove, minced
  - Ground black pepper, to taste
  - '## Any 1-2 veggies:'
  - 250g mushrooms, chopped
  - '½ broccoli, chopped and roasted'
  - 1 large courgette, chopped
  - '## Any 1-2 additional veggies:'
  - 150g peas, cooked
  - 60g spinach
  - '## Sauce'
  - Creamy tofu sauce (see recipe)
directions:
  - Cook the pasta, reserving some of the pasta water.
  - In a large pan, heat the oil and cook the garlic on a medium heat for 1 minute. Add the raw veggies and cook for 5-10 minutes, until softened and lightly browned.
  - Make the creamy tofu sauce. Add to the pan and gently heat through. Stir in a few tbsp of pasta water, adding more as needed.
  - Add the pasta and additional veggies. Stir until combined and heated through. Serve topped with ground black pepper.
