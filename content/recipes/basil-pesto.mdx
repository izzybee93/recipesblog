---
title: "Basil pesto"
date: "2025-11-08"
categories: ["sauces"]
featured_image: "/images/recipes/basil-pesto.jpeg"
servings: "serves 6"
ingredients:
  - 25g fresh basil
  - 30g spinach
  - 20g sunflower seeds, pumpkin seeds or hemp seeds
  - 25g pine nuts, cashews or walnuts, toasted
  - 2 garlic cloves
  - Zest of ½ lemon (optional)
  - ¼ tsp sea salt
  - 1 tbsp nutritional yeast (optional)
  - 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  - 2–4 tbsp water
directions:
  - Add basil, spinach, seeds, toasted nuts, garlic, lemon zest, salt and nutritional yeast to a food processor. Process into a coarse mixture.
  - Add the olive oil and a tbsp of water and process again. Add more water, a tbsp at a time, until the pesto reaches the desired consistency.
  - Serve with pasta or gnocchi, and your favourite veggies. Store refrigerated for up to 5 days, covered with a thin layer of extra virgin olive oil, or freeze small portions in ice cube trays for up to 3 months.
