---
title: Mushroom risotto
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.294Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/mushroom-risotto.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 140g risotto rice
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '½ onion, finely chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '300g chestnut mushrooms, sliced'
  - 800ml vegetable stock
  - 100ml oat or soya cream
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
directions:
  - >-
    Heat the oil in a large pan over a medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, until softened. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Add the mushrooms and cook for 8-10 minutes, until lightly browned.
  - >-
    Add the rice and cook for 2 minutes. Gradually add the stock and cook over a medium-low heat for 20-25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the rice
    is cooked al dente and the risotto is creamy.
  - >-
    Stir in the cream until heated through. Serve topped with ground black pepper.
