---
title: "Orange, almond and goji oat bars"
date: "2026-09-04"
categories:
  - breakfast
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/orange-almond-and-goji-oat-bars.jpeg"
servings: "makes 16"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  - 3 tbsp water
  - 180g oats
  - ½ tsp cinnamon
  - ¼ tsp sea salt
  - 55g goji berries
  - '50g almonds, chopped'
  - 30g pumpkin seeds
  - 100g almond butter
  - 45g maple syrup
  - Zest of 1 orange
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - 2-3 tbsp plant milk
directions:
  - 'In a small bowl, add the ground flaxseed and water and set aside for 10 minutes.'
  - Preheat the oven to 170°C. Prepare a 20cm square baking dish.
  - 'In a large bowl, combine the oats, cinnamon, salt, goji berries, almonds and pumpkin seeds.'
  - 'In a small pan, add the almond butter and maple syrup and heat over a low heat until smooth. Remove from the heat, then stir in the flaxseed mixture, orange zest, vanilla and 2 tbsp milk.'
  - Add the almond butter mixture to the dry ingredients and mix well until evenly coated. Add another tbsp milk as needed until the mixture holds together when pressed.'
  - 'Transfer to the dish and press down firmly with the back of a spoon. Bake for 22-25 minutes, until golden. Leave to cool completely before slicing into bars.'
