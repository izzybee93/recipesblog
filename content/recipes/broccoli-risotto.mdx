---
title: Broccoli risotto
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.265Z'
categories:
  - mains
featured_image: /images/recipes/broccoli-risotto.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 140g risotto rice
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - '1 small onion, chopped'
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - ~800ml vegetable stock
  - '½ broccoli, chopped into florets'
  - ½ tsp dried thyme
  - 'Ground black pepper, to taste'
  - 100ml oat or soya cream (optional)
  - 2 tbsp pine nuts, toasted (optional)
directions:
  - >-
    Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat. Add  the onion and cook for 5 minutes, until softened. 
    Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Add the rice and cook for 2 minutes.
  - >-
    Gradually add the stock and cook over a mdeium-low heat for 20 minutes, stirring occassionally.
  - >-
    Once the rice is almost cooked, stir in the broccoli and cook for 5 more minutes. Stir in the cream until heated through.
  - Serve topped with toasted pine nuts.
