title: Veggie noodle stir fry
date: '2025-08-16T15:27:07.318Z'
categories:

featured_image: /images/recipes/veggie-noodle-stir-fry.jpeg
servings: serves 2
ingredients:
  - 100g rice noodles
  - 1 tbsp sesame oil
  - '1 garlic clove, minced'
  - '120g edamame, cooked'
  - '## Any 2 veggies:'
  - 2 medium carrots, chopped
  - 1 large courgette, chopped
  - 1 pepper, chopped
  - ¼ broccoli, chopped into florets
  - 150g baby corn, chopped
  - 150g sugar snap peas
  - 150g beansprouts
  - '## Any sauce'
  - Almond stir fry sauce (see recipe)
  - Lemon, ginger and ponzu stir fry sauce (see recipe)
  - Miso and ginger stir fry sauce (see recipe)
  - Sesame, ginger and lime stir fry sauce (see recipe)
  - '## Toppings'
  - 'Handful fresh coriander, chopped'
  - 2 tsp sesame seeds
  - 2 tbsp crispy onion (optional)
directions:
  - 'Cook the rice noodles. Drain and rinse, then set aside.'
  - >-
    In a large wok, heat the sesame oil. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
    Add the edamame and veggies, and cook for 5 minutes, stirring frequently.
  - >-
    Make the sauce. Add to the wok and cook for 5-10 minutes until heated through
    and thickened slightly. Add a few tbsp water as needed. Stir in the coriander.
  - Add the noodles and toss until combined. Serve topped with a sprinkle of sesame seeds and crispy onion.
