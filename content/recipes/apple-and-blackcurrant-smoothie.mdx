---
title: "Apple and blackcurrant smoothie"
date: "2026-07-29"
categories:
  - breakfast
featured_image: "/images/recipes/apple-and-blackcurrant-smoothie.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2"
ingredients:
  - '1 apple, chopped'
  - 100g blackcurrants
  - 1 tbsp almond butter
  - '2 tbsp chia seeds, soaked in water (optional)'
  - 4 tbsp oats
  - 1 tsp cinnamon
  - 200ml plant milk
directions:
  - Place all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth.
