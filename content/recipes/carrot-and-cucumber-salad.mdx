---
title: "Carrot and cucumber salad"
date: "2026-04-09"
categories:
  - salad
featured_image: "/images/recipes/carrot-and-cucumber-salad.jpeg"
servings: "serves 2–3"
ingredients:
  - 2 medium carrots, peeled into ribbons
  - ½ cucumber, peeled into ribbons
  - Small handful fresh coriander, chopped
  - '## Dressing'
  - 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 tsp sesame oil
  - 1½ tbsp rice vinegar
  - 1 tsp maple syrup
  - 2 tsp sesame seeds
  - ¼ tsp chilli flakes
directions:
  - In a medium bowl, add the carrots, cucumber and coriander.
  - Combine the dressing ingredients in a small jug. Pour over the vegetables and toss to combine. Serve topped with extra sesame seeds.
