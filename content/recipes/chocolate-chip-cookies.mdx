title: "Chocolate chip cookies"
date: "2025-08-16T15:27:07.275Z"
categories:
  - treats
featured_image: "/images/recipes/chocolate-chip-cookies.jpeg"
servings: "makes 18"
ingredients:
  - 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  - 3 tbsp water
  - 180g plain flour
  - ½ tsp baking powder
  - ¼ tsp salt
  - 90g plant butter
  - 45g caster sugar
  - 40g light brown soft sugar
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - '60g dark chocolate, chopped'
directions:
  - Whisk together the flaxseed and water in a small bowl and set aside for 15 minutes to thicken.
  - In a medium bowl, combine the flour, baking powder and salt.
  - In a large bowl, beat together the butter, caster sugar and brown sugar until light and creamy. Beat in the vanilla extract and flax egg.
  - Add the flour mixture and mix until just combined. Fold in the chocolate. Transfer to the fridge for 30 minutes.
  - Preheat the oven to 180°C. Prepare a large baking tray.
  - Scoop out spoonfuls of dough and roll into a ball. Place on the tray, leaving space between each cookie, then lightly flatten. Bake for 12–15 minutes, until lightly golden around the edges. Leave to cool slightly before transferring to a wire rack.
  - Store in an airtight container for up to 5 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
